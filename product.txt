CartHedge — Complete Product Definition
1. One-line
CartHedge is an AI sales assistant + order desk + instant storefront for Instagram/WhatsApp-first sellers in India — it replies to every buyer DM within seconds in the buyer's own language, answers from the seller's real catalog, collects the order and confirms it inside the chat, and keeps the buyer updated on the same chat; the seller shares one branded link for everything else, the buyer orders without signing up, and an automated COD-confirmation flow cuts RTO losses that are measured back to the seller in rupees saved per month.

2. The problem
Instagram/WhatsApp-first sellers (fashion, jewellery, home decor, boutiques) run real businesses inside chat threads:

Orders live in DMs. Item, size, address and payment preference are scattered across a conversation in Hinglish. The seller retypes it into a notes app or a screenshot.
Every DM wants an answer — now. "Price?", "M size hai?", "COD milega?", "kitne din me aayega?" — the same questions hundreds of times a week, often late at night. A buyer left on seen for an hour has usually bought somewhere else. The seller either lives in the inbox or loses sales, and cannot afford staff to reply all day.
20–30% of COD orders are refused on delivery (RTO). The seller eats forward + reverse shipping, packaging and blocked inventory. A seller doing 150 COD orders/month at ₹800 AOV with 35% RTO loses roughly ₹8,000–15,000/month.
Payments are chased manually. Bare UPI QR codes have no order context, no proof, no trust.
"Bhaiya order kahan hai?" — status questions flood the DM inbox because the buyer has nowhere to track.
No numbers. No revenue trend, no repeat rate, no best-seller list, no customer history, no invoices.
Existing tools assume the buyer will leave Instagram. Storefront builders (Dukaan, DotPe) hand the seller a website. Buyers don't go there — the DM is the shop. Anything that breaks the conversation loses the sale.
3. The insight
Don't move the seller off chat. Instrument the chat — then automate it.

The order still starts in a DM. CartHedge sits behind it: AI turns the conversation into a structured order, one link carries the buyer from interest → details → payment → tracking without a signup, and everything lands on a single board with real analytics behind it. The storefront is a bonus surface, not the product.

Most DMs are the same few questions and the same order details. An assistant that knows the catalog as well as the seller, talks like the seller, closes the order inside the conversation and knows when to hand over turns the inbox from a full-time job into a queue of ready orders.

4. What the system actually is
Three products fused into one platform, sold together and configurable per plan:

AI sales assistant + order capture — answers buyers in their DMs automatically, collects the order in the chat, confirms it and places it (or hands the seller a ready draft). A chat pasted from anywhere still becomes an order card in one tap.
Zero-friction buyer link + storefront — one branded URL that handles browse, details, OTP, payment, COD confirmation and tracking. No buyer app, no buyer account.
Seller business console — orders, catalog, inventory, customers, offers, broadcasts, invoices, analytics, billing.
Plus a platform admin console for CartHedge staff to run it as a SaaS business.

7. Core loop (the product in seven steps)
Buyer DMs the seller — "pink wali kurti hai? price?" — in Hinglish, Hindi or English, at any hour.
The AI assistant answers within seconds, as the shop: price, sizes, stock, delivery time, COD — straight from the seller's catalog and notes. It sells like a good salesperson (one real product highlight, an in-stock alternative when something is out) and asks only for what is missing: size, then name, number and address, then payment. It never invents a price, discount or date; anything it cannot answer goes to the seller.
Order summary in the chat: exact items, delivery charge and total — calculated by CartHedge, not by the AI. The buyer replies "haan" to confirm; any change gets a fresh summary.
Order placed — instantly (auto-confirm on) or as a ready draft the seller approves in one tap (the default). The buyer gets the order code and a tracking/payment link in the same chat.
Payment path splits:
Prepaid → payment link in chat → buyer pays via UPI intent / card → order auto-confirmed.
COD → the buyer's "yes" to the exact summary in chat is the confirmation, with an optional ₹50–100 UPI token; link and storefront orders run the automated confirmation flow (order summary → address confirm → intent re-confirm before dispatch). This is the RTO killer.
Order moves on one kanban board — new → confirmed → packed → shipped → delivered (or rto / cancelled), with courier handoff and automatic buyer status updates on the same chat the order came from ("order kahan hai?" is answered by the assistant too).
RTO savings meter shows "you saved ₹X this month" — the retention anchor, stated in rupees.
Alternative entry: the seller skips the AI and just shares a link (product / cart / custom), or the buyer finds the item on the storefront and orders directly. All three paths converge on the same order board.

8.7 Seller — AI sales assistant (automated DMs)
One-tap connect: the seller logs in with Instagram (official Instagram API — no password sharing, disconnect anytime). WhatsApp connect runs on the same pipeline next.
Replies to every DM within seconds, 24×7, as the shop — short, warm, salesperson tone, in the buyer's own language and script (Hinglish, Hindi, English, regional scripts).
Grounded answers only: prices, sizes, stock and product details from the catalog; delivery charge and COD/online options; the seller's own notes (delivery time, exchange policy, sizing). It never invents prices, discounts, stock or dates — anything unknown, it checks with the seller.
Sells, not just answers: one genuine product highlight, an available alternative when an item or size is out of stock, the closest matches when the buyer is vague ("pink wali kurti"), and a soft close.
Collects the order the way a person would: item → size → name, number and address together → payment. Returning buyers are offered their last address.
Exact order summary card in the chat (computed by CartHedge, not the model); only a "yes" to that exact summary counts.
Auto-confirm setting: off by default — the buyer's yes becomes a ready draft the seller approves in one tap; on — the order is placed instantly. Auto-reply can be switched off anytime (chats then only become drafts).
After the order: order code + tracking/payment link in the same chat; answers "order kahan hai?" with live status, courier and tracking id; a repeat order starts fresh without re-adding old items.
Knows when to step back: complaints, refunds/returns, cancelling a placed order, bargaining, bulk deals or a buyer asking for a person → hands the chat to the seller with a WhatsApp/email alert and stays out until the seller resumes it.
The seller stays in control: replying themselves — from CartHedge or straight from the Instagram app — pauses the assistant on that chat; pause/resume per chat.
One gentle follow-up if a buyer goes quiet mid-order; ignores "ok", emojis and spam; caps automatic replies per chat to stop loops.
Honest by design: never claims to be human — asked "bot ho?", it says it is the shop's assistant and the owner also sees the chat.
Live inbox on the AI desk: every chat with its state — needs you, confirm order, awaiting buyer, AI paused, order placed — and who wrote each message (buyer / AI / seller).
Works inside Meta's rules: replies only within the 24-hour window after the buyer's message; human handover; data-deletion and deauthorize callbacks.

Subscription gating: expired/suspended → seller APIs and the public storefront both stop, with a clear renew path (billing stays reachable so they can pay).

12. What makes it different
It sells in the DM, automatically. Not a chatbot menu — a catalog-grounded assistant that talks like the seller, answers in the buyer's language, closes the order inside the chat and knows when to hand over.
The DM stays the shop. Competitors move the buyer to a website; CartHedge instruments the conversation and treats the storefront as an option.
Sold in rupees saved, not features shipped. The RTO savings meter is the retention mechanic.
Zero buyer friction. No signup, no app, no password — phone OTP only. Conversion beats a bare UPI QR because the buyer feels protected.
The link is branded per business, so buyers trust it and sellers build their own name — not CartHedge's.
Money never touches CartHedge. Sellers use their own payment account. Removes the biggest trust objection.
Data moat. Per-seller COD-refusal history today → a network-level buyer risk score tomorrow → an insurable RTO-protection product after that.
Couriers are rails, not competitors. Aggregators are integrated as a handoff step, not fought.
