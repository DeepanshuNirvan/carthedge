# Panelist 04: Devanshi Rathod, Rajkot (oxidised and silver-plated jewellery)

## Profile

- Age 32, Rajkot, Gujarat. Speaks Gujarati and Hindi at home, English for work, comfortable with apps.
- Business: "Rathod Silver Story", oxidised and silver-plated jewellery (jhumkas, anklets, necklace sets, rings). Instagram is the main channel; she also sells on Meesho (Meesho's RTO is Meesho's problem, so she ignores it in this panel).
- Numbers (Instagram only): about 350 orders a month, average order value 650 rupees, so roughly 2.27 lakh rupees a month in sales. 75% COD (about 263 COD orders), 32% of COD refused at the door (about 84 parcels a month).
- RTO maths she has done herself: each refused parcel costs about 240 rupees all in (forward and return shipping about 170, packing about 25, damaged or lost pieces and blocked stock about 45). 84 x 240 = about 20,000 rupees a month. Her biggest pain.
- Stack today: Shiprocket (pay per shipment, no subscription), WhatsApp Business app, Instagram, Meesho seller panel, Canva, Vyapar for invoices. She once looked at Bik.ai and GoKwik and found them priced for big D2C brands. She tried Shiprocket's WhatsApp COD confirmation for two months: refusals went from about 35% to 32%, because a buyer's "yes" costs nothing.
- People: husband Jaydeep (private bank job, the one who checks her numbers), mother-in-law at home, son aged 6. Part-time helper Kinjal (7,000 rupees a month) packs parcels and replies to DMs in the evening. CA Bhatt (family friend, 1,500 rupees a month) does her GST. She is in a 25-person WhatsApp group of Rajkot jewellery sellers.
- Spend: Meta ads about 25,000 rupees a month, Kinjal 7,000, CA 1,500, Canva and Vyapar about 800.
- Mindset: willing to pay well for anything that provably cuts RTO, but wants the rupee maths and a way to audit it. Distrusts claims like "refusals fall to 8%".

## Round 1

### Stage 1. Cold first impression

Scrolling at night after Kabir slept, this sponsored post comes up: "Every DM answered. Every order closed in the chat."

- What I think it is: another Instagram auto-reply bot, like the ManyChat or Bik type, that answers "price?" and takes the address. The "you approve with a tap" line makes it sound a bit more careful than a dumb bot. The name CartHedge sounds like a share-market app, not something for sellers.
- Would I tap: yes, probably, out of curiosity, because replying at night is a real drag and "no card" removes the risk of tapping. But I would be tapping with half a mind, because the ad says nothing about COD or RTO, and that is the only thing that hurts me in rupees.
- What I'd suspect: (1) Will it say something wrong to a buyer, like a wrong price or "real silver hai" when it is plated? (2) Does it work in Gujarati and Roman Gujarati, or only Hinglish? (3) Is Instagram going to restrict my account for using a bot? That page is my whole business. (4) 15 days free, then a surprise charge. (5) Who are these people, are there any real sellers using it?
- What I'd need to see next: a demo with a jewellery chat, a price that is not in dollars, one real seller from Gujarat, a line about COD and refusals, and whether it connects to Shiprocket.

### Stage 3. Learning the product

I read the product definition and the website words.

What is good: the COD flow is the heart of it (summary, address confirm, intent re-confirm, optional 50 to 100 rupee UPI token), the pincode serviceability check at checkout, OTP so a fake number cannot order, serial refusers flagged in the customer ledger, the savings meter in rupees, AI order drafts I approve with a tap, GST invoices and credit notes my CA will like, and the money going to my own Razorpay.

What bothers me, specifically:

1. For orders that start in a DM, the "COD confirmation" is the buyer replying "haan" to the summary. The token is optional. That is exactly the free "yes" that failed me on Shiprocket. The strong flow (OTP, address confirm, intent re-confirm) is for link and storefront orders. So to get real RTO protection I must push DM buyers to the link or make the token compulsory.
2. The website calculator assumes refusals fall to about 8%. The product document says "to about 20%". The two disagree. I will plan on 20%, and I will believe even that only after I see my own numbers.
3. The "RTO went from 27% to 9%" quote and the 31,000 rupees saved quote are in the website words, and there is a testimonial from "Devanshi P., silver jewellery, Rajkot, 2x reseller volume". I am Devanshi R., silver jewellery, Rajkot, and I have never used this. The file says it is a seed placeholder, but if it shows on the live site it is a fake review with my kind of profile, and I would report the ad and tell the whole Rajkot group. Remove it before launch.
4. Automatic DM replies are only on Pro (1,999). Growth only makes drafts from DMs. The ad promises "every DM answered" without saying which plan.
5. Languages listed: Hinglish, Hindi, English, and scripts like Tamil, Bengali, Telugu, Kannada. I do not see Gujarati anywhere.
6. WhatsApp: status updates and COD messages go from CartHedge's own number, not mine. My buyers will see an unknown number. Auto-replies on my own number is "coming next".
7. Photo and story matching is "coming". Half of my DMs are a screenshot of a Reel with "ye wala price?". Without that the AI will keep saying "send me the product name".
8. Nothing says it syncs two-way with Shiprocket, so I do not know how the RTO meter learns that a parcel was refused.
9. "Order quota": unclear whether a refused order, a cancelled order or a draft I never approve counts toward the 500.

### Stage 4. The interview

**1. In my words, what does it do? What confused me?**
It is an AI that answers my Instagram DMs in the buyer's language from my catalog, collects size, name and address, shows an order summary, the buyer says haan, and I approve with one tap. Then the buyer gets a link to pay or track. Plus an order board, returns, GST invoices, and a COD confirmation flow that is supposed to cut refusals, with a meter that shows me the rupees saved.
Confused me: which plan gives auto-reply versus drafts; what counts as an "order" in the quota; that DM-origin COD orders get only a free "haan" while link orders get the real flow; whether Gujarati works; whether it talks to Shiprocket; why the site says 8% and the doc says 20%; the fake-looking Devanshi testimonial.

**2. Top 3 things that excite me, ranked**
1. COD confirmation with the 50 to 100 rupee token, OTP and pincode check, plus the rupee savings meter. My loss is about 20,000 rupees a month. If I save even 7,000 net it pays for itself several times.
2. Serial refusers flagged in the customer ledger by phone number. If a buyer refused twice, I want COD hidden for them. This is the real RTO cut.
3. Auto-replies at night and drafts I approve with a tap. Navratri and Rakhi stories bring 100+ DMs overnight. Kinjal only comes in the evening, and a buyer left on seen is gone to another Instagram seller.

**3. Top 3 doubts, fears or deal-breakers**
1. RTO claims are unproven for jewellery. 8% is fantasy. DM orders only need a free "haan". 15 days of trial cannot show the result because refusals arrive 10 to 14 days after dispatch. Deal-breaker if I cannot see before and after in rupees from my own parcels.
2. The AI saying something wrong to a buyer: a wrong price, a promise of "colour nahi jayega", a discount I never gave, or a bad Gujarati message to a Saurashtra customer. And Instagram account risk: if my page gets restricted, I am finished. Deal-breaker if there is no one-tap off switch (there is one, good) and no export of my chats and customers.
3. A new company with no real reviews, sending messages to my buyers from an unknown WhatsApp number that carries no "Rathod Silver Story" name. Also price creeping up as I grow (percent of sales or surprise per-order fees).

**4. Missing features I would need**
- Shiprocket two-way sync: book the shipment from CartHedge, pull back delivered, NDR and RTO status, feed the RTO meter with real data. MUST HAVE before I pay.
- Gujarati replies (Gujarati script and Roman Gujarati like "kitna ma malse", "che"). MUST HAVE before I pay for the AI plan.
- Compulsory token, or a prepaid nudge, on DM orders: payment link in the DM with "pay 50 to confirm COD", and a small prepaid discount (30 rupees off). MUST HAVE; this is the part that actually works.
- Auto-rule: hide COD, or force token, for any buyer with 1+ refusal or a risky pincode. MUST HAVE.
- My brand name in the messages buyers receive ("Rathod Silver Story, order confirm karein"). MUST HAVE, even if it still comes from CartHedge's number during the trial. Sending from my own number: nice later but strongly wanted.
- Screenshot and story/Reel matching for "ye wala" DMs. MUST HAVE before I pay for the Pro-level AI. Comment-to-DM on Reels ("price" comments): nice later, but I would use it daily.
- A second login for Kinjal (limited to orders and chats, no billing). MUST HAVE.
- Setup help for 500+ designs, because my photos live on Instagram, not in a spreadsheet. MUST HAVE as a service (see add-ons); IG-to-catalog import would be nice later.
- Network-level refuser list across sellers (phone number plus pincode risk score). Nice later, but it is the thing I would stay for.
- Meesho orders in the same ledger. Nice later.

**5. Features I would never use**
Live selling (I do not do lives), reseller price tier (I do not run a reseller programme right now), back-in-stock waitlist (my designs are limited drops), accounting exports beyond what my CA already gets, the storefront as a browsing site (my buyers live in the DM, I would use only the checkout link), "best broadcast window" insights.

**6. What I spend today and how I like to pay**
Kinjal 7,000, CA 1,500, Canva and Vyapar about 800, Meta ads about 25,000, Shiprocket per parcel (no subscription). Tools-only spend is about 2,300 a month. I like UPI AutoPay monthly, so I can stop it myself. No card on file for a trial. I would pay yearly upfront only after about three months of proof, not before.

**7. Price sensitivity (for the plan I would actually need: COD flow plus auto-replies, which today means Pro)**
My rule of thumb: I pay about 20 to 25% of the net money a tool proves it saves me.
- (a) So cheap I would doubt it: below 600 a month. "AI plus RTO plus invoices for 499? Kuch catch hai."
- (b) A bargain: 1,000 to 1,500 a month.
- (c) Getting expensive but I would still pay: 2,500 to 3,500, only if the meter shows 10,000+ net saved for two months running.
- (d) Too expensive: 4,500 and above, or anything that grows with my sales.
Rupee maths on my numbers (263 COD orders, 84 refused, 240 per refusal): at the site's 8% I would save about 63 parcels, about 15,100 a month. At my realistic 20% I save about 31 parcels, about 7,400. At a pessimistic 26% I save only about 16 parcels, about 3,800. Subtract friction: a 50 rupee token will scare off some genuine buyers, say 4 to 6% of COD orders, about 2,000 to 3,000 in lost margin. So my honest net is about 4,500 to 5,500 a month at 20%. That supports 1,000 to 1,500, not 3,000, until the meter proves more.

Festive months (Diwali, Rakhi) I touch 600 to 700 orders, so I like Growth's 2.50 overflow, but I want it capped or warned before it bills.

- What makes me upgrade: more volume, auto-reply in Gujarati, my own WhatsApp number. What makes me leave: refusals not falling after 2 months, one embarrassing AI mistake with a buyer, Instagram trouble, price creep, or no export of my customers.
- Flat versus per-order versus percent of sales: I trust a flat fee. Per-order only for overflow, capped. Percent of sales never: 2% of 2.27 lakh is 4,500 a month for doing nothing extra. If CartHedge is so sure about RTO savings, a small result-linked part would impress me, but only if I can audit the baseline from my own Shiprocket data.

**9. Free plan or free trial? How long? Annual discount?**
Trial, not a free plan. 15 days is too short for RTO, I need 30 days, or 15 days plus a money-back first paid month if refusals do not drop by an agreed number of points. A tiny free plan (20 orders a month, COD flow only) would get me and my sister in Surat started, but I do not need it. Annual: not at the start. After 3 months of proof, 2 months free (about 17% off) is fine; 25% would tempt me.

## Round 2 notes

- Now believe: the token friction is bigger than I assumed. Pooja pays UPI only to a seller who already delivered once, so new buyers (who refuse most) will balk at a token. My friction estimate moves from 4 to 6% to 8 to 10% of COD orders, so net saving at 20% RTO drops from about 4,500 to 5,500 to about 3,000 a month.
- Current price range: bargain 800 to 1,200 a month (was 1,000 to 1,500); 2,500 to 3,500 only if the meter shows 10,000+ net two months running; 4,500+ no. Flat fee, overflow capped and warned at 80%, WhatsApp messages in a prepaid wallet at Meta cost, today's price written for 12 months (Vikram's chatbot price-jump story). Starter at 350 orders (1,249) costing more than Growth is a trap to fix.
- New fears and asks: scam pages getting free pretty links on a no-card trial could make my buyers distrust every CartHedge link, so seller verification must be real. CA Bhatt and Meenal both ask when the invoice is raised for a COD parcel that returns RTO and whether a credit note follows. Gujarati still missing.
- Agree with: Pooja (token adjusted in COD, no convenience fee), Meenal (GST and Gujarati gaps), Vikram (price lock, written cap). Push back on Hiren: a quota is a per-order fee in disguise, so tiers with capped overflow are fine. Asked Hiren whether Interakt's COD confirmation cut his 30%; I expect the same free-"yes" failure I saw on Shiprocket (35% to 32%).

## Round 3 notes

- Rohit's prepaid nudge test: my net RTO gain drops to ₹1,500 at 20% with 8 to 10% friction. Real win only if CartHedge shows prepaid in the DM (not just token) so first-time buyers choose it themselves. I need to retest Shiprocket with a discount nudge before I commit.
- Plan I would design: Growth ₹999 for 500 orders, ₹2 per overflow capped ₹200/month, auto-reply add-on ₹500. Total ₹1,499 in Diwali, ₹999 normally. Makes me pay: prepaid in the DM shown at checkout summary. Makes me cancel: credit note loop unclear or Gujarati still missing at month 2.
- GST credit note gap is my blocker now, not RTO savings. Meenal is right: my 84 refused parcels monthly become 84 credit notes if invoice at dispatch, else my GSTR-1 overstates. CartHedge must write the flow before I trial. Also: disagree with Vikram on one-size pricing. Growth at ₹999 for me, Pro at ₹1,499 for Hiren, not ₹1,999 for both.

## Round 4 vote

- **Vote:** Growth ₹1,299, yes after trial. The 30-day trial and auto-reply in Growth (not Pro-only) remove my biggest fears; the hard cap and price lock are written safeguards I trust.
- **Blockers:** GST credit note flow still unstated (decides RTO compliance), Gujarati still missing (my buyers live there). Will trial, but no paid commitment until both ship and Shiprocket sync feeds the meter real refusal data.
