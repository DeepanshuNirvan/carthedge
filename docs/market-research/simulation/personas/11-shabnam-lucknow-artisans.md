# Panelist 11: Shabnam Ansari, Lucknowi Taar (Lucknow)

## Profile

- **Who:** Shabnam Ansari, 26, Lucknow (mohalla near Chowk). Runs "Lucknowi Taar" with her elder sister Rehana (31). Hand-embroidered chikankari kurtas made by about 14 women artisans in the neighbourhood, paid per piece, cash, every week.
- **Split of work:** Shabnam does photos, Instagram, chatting with buyers and packing. Rehana handles the karigars, the cash and the courier pickups. Abbu (retired) worries about "online fraud". Cousin Danish bhai set up the Instagram page a year ago and still fixes things for free.
- **Numbers:** about 25 orders a month, average order about 2,200 rupees, so roughly 55,000 rupees of sales. After karigar payment, thread, packing and courier she thinks she keeps around 30 percent, about 16,000 rupees, and that has to feed two households. Monsoon months drop to 8 to 10 orders, festive and wedding season goes to 40 plus.
- **Selling style:** Most buyers DM on Instagram, then move to WhatsApp for address and payment. A lot of chatting (fabric, "asli chikankari hai na?", measurements, delivery date, "thoda kam karo") before a UPI payment on her own QR. Mostly prepaid, very little COD.
- **Language:** Hindi and Urdu natively, types Roman Hindi/Urdu, basic English. Words like kanban, ledger, HSN, quota feel foreign.
- **Tech and money habits:** Android phone, Instagram and WhatsApp Business, free Canva. Has never paid for any software. No Razorpay account, no GST registration (turnover about 6.5 lakh a year), no CA of her own. Pays by UPI, does not trust autopay. Thinks in "kitna padega per order".
- **What she wants:** to look as professional as the big brands, and to stop losing buyers who message at night or while she is sitting with the karigars.
- **Represents:** first-time micro sellers.

## Round 1

### Stage 1. Cold first impression

Instagram pe scroll kar rahi thi, raat ke 11 baje, ye sponsored post aaya: "Every DM answered. Every order closed in the chat."

**What I think it is:** Ek robot jo mere DMs ka jawab dega, aur order bhi le lega. Jaise ManyChat jaisa kuch, jiske baare mein Danish bhai ne bataya tha. "You approve with a tap" ka matlab mujhe poora samajh nahi aaya: kya mujhe har order pe ek button dabana padega? Shayad. "Catalog" bhi bola, matlab mujhe saare designs wahan daalne padenge.

**Would I tap?** Haan, ek baar tap karungi, sirf ye dekhne ke liye ki price kitna hai. "15 days free, no card" achha laga, card to mere paas online use ke liye hai hi nahi. Par sign up nahi karungi jab tak price na dekh lun. Mera dhyan sabse pehle "free" ke baad kya hai pe jata hai.

**What I'd suspect:**
- "Free trial" ke baad chupke se paise na kaat lein. Mujhe autopay se dar lagta hai.
- Bot galat price ya galat date bata de, aur buyer se jhagda mere upar aaye. Mere kurte 10 se 15 din mein bante hain, bot "3 din mein" bol de to?
- Meri Instagram id ban na ho jaye. Suna hai automation wale tools se account block hota hai.
- Ye bade sellers ke liye hoga, 25 order wale ke liye nahi.
- Comments mein koi review nahi hai, naya sa lagta hai.

**What I'd need to see next:** Pehle price, rupees mein, saaf. Phir Hindi mein 1 minute ki video jisme kisi chikankari ya kurti wale ki dukaan pe dikhaye ki bot kaise baat karta hai. Aur ye ki mera Instagram safe hai (official hai ya nahi).

### Stage 3. What I learned from the product files

Maine product.txt aur website ke shabd padhe. Seedhi baatein:

- **Sabse bada jhatka:** Ad mein likha hai "every DM answered" (auto reply), par product.txt ke hisaab se **automatic DM reply sirf Pro plan (1,999 rupees)** mein hai. Starter (499) mein koi AI hai hi nahi, sirf order link aur board. Growth (999) mein AI sirf DM se order ka draft banata hai, jawab mujhe khud dena padega. ### Stage 4. The interview

**1. In your words, what does it do? What confused you?**

Ye mere Instagram DMs ka jawab apne aap deta hai, Hindi ya Hinglish mein, mere catalog se dekh ke (price, size, stock). Phir buyer se naam, number, address leta hai, summary dikhata hai, buyer "haan" bolta hai, aur order mere board pe aa jata hai. Main ek tap se approve karti hoon. Saath mein ek link/dukaan mil jati hai jahan buyer OTP se order karta hai aur tracking dekh sakta hai. Mere hisaab se ye ek AI naukar hai jo raat ko bhi jaagta hai.

Confused: (1) "Approve with a tap": har order pe mujhe notification milega aur main button dabaungi, theek hai, par mere jaise kaam mein "haan" ke baad bhi measurements puchni hoti hain. (2) Ad aur price list mein mismatch (upar). (3) Razorpay. (4) Kitna English: kanban, quota, ledger, HSN, "AI desk". (5) Kaun sa chat: Instagram ya WhatsApp? Ad ne dono jaisa suggest kiya, par WhatsApp abhi nahi hai.

**2. Top 3 that excite me, ranked**

1. **Raat ko aur karigar ke saath baithne pe bhi jawab.** Main din bhar karigaron ke saath ya packing mein hoti hoon, phone silent. Raat 11 baje message aata hai, subah tak buyer kisi aur ko order de deta hai. Agar sach mein ye mere jaise Hinglish mein jawab de, to mere liye 3 se 4 order extra mahina bhi bahut hai (ek order 2,200, mera munafa lagbhag 700).
2. **Apna branded link aur order tracking.** "Lucknowi Taar" ka apna link, QR, aur buyer khud dekh le order kahan hai. Abhi main Delhivery ka screenshot bhej-bhej ke thak gayi hoon. Aur ye mujhe bade brand jaisa dikhayega, jo mera sapna hai.
3. **Saare order ek jagah.** Notebook, WhatsApp scroll, screenshots sab bhulne wale hain. Rehana bhi dekh sake ki kitne order aaye, aur WhatsApp pe alert aaye.

**3. Top 3 doubts / deal-breakers**

1. **Price aur plan ka khel.** Jo mujhe chahiye (auto reply) wo 1,999 mein hai. Wo mere liye nahi ho sakta. Deal-breaker: agar 500 se kam mein auto reply nahi mila to main nahi lungi. ReplyKaro 99 mein keyword reply de deta hai, tum 499 kyun?
2. **Bot galat bol de.** Delivery date (mere kaam mein 10 se 15 din), bargaining, "ye asli chikankari hai", fabric, naap. Ek galat wada aur meri reputation gayi. Buyers ko pata chal gaya ki bot hai to bharosa kam hoga. "Chat hi mera bharosa hai."
3. **Naya aur bina review.** Koi aur Lucknow ya Delhi ka chhota seller bol de ki use kiya hai, tab main manungi. Aur Instagram id ka risk. Deal-breaker bhi: autopay jo mujhse chupke se paisa kaat le, ya "per order fee" jo bhaag jaye.

**4. Missing features I need**

- **Must have before I pay:**
  - **Hindi (aur ideally Urdu) mein dashboard.** Rehana ki English mujhse bhi kam hai. Sab kuch Hindi mein ho to hi wo manegi.
  - **Plain UPI se kaam.** Mere paas Razorpay nahi hai. Buyer mere UPI pe pay kare, "maine pay kar diya" dabaye, main verify karun. Ye bina Razorpay ke aur bina extra form ke chalna chahiye.
  - **Naap (measurements) aur "banne mein kitne din".** Chikankari aksar naap ke hisaab se banti hai. AI ko bust, length, sleeve puchna aana chahiye, aur har design pe "10 se 15 din" jaisa likha hua time dena. AI ko ye kabhi apne se nahi badalna chahiye.
  - **Advance + baaki paisa.** Naap wale kurte pe 50 percent advance, baaki delivery se pehle. Ye mere kaam ki jaan hai.
  - **Meri apni WhatsApp number pe auto reply** (jo coming hai). Mere chats WhatsApp pe khatam hote hain. Bina iske main 1,999 ya 999 to kya 499 bhi sochungi nahi, par agar Instagram hi chahiye to wait kar sakti hoon.
  - **Photo/screenshot match ("ye wala kitne ka")**: mere buyers mere hi post ka screenshot bhejte hain. Ye coming hai, aur iske bina AI bahut adhoora lagega.
  - **Aisa plan jo 25 order pe 500 rupees se neeche ho.**
- **Nice later:**
  - Voice note samajhna (mere purane customers, auntiyan, voice note bhejti hain).
  - Urdu script (Nastaliq) mein jawab (Roman Urdu abhi chal jayegi).
  - Artisan kahani wala page ("haath se bana, ye karigar ne banaya") taaki trust bane.
  - Buyer ke photo aur review dukaan pe.
  - Sasti courier booking (Delhivery, India Post).
  - Limited piece ka "sirf 2 bache hain" dikhana (stock se ho sakta hai, par achha dikhna chahiye).

**5. Features I would never use**

Broadcast consent aur START/STOP wali baatein. Admin, MRR sab dikhta hai par mera nahi. Insights panel shayad ek mahine mein ek baar dekhungi. Dropped checkout. Packing slip, pick list (25 order mein haath se ho jata hai).

**6. What I spend today, and how I pay**

Software pe 0 rupaye. Canva free, WhatsApp Business free, Instagram free, Danish bhai ki madad free. Kabhi kabhi Diwali ya Eid pe reel boost karti hoon, lagbhag 500 se 1,500 rupees us mahine. Agar autopay hi ho to maximum limit khud set kar sakun aur hamesha SMS aaye. Saal bhar ka ek saath dena abhi mushkil, kyunki karigaron ko hafta wise paisa dena hota hai.

**7. Price sensitivity, for the plan I'd actually need (auto reply on Instagram, 25 orders a month), in rupees per month**

- (a) **So cheap I'd doubt it:** 99 rupees se kam. ReplyKaro 99 mein hai par wo keyword wala hai. AI bot 99 mein ho to main soch lungi "kuch dikkat hai", ya "free hai to data bech rahe honge".
- (b) **A bargain:** 199 se 299 rupees. 25 order pe 8 se 12 rupees per order.
- (c) **Getting expensive but still pay:** 499 rupees, bas tab jab 2 mahine mein dikh jaye ki 3 order extra aaye. 499 = 20 rupees per order, aur ek extra order hi 2,200 ka hai.
- (d) **Too expensive:** 799 aur uske upar. 999 aadhe order ki value hai, 1,999 ek poore order ki. Ye main khud ke liye sochna bhi band kar deti hoon.

**8. Reaction to the price list**

- **Which plan fits me:** Koi nahi. 25 order ko 100 order ki jarurat nahi, aur jo feature chahiye wo upar ke plan mein hai. Starter 499 mein AI hi nahi hai. Mujhe "Pro ke feature Starter ke daam pe" chahiye, ya ek naya chhota plan 249 ya 299 mein, 30 order tak.
- **Would I pay after the trial:** Agar auto reply Instagram pe 499 se kam mein ho, aur trial mein mere 8 se 10 buyers ne bot se achhi baat ki ho, haan. 499 pe sochungi, 999 pe nahi.
- **What makes me upgrade:** Jab orders 60 se 80 ho jayein (festive season) aur main thak jaun. Ya jab WhatsApp pe auto reply aa jaye aur kaam kare.
- **What makes me leave:** Bot ek galat wada kare. Support Hindi mein na mile. Trial ke baad autopay se paise kat jayein. Ya jab monsoon mein 8 order aayein aur main phir bhi 499 deti rahun.
- **Flat vs per-order vs % of sales:**
  - Sabse zyada bharosa **per order fee** pe: "jitna kaam utna paisa", aur jis mahine order kam hon to kam dena. 25 order pe 5 se 8 rupees per order = 125 se 200, mujhe ye samajh aata hai. Par saaf likha ho ki kab lagta hai (delivered pe ya bas order bana pe).
  - Phir **flat fee**: pata hai kitna dena hai, par monsoon mein dard hota hai.
  - **% of sales** pe bharosa nahi. "Ye to Meesho ka commission jaisa hai." Jab mera ek 2,200 ka order aaye aur 5 percent ka matlab 110 rupees jaye, to bura lagta hai. Aur wo wada ("no cut") bhi tod deta hai.
  - Sabse achha mujhe lagta hai: ek **prepaid pack**, jaise mobile recharge: 500 rupees mein 100 order, 6 mahine tak valid. Order kam aaye to paisa jalta nahi.

## Round 2 notes

- **What I now believe:** Per-order pricing is not automatically safe. Vikram's story (chatbot raised from 1,500 to 3,800) taught me an uncapped per-order fee can run away too. I want a written monthly cap or a prepaid pack (about 500 rupees for 100 orders, 6 months valid). Autopay is optional per product.txt ("by hand each month"), and per-order fees only start above quota (Starter quota 100, I do 25), so my autopay fear is smaller than I thought, but I still want it in writing.
- **Price range (unchanged):** 199 to 299 a bargain, 499 only after 2 months show 3 extra orders, 799 and above no. WhatsApp message pack fine at about 1.30 to 1.50 rupees per message (Meta 0.86 plus GST is about 1 rupee), never 3. Trial 30 days from Instagram connect, free plan first.
- **Learned about CartHedge:** Razorpay is not mandatory (UPI ID plus "buyer reported payment" alert exist), but I saw nothing for 50 percent advance plus balance, and nothing that stops the bot overwriting my 10 to 15 day making time.
- **Agree with:** Sneha (advance, delivery time, made-to-order needs), Ritu (screenshot DMs; but her 499 to 799 "bargain" is for 180 orders, not my 25), Vikram (cap in writing), Pooja (bot must say it is AI, owner handover in one tap).
- **Disagree with:** Hiren (never per-order; fine at 1,200 orders, wrong for me), Arjun, Tanya and Kabir (they find 1,999 cheap; so the fix is a small-seller plan, not a Pro price cut). Open question for Ritu: who answers "asli chikankari hai na?", because I will not let a bot answer that.

## Round 3 notes

- **Plan I'd design:** Artisan Pro ₹299/month or ₹1,500/6 months prepaid, auto-reply on Instagram+WhatsApp, advance-balance split, custom time/measurements, no Razorpay forced, no autopay. For 25-50 orders, breaks the feature-count trap Ritu/Hiren/Farheen hit.
- **One feature to pay this month:** Hindi dashboard (Rehana ka acceptance) + advance/balance logic for stitching work. Without these two I stay on WhatsApp spreadsheet, money or no money.
- **One feature to cancel:** Bot saying stitching time wrong (mine is 10-15 din, it must learn this, never override) or measurements giving bad advice. Trust is my only edge, bot destroys it in one order. Also if autopay surprise happens, band.
- **Disagree with:** Hiren says "per-order never", but my maths changes at low volume. His annual commitment also wrong for me: monsoon 8 orders, Diwali 40, annual prepaid bleeds. 3-month trial then 6-month prepaid packs fit.

## Round 4 vote

- **Stayed on Free**: My 25 orders fit exactly; Growth at ₹1,299 is a whole order's price. Fairness rating 3 because safeguards (hard cap, WhatsApp prepaid, price lock) are real, but Hindi dashboard missing and auto-reply still too expensive.
- **Deal-maker to upgrade**: Auto-reply + Hindi dashboard + advance-balance split at ₹499 (Starter level). Right now I stay on Free and DM WhatsApp myself until the plan catches up to my needs.
