# Panelist 01: Ritu Agarwal, Ritu's Kurti House, Jaipur

## Profile

- Age 38, owner of "Ritu's Kurti House", Jaipur. Cotton and chikankari kurtis and co-ord sets.
- Channels: Instagram (about 24k followers), plus a WhatsApp broadcast list of 1,100 past buyers.
- About 180 orders a month, average order ₹1,100, so roughly ₹1.98 lakh a month in sales.
- 65% cash on delivery (about 117 orders), about 25% of COD comes back as RTO (about 29 parcels a month).
- Husband books shipping on Shiprocket in the evenings. One part-time helper (₹6,000 a month) answers DMs 11am to 6pm. Ritu answers the rest at night.
- Tools today: WhatsApp Business app, Instagram, Shiprocket, an Excel sheet and a notebook. Tool spend is close to ₹0 a month apart from the helper.
- Burned once: about ₹6,000 for a yearly Dukaan website two years ago that got almost no traffic. She still mentions it.
- Speaks Hinglish, comfortable on a phone, rarely opens a laptop. Decisions are discussed with her husband, who is careful with money.
- Working numbers I use for her (her own rough estimates, not facts): one RTO costs her about ₹150 (forward plus reverse courier plus packing), so about ₹4,400 a month. Margin on a kurti is about 35 to 40%, so about ₹400 per order.

## Round 1

### Stage 1. Cold first impression (the Instagram ad)

**What I think it is.** Ek chatbot jo DM me auto-reply karta hai. Jaise un apps ke ads aate hain "auto reply on Instagram". Par isme "order closed in the chat" likha hai, to lagta hai ye sirf "price kya hai" ka jawab nahi deta, order bhi le leta hai. "You approve with a tap" sunke achha laga, matlab bina mere haan ke kuch nahi hoga.

**Would I tap?** Haan, shayad. Sirf isliye ki "15 days free, no card" likha hai. Dukaan ke time mujhe pehle hi din card aur yearly payment pe daal diya tha. Main tap karungi, par signup nahi, bas page dekhungi. Pehle screenshot lekar husband ko bhejungi.

**What I'd suspect.**
- Naam "CartHedge" kuch finance ya share market jaisa lagta hai, kurti wale se koi connection nahi. Kaun si company hai, kahan ki hai?
- "In their language" ka matlab kya? Hinglish samjhega? Mere customers "pink wali hai kya, M me?" likhte hain, "Hello, is the pink kurti available in medium?" nahi.
- Instagram pe auto-reply se mera account restrict ho gaya to? Mera 24k ka account hai, wahi meri dukaan hai.
- Free ke baad kitna? Ad me price kahin nahi hai. Jab price chhupaya jaata hai to mehenga hota hai.

**What I'd need to see next.** Ek chhota Hindi video ya reel, kisi asli seller ki chat dikhate hue, phone pe. Price rupees me, GST ke saath ya bina. Kaun log chala rahe hain. Aur koi seller jise main jaanti hoon ya Jaipur ke groups me jiska naam suna ho.

### Stage 2. Looking around like a real person

I did what I would do on my phone: Google, then ask husband to check two things. Short version:

- **"CartHedge reviews":** Nothing. No reviews, no YouTube video, nothing in the seller-group chatter I could find. Google only showed general lists of Instagram DM automation tools ([example](https://flowgent.ai/blog/instagram-dm-automation-tool)) and CartHedge was not in them. A brand-new name with zero proof. Not a deal-breaker for a 15-day free try, but I will not pay a rupee before I see it work on my own page.
- **Alternatives I already half-know from ads:** Interakt, WATI, AiSensy. Interakt's starter is about ₹999 a month, the next plan about ₹2,799, and Meta's message charges come on top ([WebMaxy comparison](https://www.webmaxy.co/blog/whatsapp-commerce/interakt-pricing/)). These are WhatsApp tools. None of them says it closes an Instagram DM into an order, so CartHedge is at least a different thing.
- **WhatsApp charges:** A marketing message from the official API is about ₹0.86 before GST, so about ₹1.02 with 18% GST, and utility messages (order updates) about ₹0.115 ([MyOperator](https://myoperator.com/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india-2026), [ChatMaxima](https://chatmaxima.com/whatsapp-api-pricing/india/)). For my 1,100-buyer list one festive broadcast is about ₹1,120. Right now my broadcast from the WhatsApp Business app costs ₹0. So I want to know who pays this.
- **COD confirmation:** Husband says Shiprocket already has a COD confirm option. Shiprocket Engage lists about ₹2.49 per order for the advanced features, but it is a Shopify app and we are not on Shopify ([Shopify app page](https://apps.shopify.com/shiprocket-engage/reviews)). So COD confirm alone is not a new idea. Fine.
- **Dukaan, the sore point:** Their Starter is still about ₹5,759 a year plus a 3.99% fee on transactions, with a 35% discount if you pay yearly ([TechJockey](https://www.techjockey.com/detail/dukaan), [SoftwareSuggest](https://www.softwaresuggest.com/dukaan/pricing)). I paid that kind of money, got a website nobody opened, and the buyers still came in DMs. This is exactly why I trust "DM is the shop" more than "here is your website."
- **Worried about:** Festive season is starting (Dussehra and Diwali). DMs will double, so a good tool would help most now. But I also cannot afford a bad setup week now. My helper is already stretched.

### Stage 3. After reading the product definition and the website words

**Live today versus coming (as I understood it):**
- Live: Instagram DM assistant, order board, share links, OTP checkout, UPI via my own Razorpay, COD confirm, returns, GST invoices, alerts to my WhatsApp.
- Coming: assistant on my own WhatsApp number, comment-to-DM, live selling, photo and story matching, WhatsApp broadcasts.

My honest reaction: the half that is live is not the half I need most. My orders are about half Instagram, half WhatsApp, and half the DMs I get are "ye wala" screenshots or story and reel replies. Those are on the "coming" list.

**Things that confused me while reading:**
1. The ad says every DM is answered automatically, but the price list says automatic replies are only on Pro (₹1,999). Growth only makes "ready drafts". Starter has no DM help at all. So the ad shows me the ₹1,999 product.
2. Three ways to order (chat, link, storefront). Which one do I use? I will never push a website, I tried that.
3. COD confirm works on link and store orders. For DM orders the "haan" in chat is the confirmation. But my buyers already say "haan" in DM today and still refuse at the door. What is actually new for my DM orders? The ₹50 to ₹100 token is the real filter, and I am scared it kills my COD buyers.
4. "Order quota": does a cancelled order count? An RTO order? A draft I reject? What exactly is "an order"?
5. I need my own Razorpay account. I take UPI directly today for prepaid. Razorpay means KYC and a fee on every prepaid order (the product text does not mention it).
6. WhatsApp updates today come from CartHedge's own number, not mine. My buyer sees an unknown number, not "Ritu's Kurti House", and may think it is spam.
7. Prices: with GST or without? I am not GST registered (our CA says not needed yet), so 18% extra is a pure cost to me.
8. If the testimonials on the website are shown live: one says "Ritika S., Jaipur juttis and bags, ₹31,000 saved in month one". I know most Jaipur sellers and I do not know her. "RTO from 27% to 9%" also does not match what I hear in sellers' groups. Fake-looking proof is worse than no proof.

### Stage 4. The interview

**1. In my words, what does it do? What confused me?**
It sits behind my Instagram DM. A buyer asks "pink wali hai, M me?", it answers from my catalog in Hinglish, takes name, number, address, shows the order summary, buyer says "haan", and I approve with one tap. After that the buyer gets a link to track the parcel, and a COD confirmation step tries to stop refusals. The same app has a board, returns, GST and reports. Confusing parts are listed above: ad shows Pro features, three ways to order, what counts as an "order", and what COD confirm really adds for a DM order.

**2. Top 3 things that excite me, ranked**
1. **Replies at night and during festive rush.** Helper leaves at 6. My buyers (housewives, working women) scroll at 9 to 11pm and message then. I answer from bed with one eye open. If someone answers in 4 seconds in my style, that is sales I lose today. I guess 15 to 20 buyers a month go cold on "seen". I cannot prove it, but I feel it.
2. **DM becomes an order, no retyping.** Today I copy from chat into Excel and notebook, and my husband retypes into Shiprocket. The same address typed three times, and one wrong pincode means RTO.
3. **COD confirmation before dispatch.** 29 parcels a month come back, about ₹4,400 a month gone. If it even brings 25% down to 15% I save around ₹1,500 to ₹3,000 a month. It is useful, but it is not the ₹8,000 to ₹15,000 the website says, not for my numbers.

**3. Top 3 doubts, fears or deal-breakers**
1. **AI saying something wrong in my name.** Wrong price, a size that is out, a "chikankari" claim I did not make. Many of my pieces are one or two in stock, so if my stock in CartHedge is not exactly right, it will sell what I do not have. I already keep stock in Excel, so I would be maintaining it twice. Also my buyers like talking to "didi". "I am the shop's assistant" is honest, but it will cool some buyers. Deal-breaker: one wrong promise that I find out after the buyer is angry.
2. **Real cost is not clear.** GST extra or not, per-order fee on RTO and cancelled orders, who pays Meta's WhatsApp message charges on broadcasts, autopay overage added silently to the next charge. And the thing the ad promised is the most expensive plan.
3. **Setup burden and "another Dukaan".** I have around 150 live designs with sizes and photos, 10 to 12 new ones every drop. I rarely use a laptop. If I cannot do everything on my phone, and if nothing works properly in 15 days during Diwali rush, I will have wasted the best weeks of the year. Also: unknown company, no reviews, nobody in my groups uses it.

**4. Features missing that I'd need**
- Everything usable on a **phone** (adding products, approving drafts, stock, board). **Must have before I pay.**
- **Photo, screenshot and story-reply matching** ("ye wala" with a picture). Nearly half my DMs. **Must have before I pay.**
- **Comment-to-DM** for "price?" under reels. **Must have before I pay** (this is how my reels turn into orders).
- **Assistant on my own WhatsApp number**, with my Business app still working, and my 1,100 list. **Must have before I pay** (or at least a firm date within about 3 months, and until then I pay less).
- **Shiprocket link**: orders and addresses flow into Shiprocket so my husband does not retype. **Must have before I pay.**
- **Hindi (WhatsApp or phone) support**, not English tickets. **Must have.**
- **Helper login** and a **husband login**, at least 3 logins on the plan. **Must have.**
- Stock that handles "only 1 left" and does not oversell. **Must have.**
- **Voice note** understanding on WhatsApp (buyers send them a lot). Nice later.
- Fit help from measurements ("bust 38, kaunsa size?") from my size chart. Nice later.
- A custom domain or my shop name on the order link instead of carthedge.in. Nice later.
- Hindi screens in the seller app. Nice later.
- A shared "serial refuser" list across sellers so I can see a bad COD buyer before dispatch. Nice later, and I would pay for it.

**5. Features I would never use**
Live selling (I have never done a Live). Storefront and website (buyers do not come there). Reseller pricing (not now). Custom made-to-order links. GST credit notes and invoices today (not registered, may use later if CA says). Admin and "insights" suggestions I would glance at, not depend on. Dropped-checkout reminders (low interest, my buyers do not use checkout, they DM).

**6. What I spend today, and how I like to pay**
- Helper: ₹6,000 a month. That is my only real cost on this work.
- WhatsApp Business app: ₹0. Instagram: ₹0. Excel and notebook: ₹0. Shiprocket platform: ₹0 (I pay per parcel).
- Dukaan: ₹6,000 two years ago, a sunk cost I still remember.
- Payment preference: **UPI, monthly**, ideally UPI AutoPay with a fixed amount that I can stop from my own app. No yearly upfront for a new tool. Card is with my husband. Overage should never be added silently, I want a message first.

**7. Price sensitivity for the plan I'd actually need (automatic DM replies, about 180 orders a month), all-in with GST, per month**
- (a) So cheap I'd doubt it: under ₹300.
- (b) Bargain: ₹499 to ₹799.
- (c) Getting expensive but I'd still pay: ₹1,200 to ₹1,500.
- (d) Too expensive: above about ₹1,800 before I have seen proof. (₹1,999 plus 18% GST is ₹2,359, which my husband would reject on the spot.)

My own check: the helper costs ₹6,000 for 7 hours. If the assistant really closes the night DMs and stops 15 to 20 buyers from going cold, at about ₹400 profit each that is ₹6,000 to ₹8,000 a month. So after proof I could go to ₹1,500 comfortably. But I have not seen it yet, so no.

**8. Reaction to the current price list**
- **Which fits me:** Honestly nothing fits cleanly. Starter ₹499 with 100 orders then ₹3 an order means about ₹739 for my 180, but it has no DM help. Growth ₹999 covers 500 orders, gives order drafts from DMs, but replies are still manual. Pro ₹1,999 is the one with automatic replies, and I would use only 9% of its 2,000 orders. I pay ₹1,000 more for one feature and for headroom I will never use. The jump from Growth to Pro is the problem.
- **Would I pay after the trial?** Growth ₹999: probably yes, if the drafts work and the COD flow saves money. Pro ₹1,999: only after two or three weeks of seeing night DMs actually close, and my husband agrees.
- **What makes me upgrade:** Seeing real orders that came in overnight by themselves. A proof number in rupees on screen, my own, not a website claim.
- **What makes me leave:** One wrong price or wrong promise to a buyer. Hidden charges. Support that does not reply in Hindi. COD refusals not dropping after 2 months. A promised WhatsApp feature that keeps slipping. Instagram trouble because of the tool.
- **Flat vs per-order vs percent of sales:** Flat fee, because I can write it in my notebook and plan. A small per-order fee above a quota is fine if it is clear and has a ceiling, and RTO and cancelled orders must not count. Percent of sales never: Razorpay already takes about 2%, courier takes its share, I will not hand over a slice of every good month.
- My suggestion: a middle plan at about ₹1,299 to ₹1,499 with auto-replies and 300 orders, or auto-reply as an add-on on Growth. Do not make me buy 2,000 orders.

**9. Free plan or free trial? Annual discount?**
- I prefer a **small free plan** (for example 30 orders a month with COD confirm) that never expires, plus a **30-day trial of the paid features**. The reason is simple: an order takes 3 to 5 days to deliver, and an RTO comes back after 10 or more days. In 15 days I cannot see whether RTO fell. I can see the DM replies in 3 days, but not the RTO result. If someone completes setup, give 15 more days.
- **Annual:** not at the start. After 3 months of paying monthly and seeing results, I would think about it, and only at **30% off or more**. (Dukaan gave 35% and I still lost.) Two months free (about 17%) does not tempt me. It must be refundable pro-rata if I stop, paid by one UPI payment.

**10. Add-ons I'd actually pay for (all-in monthly unless stated)**
- **Auto-replies on my own WhatsApp number:** I expect it inside Pro. If separate, ₹499 to ₹699 a month.
- **WhatsApp message packs for broadcasts:** at Meta's cost plus a small margin. About ₹1.25 per marketing message, so a pack of 1,000 for about ₹1,250, bought only when I do a festive drop. A markup like Interakt's (₹0.20 to ₹0.40 per message) is the most I would accept.
- **Done-for-you catalog setup and photo upload:** ₹1,500 to ₹2,500 one time for about 150 to 200 designs. I would pay this gladly if the app is hard on my phone. Big reason I would say yes.
- **Extra staff logins:** 3 included, then ₹149 per login a month.
- **COD/RTO protection (shared refuser list or risk score):** ₹3 to ₹5 per COD order checked, only if it flags real serial refusers. At 117 COD orders that is ₹350 to ₹600 a month. I would pay after proof.
- **Courier booking inside the app:** no extra fee for the feature, but Shiprocket's rates or better. It saves my husband's evening.
- **More AI replies:** I will not buy "reply packs". Meters make me nervous. Fair use, unlimited.
- **Remove CartHedge branding:** I would not pay. But my shop name on the order link, ₹200 a month, maybe.
- **Priority support on call in Hindi:** ₹299 a month, or Hindi WhatsApp support included in every plan (better).

**11. Who else influences the decision, and what they'd ask**
- **Husband (careful with money):** "Total saal ka kitna? GST alag? Band karna ho to kaise? Ye company kaun hai, kal band to nahi ho jaayegi? Instagram account ko khatra to nahi? Dukaan jaisa to nahi hoga?" He also looks at Shiprocket and asks why it cannot book couriers.
- **Helper (about to be called "replaced"):** Will I lose my job? She must be shown it is for night and rush, and that she approves drafts. If she feels replaced she will quietly not use it.
- **Jaipur sellers' WhatsApp group:** I will ask there first. If two people I know say they use it, I move fast. If nobody does, I wait.
- **CA:** only "can you give me the month report", not a decider for now.

**12. One sentence to a friend right now**
"Ek naya app hai jo Instagram DM me raat ko bhi order le leta hai aur mujhse bas ek tap ka approval maangta hai, free try hai to dekh rahi hoon, par jab tak mere paas dikha nahi deta aur WhatsApp wala hissa nahi aata, main paise nahi dungi."

## Round 2 notes

- I now rank night replies above COD confirm. Devanshi's Shiprocket test (RTO 35% to 32%) tells me a free "haan" does little, so the RTO saving for my 29 parcels is probably nearer ₹1,000 to ₹2,000 than the website's claim. The ₹50 to ₹100 token may cost me more in lost COD buyers (each lost order is about ₹400 profit) than it saves, so I want it optional and testable on a few orders first.
- New fear from Pooja: a scammer copying my shop name behind a CartHedge link. "Seller verified" must have a clear meaning, because my 24k-follower name is my shop.
- Price view now: flat fee sized to my volume (my ask is about ₹1,299 for 300 orders with auto-replies, all-in with GST because I am unregistered). Current range: bargain ₹499 to ₹799, will pay ₹1,200 to ₹1,500 after proof, above ₹1,800 no. Flat ₹1,999 suits Hiren (about ₹1.7 an order) but is about ₹11 an order for me. WhatsApp message costs only through prepaid packs, never billed after. Small free plan plus 30 days. No yearly upfront.
- Agree with Devanshi, Shabnam and Vikram on ad-versus-plan mismatch and silent autopay overage. Disagree with Shabnam's ₹199 to ₹299 range (too cheap, I would doubt the company survives) and with Hiren's view that flat suits everyone. Still unconvinced by any claim until I see my own numbers.

## Round 3 notes

- Designed "Night Shift" plan: ₹1,099 GST all-in for 300 orders, auto-replies + COD confirm + returns, token optional. My concrete ask is ₹3.66 per order above Starter, not ₹11. Husband budgets on Razorpay mandate cap in writing, not on trust. This plan is smaller than Pro (fewer features, right volume) and bigger than Growth (auto-replies, token choice).
- Disagree with Vikram's free plan fear: a limited free plan (30 orders COD only) is not like Dukaan because buyers won't rely on a limited feature set. 30-day trial + free plan works because trial proves night replies close DMs, then buyer decides to pay for better limits.
- Token is conditional on data: if bot can predict which buyers refuse most (new or late delivery), I'll test token on those only, not all. Without that pattern, token is a ₹1,500 to ₹3,000 per month loss, not a gain. After week 3 of trial, CartHedge must show me my own refusal trend so I decide, not guess.

## Round 4 vote

- Growth at ₹1,533 is steep (₹434 above my ask) and WhatsApp on own number is now an add-on, not included. But safeguards (hard caps, clear order definition, pause not block) matter more to me than I expected. Three logins fit my team. I vote yes after trial proof, with WhatsApp on own number as the deal-maker within 3 months.
- Fairness 4: auto-replies finally in Growth, not Pro. But the jump from ₹999 to ₹1,299 is big for my 180 orders a month (₹7.17 per order instead of ₹5.55). Would pay ₹1,533 if night replies close DMs and RTO drops after 3 weeks. If not, I walk.
