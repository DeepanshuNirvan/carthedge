# Panelist 06: Mousumi Das, "Baluchari Ghar", Kolkata

(The persona file did not exist when Round 1 started, so this profile is written from the task description plus plausible detail. Everything below the profile is Mousumi speaking.)

## Profile

- **Person:** Mousumi Das, 47, lives in Behala, Kolkata. Runs Baluchari Ghar from the front room of her flat.
- **Sells:** handloom sarees (Baluchari, Jamdani, cotton, some Kantha) and terracotta decor. Most sarees are one-of-a-kind pieces, so "stock" is often 1. Decor items are repeatable.
- **Numbers:** about 40 orders a month, average order ₹2,400, so roughly ₹96,000 a month in sales. Puja and Poila Boishakh months run 80 to 100 orders. Her own guess at margin after weaver payment and packing: about 30 percent, so about ₹30,000 a month in her hand.
- **Shipping:** India-wide. India Post (Speed Post) for most parcels, one private courier for heavy or fragile terracotta. Mostly prepaid by UPI to her own QR. A few COD orders.
- **Channels:** Instagram page (her daughter Tuli set it up and posts on weekends). The real selling happens on WhatsApp, where regular customers message her photos and "didi ei ta ki dam?". Roughly 6 in 10 orders start on WhatsApp, 4 in 10 on Instagram. She answers slowly, often next morning.
- **Languages:** Bengali first, then Hindi, then English. Reads English but dislikes it for anything with money or rules.
- **Tech:** Smartphone, WhatsApp, PhonePe. Not comfortable with dashboards, CSVs or logins. Tuli handles anything with a password.
- **Fears:** complicated apps, monthly auto-debits she forgets, an AI quoting a wrong price or being rude to regulars (one regular, Sharmistha di, has bought 11 sarees from her).
- **Tools spend today:** about ₹0. WhatsApp Business app (free), Instagram (free), Canva (free). Boosts a post for ₹500 to ₹1,500 some months. A part-time packing helper at ₹4,500 a month (not for chat).
- **Support preference:** a phone call in Bengali or Hindi. Very loyal once she trusts something.
- **Date of this round:** 4 October 2026. Durga Puja is two weeks away, so she is at her busiest and least willing to learn anything new.

## Round 1

### Stage 1. Cold first impression (the Instagram ad)

I was scrolling after dinner, dishes done, and this came between two saree reels. "Every DM answered. Every order closed in the chat." Ki bolchhe eta?

**What I think it is:** Some computer or robot that answers my customers' messages on Instagram, and then takes the order too. "In their language" I read as: if the customer writes Bangla, it writes Bangla. I am not sure it really can. "From your real catalog" confused me, because I do not have a catalog, I have photos on the page and photos in WhatsApp. "You approve with a tap" is the one line that made me relax a little: so it does not finish things behind my back.

**Would I tap:** Not tonight, and not alone. I would screenshot it and send it to Tuli with "eta ki? scam na to?". If Tuli says it looks fine, then maybe she taps on Sunday.

**What I would suspect:**
- Sponsored posts that promise sales are very often cheats. "No card" is nice, but they will ask for my phone number and maybe my Instagram password.
- A robot talking to Sharmistha di and the other regulars, calling them "Dear customer". Or saying a wrong price. Each of my sarees has a different price, and some I give 5 to 10 percent off to regulars.
- After 15 days the money starts. I do not trust "free" for things I forget about.
- Who is this company? Is there a real person, a phone number, an office?

**What I need to see next:**
- A short video or screenshots in Bangla, with a saree, not a kurti.
- A saree or decor seller like me who already uses it, from Kolkata if possible.
- The price in rupees, right there, and whether it is with GST.
- How to stop it with one button.
- A phone number I can call.

### Stage 3. What I learned from the product files (read with Tuli over the phone, plain version)

- It is an assistant that replies on Instagram (WhatsApp on my own number is "coming next"), reads from products I add, collects item, address and payment, shows the buyer an order summary with the price worked out by the system and not the AI, and then either places the order or gives me a draft to approve with one tap. Draft approval is the default. Good.
- Also a shop link with my name, a board of orders, tracking page for the buyer, returns, GST invoices, reports, broadcasts. Too many things for me. I need about one fifth of it.

Growth at ₹999 only turns chats into drafts (I would still be the one replying). Starter at ₹499 has no AI at all. So the thing in the advertisement costs ₹1,999.

- Good things I noticed: a practice chat where I can pretend to be a customer and nothing is sent; every automation is a switch; if I reply myself the assistant steps back on that chat; bargaining and complaints can be sent to me; it says it is the shop's assistant and not a human; no password sharing for Instagram; after the trial the store pauses and data is safe.
- The RTO and COD story is for fashion sellers with lots of COD. For me, about 10 COD orders a month and maybe 2 refused (₹400 to ₹600 lost), it is not my reason.
- No reviews, and the testimonials on the site are marked as sample.

### Stage 4. The interview

**1. In your words, what does it do? What confused you?**
It is a robot shop assistant that answers customers on Instagram in their language, using the products I give it, takes the order and shows me a ready order to approve. It also gives a shop link and a board to track orders and a page where the buyer sees tracking.
Confused me: Instagram or WhatsApp (most of my regulars write on WhatsApp)? "Catalog" when most of my sarees exist only once. Words like kanban, SKU, HSN, RTO, "drafts". Razorpay. And why the ad says every DM but the Pro plan is needed.

**2. Top 3 things that excite you, ranked**
1. Replies at night and when I am packing or cooking, in the customer's language, in my tone. I think I lose 3 or 4 buyers a month because I reply next morning. At ₹2,400 that is ₹7,000 to ₹10,000 of sales, around ₹3,000 of my profit. Puja season is worse.
2. Control: approve with a tap, practice chat first, every automation a switch, it steps back when I reply myself. This is the only reason I would even try, because of my fear of a wrong price.
3. Order details collected in one place with the address checked, and the buyer tracking page. Now I scroll WhatsApp and write in a notebook, and every Speed Post parcel brings 5 messages of "didi, parcel kothay?".

**3. Top 3 doubts, fears or deal-breakers**
1. A wrong price, a saree sold twice (it exists once), or a rude or cold reply to a regular like Sharmistha di. One such mistake and I switch it off forever. Deal-breaker: no way to tell it "these customers, always me".
2. Setup and support. If I get stuck and nobody speaks Bangla or Hindi on the phone, I stop. Deal-breaker.
3. Money: auto-debit I forget, surprise per-order charges in Puja month, ₹1,999 for 40 orders, and a company with no reviews that wants my Instagram and a Razorpay KYC. Deal-breaker: any charge without a reminder first.

**4. Features missing that you would need**
- Auto-replies on my own WhatsApp number (it is coming, not here): **must have before I pay**. That is where most of my customers are.
- A "my regulars" list that the assistant never answers by itself, only alerts me: **must have before I pay**.
- One-of-a-kind pieces: stock 1, marked sold the moment it is ordered, and held for a day while the buyer pays: **must have before I pay**.
- Someone uploads my first 100 or so products from my WhatsApp and Instagram photos, with me on a phone call in Bangla. I will not do a CSV: **must have before I pay**.
- Phone support in Bangla or Hindi, with a real call back the same day: **must have before I pay**.
- A payment reminder on WhatsApp 3 days before the monthly charge, pay by UPI link, and if I miss it the account pauses and nothing is deleted: **must have before I pay**.
- A hard monthly cap I set on order fees, so Puja month has no surprises: **must have before I pay**.
- Enter an India Post Speed Post tracking number by hand and have the buyer get it on WhatsApp: **must have before I pay** (I do not know if this works today).
- Photo and story matching ("ei ta" with a screenshot of my post). Most enquiries come with a photo. Until it exists the assistant must hand over to me at once instead of guessing: photo matching **nice later**, safe handover **must have**.
- Voice notes (many customers send Bangla voice messages): handover only, **nice later** for anything more.
- Seller app buttons in Bangla or Hindi: **nice later** if phone support is good.
- Order and tracking link carrying my shop name, not CartHedge's: **nice later**.
- Auto-booking of India Post pickups: **nice later**, but I would love it.

**5. Features you would never use**
Kanban drag and drop, CSV bulk import, GST credit notes and reports (my CA uncle says I may not need GST yet), reseller prices, coupon codes with five limits, dropped-checkout reminders (they feel like chasing people), the RTO calculator, QR codes for links, admin-style reports. Broadcasts: not now. I do not want to spam my regulars, maybe one message at Poila Boishakh later.

**6. What you spend today, and how you like to pay**
About ₹0 a month on tools. WhatsApp Business, Instagram, Canva and PhonePe are free. A post boost of ₹500 to ₹1,500 in some months. A packing helper at ₹4,500 a month (not for chat). Courier and Speed Post are per parcel, about ₹60 to ₹120, passed on to the buyer in the price. I pay by UPI, month by month. No autopay, no card. A yearly payment only after I trust it.

**7. Price sensitivity, ₹ a month (for a plan with automatic replies for about 50 orders; I mean the full amount leaving my account, GST included)**
- (a) So cheap I would doubt it: under ₹199.
- (b) A bargain: ₹499.
- (c) Getting expensive but I would still pay: ₹799 to ₹999, only while I can see it bringing orders.
- (d) Too expensive: ₹1,499 and above. ₹1,999 for 40 orders is too expensive for me.

**8. React to the current price list**
- Starter ₹499: the volume fits me (100 orders, I do 40) but there is no AI, so it is a fancy notebook. I would not pay ₹499 for that.
- Growth ₹999: drafts from chats, but I would still be the slow one replying. No.
- Pro ₹1,999: the only plan that does what the ad says, but it is ₹50 an order for me, about 2 percent of sales and 6 percent of my profit. After the trial I would not pay this unless it clearly brings 6 or more extra orders every month.
- What would make me pay: a plan sized for me, around ₹699 to ₹999, with automatic replies, 50 to 60 orders and phone support. What makes me upgrade: Puja and Boishakh months over 100 orders, WhatsApp on my own number live, and three real orders it closed at night. What makes me leave: one wrong price, one rude reply to a regular, a surprise charge, or nobody answering my call.
- Flat fee, per-order or percent: flat fee, because I know what leaves my account. Per-order above the quota is okay only with a cap and a message before. Percent of sales, no, it feels like a cut from the weavers' money, and "CartHedge takes no cut of buyer money" is a good line, keep it.

**9. Free plan or free trial? How long? Annual?**
If I must choose, a small free plan (say 30 automatic replies a month) because I trust slowly, and I could move up when I see it working. If it is a trial, 30 days, and the clock should start when I press go, or after my first live chat. 15 days over Puja is nothing. Annual: tempting only after 3 months of use, and only at about 2 months free (roughly 17 percent) with the remaining money coming back if I leave. 25 percent off would make me think harder.

**10. Add-ons I would actually pay for**
- Auto-replies on my own WhatsApp number: ₹300 to ₹500 a month on top of the plan, if it is not already in it.
- Phone support in Bangla or Hindi, priority: ₹199 to ₹299 a month. I would pay it happily; better if it is in the plan.
- Done-for-you setup and upload of my first 100 products, with a phone call: ₹1,500 one time. ₹2,000 is my limit.
- Extra logins: Tuli's login should be free. A third one, no.
- WhatsApp message packs: ₹200 for a small pack, but I would use it rarely.
- COD/RTO protection: about ₹10 per COD order, refunded if the parcel comes back refused. Nice later.
- Courier or India Post booking: only if it is cheaper than what I pay now. Nice later.
- Removing CartHedge branding: ₹0, I would not pay. More AI replies: no, it should feel unlimited.

**11. Who else influences the decision, and what would they ask**
- Tuli, my daughter: "Who are these people? Why no reviews? Can we turn it off in one tap? Does it take my Instagram password?" She does the setup, so she decides if I can even try.
- My husband Subir: "Kito taka mashe? Auto-kete nebe? Cancel korle ki hobe?" (How much a month? Will it deduct on its own? What happens if you cancel?)
- Sharmistha di and my other regulars: not asked, but one complaint from them and I stop.
- My CA uncle: only the invoices and GST question.
- Mitali and the saree sellers' WhatsApp group: "Is anyone using it? Has it made any mistakes?"

**12. One sentence to a friend right now**
"It is a robot that answers customers when I am asleep, and I will try it after Puja only if Tuli says it is safe, they let me switch it off, and someone picks up the phone and talks to me in Bangla."

## Round 2 notes

- Pricing view hardened: flat fee only, no percent of sales (agree with almost everyone). Per-order above quota only with my own monthly cap and a WhatsApp warning first. Add a 12-month price lock in writing (Vikram's chatbot went from ₹1,500 to ₹3,800). Meta message charges shown as a separate line, no CartHedge markup. Annual only after 3 months, about 2 months free.
- Current price range for AI replies, GST included: ₹499 bargain, ₹799 to ₹999 fine while I see orders, ₹1,499 and above no. Plans should be cut by volume, not by features: big drop sellers (Tanya, Kabir) carry the big plan, small sellers get AI at ₹799 to ₹999.
- Agree with: Shabnam (same craft seller, same Pro-only "bait and switch" feeling, but I would pay ₹499, she says ₹199 to ₹299), Vikram (autopay and quota surprises), Pooja (must say it is an assistant), Lakshmi (WhatsApp on own number is the real need, Puja timing). Disagree with: Shabnam on per-order fee being acceptable, Tanya on ₹1,999 being "too cheap to trust".
- Unchanged: I try it only after Puja, only if Tuli says it is safe, I can switch it off in one tap, and someone answers the phone in Bangla or Hindi.

## Round 3 notes

- Proposed volume-based tiers as the fix: ₹799/60 orders for me, ₹2,500/2,000 for Tanya, ₹1,499/1,200 for Hiren. Same AI, different prices because our business rhythms differ. Feature-based tiers are unfair to small sellers.
- Concrete plan: ₹799/month all-in with GST, 60 auto-replies, draft approval, regulars list (no bot replies to Sharmistha di), Bangla phone support within 4 hours, no per-order fees above quota. Makes me pay: 3 real night orders. Makes me cancel: one screenshot of a wrong price, one rude reply to a regular, one forgotten auto-debit.
- Phone support language is now a deal-breaker, moved above pricing. If CartHedge has no Bangla line I am a free trial, not a customer. Same applies to Ritu (Hindi), Lakshmi (Tamil), Rohit (Gujarati). English-only support excludes half the seller panel.

## Round 4 vote

- Vote Growth at ₹1,299/month: AI finally moves out of Pro-only, safeguards are strong (hard cap, alerts, price lock), 30-day trial timing is real help. Pay after trial: maybe, contingent on deal-breaker.
- Deal-breaker confirmed: Bangla phone support included on Growth, same-day callback. Without it, I stay free trial forever. ₹1,299 is 63 percent above my ₹799 ideal but inside the ₹800-1,500 range I said I would still pay.
