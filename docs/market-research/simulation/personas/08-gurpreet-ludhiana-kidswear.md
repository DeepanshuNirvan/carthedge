# Panelist 08: Gurpreet Kaur, Little Pind, Ludhiana (kidswear)

## Profile

- Gurpreet Kaur, 40. Co-owner with her brother Harpreet of **Little Pind**, a kidswear manufacturer in Ludhiana, Punjab.
- About **900 orders a month**: 600 wholesale (boutiques and shops across Punjab, Haryana, Himachal order cartons over WhatsApp, a typical order is roughly Rs 6,000 to Rs 15,000) and 300 retail (Instagram and WhatsApp).
- GST registered. Needs proper tax invoices, B2B buyers with GSTIN, credit notes, and a monthly export for the CA, who works in Tally.
- Four staff handle orders, billing and dispatch. Simran (billing, six years with them) is the one everyone depends on.
- Tools today: Vyapar (about Rs 4,000 a year), WhatsApp Business app on the old business number, Instagram, Delhivery.
- Speaks Punjabi and Hindi, English for paperwork and the CA.
- Practical. Wants staff logins with limited rights. Worries about staff misuse and customer data walking out with a staff member.
- Date in the simulation: 4 Oct 2026. Winter-wear orders are peaking and Diwali is five weeks away, so she is not in the mood to experiment with her order flow this month.

## Round 1

### Stage 1. Cold first impression

Night time, dispatch is packed, I am scrolling Instagram in bed and this ad comes.

**What I think it is:** A chatbot for Instagram DMs. Customer asks price, bot answers, bot takes the order. "You approve with a tap" means the bot does not do everything alone, which is the one sensible line in the ad. I have seen ten of these ads this year, they all say AI.

**Would I tap:** Yes, once, only to look at the page. Simran keeps saying Instagram DMs are left unanswered when she is busy with the cartons, so there is a real itch. But I would not type a single detail or sign up from my phone on the ad. I would send the link to Harpreet and Simran and say "dekho".

**What I suspect:**
- "Every DM answered, every order closed" is a big claim. My Instagram is only one corner. Most of my money comes on WhatsApp from boutique owners who send a voice note or a photo of a design and say "5 piece bhej do". Nothing in this ad says WhatsApp.
- Which catalog? "Your real catalog" for me is 400 designs in a phone gallery, a Drive folder and a PDF. Who types that in?
- What if it tells a boutique the wrong price? A wrong rate on a carton is not a small mistake.
- "No card" is fine, but then where do they earn? And who is behind it, a proper company with an address and a phone number, or a college project?
- Meta could ban my page if I connect some automation. My number and page are years old.
- My customer list is my business. Where does it sit and who in their company can see it?

**What I'd need to see next:** Price on the page (no "contact us"). Whether it works on WhatsApp, not just Instagram. Whether it gives a GST invoice with GSTIN and credit notes. Whether my staff can have separate logins. A video or demo with Hindi or Punjabi, not only kurti and Delhi examples. One real shop using it, ideally a wholesale one. A phone number I can call.

### Stage 2. Looking around

I did what I normally do before touching a new app: check what it costs against what I already pay, ask what the "free" part is, and look for complaints. I stayed short.

- **Reviews:** I searched "CartHedge reviews Instagram DM AI sales assistant India". Nothing about CartHedge at all, only general lists of other Instagram AI tools ([search result example: Inro](https://www.inro.social/), [SleekFlow blog](https://sleekflow.io/en-us/blog/instagram-ai-agent)). New product, no proof either way. Not scary, not comforting. For something that will hold my GST invoices, no reviews means I ask for references.
- **What I already pay (Vyapar):** I checked my renewal. Desktop plans about Rs 3,800 to Rs 4,100 a year, combo with mobile Rs 4,000 to Rs 5,000 ([Techjockey](https://www.techjockey.com/detail/vyapar), [ITforSME](https://www.itforsme.in/pricing/vyapar-india)). That is about Rs 335 a month. This is my yardstick: anything that wants six times that must replace something.
- **Why Vyapar stays:** it exports sales and credit notes to Tally as master.xml and voucher.xml, so my CA never complains ([Vyapar guide](https://vyaparapp.in/guides/how-to-import-and-export-vyapar-app-data-into-tally)). Any new tool has to do this or I am doing double entry.
- **Alternatives I know about:** my cousin who sells hosiery uses Wati. Interakt starter is about Rs 999 a month and is more Instagram focused, growth is about Rs 2,799 on monthly billing. Wati starts about Rs 1,800 a month and charges per agent seat. Zoko is about Rs 4,500 a month ([Zoko's Interakt guide](https://www.zoko.io/post/interakt-pricing-guide), [Richautomate](https://richautomate.in/blog/wati-vs-interakt-india-2026-pricing-decoded)). Note these comparisons are written by competitors, so I treat the numbers as rough.
- **WhatsApp charges from Meta:** marketing message about Rs 0.86 each, about Rs 1.02 with GST; utility and OTP about Rs 0.115 each; and from 1 Oct 2026 one blog says service replies beyond the first 1,000 a month per number are charged at the utility rate ([MyOperator](https://myoperator.com/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india-2026), [EngageLab](https://www.engagelab.com/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing)). Utility is cheap even at my volume. Marketing blasts to 2,000 boutiques would be about Rs 2,000 a month. I could not confirm the 1 Oct change on Meta's own page, so I am not relying on it.
- **Worried about:** moving my WhatsApp to an API number and losing the Business app on my phone; staff access; Tally.

### Stage 3. What I learned from the product files

**What is live and what is not, as I read it.** Live: Instagram DM assistant, order board, store and share links, OTP checkout, UPI and card through my own Razorpay, COD confirmation, returns and exchanges, GST invoices with CGST/SGST/IGST, credit notes, buyer GSTIN at checkout, bulk actions up to 100 orders, packing slips and pick list, customer ledger, alerts. Not yet: auto-replies on my own WhatsApp number, comment-to-DM, live selling, photo and story matching, Tally-style accounting exports (product.txt lists it under roadmap).

**What I noticed, good:** GST invoices with per-product HSN and rate, credit notes made automatically for refunds, buyer GSTIN and place of supply, and invoice numbers that restart each financial year are exactly what the CA asks for. The pick list for 100 orders and packing slips with a QR are useful for my dispatch table. The assistant hands bulk deals and bargaining to me, and "replying yourself pauses the assistant" is the right way. Money goes to my own account.

**What I noticed, bad for me:**
- Every example is a single kurti, a jewellery piece, a COD parcel. There is no wholesale language anywhere: no carton or set packs, no minimum order, no party rate lists, no credit or udhaar, no outstanding balance, no transport LR number, no e-way bill.
- The only "reseller" tool is one second price. I have at least three rates: retail, shop, distributor.
- The actor table says the seller logs in with email and password to their own workspace. No staff accounts, no roles, no activity log. The "download all store data as one file" button sits with that one login, and my entire buyer list and rates would sit inside it.
- "View as seller": CartHedge staff can open my store for 30 minutes read only, recorded. I would want to know who, and to see that log myself.
- Razorpay settles retail well. A boutique paying Rs 25,000 for a carton will pay by NEFT or UPI to my current account, not through a gateway that takes about 2 percent.
- GST invoices and reports are in Growth, AI auto-reply is in Pro. Fine, but the plan names do not say it plainly and I had to read twice.
- The RTO and COD story is the whole sales pitch. That is for my 300 retail orders only, and even there it is small money next to wholesale.

### Stage 4. The interview

**1. In your words, what does it do? What confused you?**
It sits behind Instagram (and soon WhatsApp), answers buyers from my catalog in their language, turns the chat into an order with an exact summary, and I approve it with one tap. Then the order goes on a board, I print slips, make GST invoice and credit note, and the buyer tracks it on a link. Payment goes to my own Razorpay. So it is an assistant plus an order desk plus an invoice app, built for retail sellers.
What confused me: (a) who it is for, because wholesale is not mentioned once; (b) what is live today, since the headline says Instagram and WhatsApp but WhatsApp on my own number is "coming next"; (c) what counts as one "order" for the quota, a carton order or a cancelled one; (d) are the prices with or without GST; (e) "Tally-style reporting" is on the roadmap but the site says "export for your accountant", so which is it today; (f) who in CartHedge can see my store.

**2. Top 3 things that excite you, ranked, and why for your business**
1. **GST invoices, credit notes and B2B GSTIN in the same place as the order.** Today Simran types the order into Vyapar after it is on WhatsApp. If the order and the invoice are one thing, that is the saving, and credit notes made automatically on refunds will stop arguments with the CA.
2. **Bulk actions, packing slips and pick list.** Select 100 orders, print the pick list, the dispatch table works from one sheet. With kids sizes and sets this saves real running around.
3. **The Instagram assistant that hands over what it should (bargaining, bulk deals) and keeps quiet when we reply.** I do not want a bot arguing with a boutique owner about a rate. For my 300 retail buyers asking "age 3 saal, size kya hoga, COD?" it would save Simran an hour or two daily.

**3. Top 3 doubts, fears or deal-breakers**
1. **No staff logins and roles, and one button that exports everything.** Four staff. I will not give four people my password. If a girl leaves with my buyer list and wholesale rate list, my competitor in Ludhiana has my business by evening. I need rights per staff (orders only, billing only, packing only), phone numbers hidden for some roles, no export and no delete for staff, and a log of who did what. Also what your own CartHedge staff can open. **Deal-breaker.**
2. **WhatsApp is my business and it is "coming".** 600 of 900 orders live there, on a number that every boutique in three states already has saved. I will not change number, will not lose the Business app on my phone, and I will not pay for a promise. Also a ban risk on a 12 year old number.
3. **Vyapar and Tally overlap, and what if you shut down.** Two invoice series is a problem for my CA. Double entry is a problem for Simran. And GST invoices are legal records: a young company with no reviews has to show me bulk invoice download, my data export, and what happens to my numbering if they close. Smaller doubts: AI quoting a wrong carton price, Punjabi support, nobody to call.

**4. Features missing that you would need**
Must have before I pay:
- **Staff logins with roles, limited rights, hidden phone numbers, owner-only export and delete, activity log.** (the gate for everything)
- **Tally-ready monthly export:** sales, credit notes, GST split, party GSTIN, HSN summary, in Tally XML or at least the exact Excel my CA can import. Vyapar does it today.
- **Wholesale pricing:** three or more price tiers, tier per buyer, wholesale rates not visible to the public, sold in sets or cartons with a minimum order.
- **Auto-replies on my own WhatsApp number, with the Business app still on my phone.**
- **Credit and outstanding:** party-wise balance, credit limit, partial or advance payments, reminders. Without it wholesale stays in Vyapar and I run two systems.
- **Transport and e-way bill:** LR number, transporter, e-way bill for large inter-state loads.
- **Delhivery handoff:** book, label, AWB, status. Tell me which couriers actually work.
- **Punjabi:** replies in Gurmukhi and in Punjabi typed in English letters. I could not see Punjabi in your language strip (Tamil, Bengali, Telugu, Kannada are there).
- **Complete data export on request, including all invoices as PDFs, and a clear promise on retention.**

Nice later:
- Repeat order for a boutique ("last time wala order dobara"), with the assistant offering it.
- Catalog PDF or link to share on WhatsApp with different prices per buyer group.
- Age-size handling (0 to 3 months, 2 to 3 years), assorted colour packs.
- Agent or salesman login for field agents who bring orders.
- Barcode scanning at packing.
- Weekly backup to me and the CA by email.

**5. Features you would never use**
Live selling, comment-to-DM for my wholesale side, the "fun" tone with emojis (my boutique owners are older and serious), the COD token and the RTO savings meter for wholesale, dropped-checkout reminders for wholesale buyers, and the storefront as a browsing website (my retail buyers come from chat, my wholesale buyers will not do OTP checkout at all, they will send a voice note).

**6. What you spend today, and how you like to pay**
- Vyapar about Rs 335 a month (Rs 4,000 a year, paid once).
- WhatsApp Business app and Instagram: free.
- Delhivery: per parcel, not a tool cost.
- Meta ads: Rs 5,000 to Rs 10,000 a month in busy months, nothing in slow ones.
- CA and GST filing: about Rs 3,000 a month.
- Four staff: about Rs 55,000 to Rs 60,000 a month together. Roughly half of one person's day is typing the same reply and one more person's day is retyping orders and invoices.
- Paid apps in total: under Rs 1,000 a month. No agency.
- **How I pay:** yearly upfront by bank transfer or UPI for anything I trust (that is how Vyapar works). Monthly UPI autopay for Rs 1,000 to Rs 2,000 is fine if the GST invoice comes in my firm's name with GSTIN so I can take input credit. I hate card-on-file subscriptions that I forget about.

**7. Price sensitivity for the plan I'd need (Rs per month)**
At 900 orders, with staff logins, wholesale features and WhatsApp on my number all working:
- (a) So cheap I would doubt it: under about Rs 600. GST invoices for 900 orders at Vyapar's price makes me think it will disappear.
- (b) A bargain: Rs 1,200 to Rs 1,800.
- (c) Getting expensive but I would still pay: Rs 2,500 to Rs 3,000, if it replaces Vyapar and half a person's work.
- (d) Too expensive: above Rs 4,000 a month. That is a full year of Vyapar every month. Between Rs 3,000 and Rs 4,000 I will bargain.
As it exists today (Instagram only, one login, no wholesale), I would pay Rs 0 and just use the trial to test the retail chat.

**8. React to the current price list**
- Maths at my volume: Starter is Rs 499 plus 800 extra orders at Rs 3, so Rs 2,899. Growth is Rs 999 plus 400 extra at Rs 2.50, so Rs 1,999. Pro is Rs 1,999 flat with room up to 2,000. So Growth and Pro cost the same for me and Pro is the obvious one. The ladder is odd: Starter is a trap for anyone above 100 orders.
- If I run only my 300 retail orders on it, Growth at Rs 999 fits, with a 500 quota to spare in season. That is how I would start, with wholesale staying in Vyapar until the wholesale features exist.
- **Would I pay after the trial?** Not today. I would pay the day staff logins and Tally export exist and I have seen the WhatsApp number automation work for a month.
- **What makes me upgrade:** wholesale on the system, more staff, the assistant on my own WhatsApp number.
- **What makes me leave:** one wrong price quoted to a boutique, any data leak, a Meta warning on my number or page, two invoice series that my CA complains about for more than two months, no one answering the phone, a surprise price rise.
- **Flat vs per-order vs percent:** flat monthly fee, because I can plan it and claim GST on it. Rs 2 to Rs 2.50 per extra order is fair for me because a wholesale order is Rs 6,000 or more, but tell me exactly what counts (carton order, cancelled order, exchange, manual order, test order). A percentage of sales I would never accept: even 0.5 percent of my wholesale turnover would be over Rs 20,000 a month, more than my CA. "No cut of your sales" is your best line, keep it.
- Not stated: whether Rs 499, 999 and 1,999 include 18 percent GST. I can claim it back, a small retailer cannot, so say it clearly.

**9. Free plan or free trial? How long? Annual discount?**
Trial, but 15 days is too short for wholesale. A boutique orders every three to four weeks and my CA's export is only seen at month end, so I need at least 30 days to see one full GST month. I would also like a small free plan (say 30 to 50 orders, one login), the way the Vyapar mobile app is free, so staff can practise without a clock. Annual: I would only take it after three months of real use, and only at about 20 to 25 percent off (two to three months free) with a refund if the staff roles or WhatsApp number automation are not delivered as promised. Ten percent off does not move me.

**10. Add-ons I would actually pay for, with a price**
- **Staff logins:** I want five included in the plan. Extra beyond that Rs 100 to Rs 150 per login per month. I will not pay Wati-style per-seat charges for every girl in the room.
- **Done-for-you setup and catalog upload** (400 designs, sizes, colours, three price tiers, in Hindi or Punjabi on a call): Rs 5,000 to Rs 8,000 one time. I would pay this happily, I do not have the time.
- **Auto-replies on my own WhatsApp number:** Rs 500 to Rs 700 extra per month, but only after I see it work for a month. Better if it is simply in Pro.
- **WhatsApp message pack for new-collection broadcasts to boutiques:** at cost plus a little, Rs 1.00 to Rs 1.25 per message all in. At Rs 2 a message I would use my own broadcast list.
- **Priority support on phone in Hindi or Punjabi:** Rs 500 a month.
- **Weekly backup of everything to me and the CA:** Rs 200 a month.
- **No CartHedge branding on invoices and customer pages:** my GST invoices must never say "powered by". I would pay Rs 300 a month, but for tax documents it should just be free.
- **Courier booking:** I have my own Delhivery rates. I pay nothing extra for this.
- **COD and RTO protection:** retail only, small money, I would not pay.
- **Tally export:** should be in the plan, not an add-on. If it is an add-on, Rs 300 a month and I will be annoyed.

**11. Who else influences the decision, and what they would ask**
- **Harpreet (brother and co-owner):** "WhatsApp chal raha hai, why change? What does it cost per month against Vyapar, and where does our data sit?"
- **The CA (Bhalla ji):** "Does it import into Tally? Is the invoice series continuous with Vyapar? Do credit notes carry the original invoice number? Is there a GSTR-1 style sheet? Is the subscription invoiced in our GSTIN so we take ITC?"
- **Simran and the other three staff:** "Will we type in two places? Will this replace us? Why can I not see the price list?" Simran will be the real user, so if she is unhappy it is dead.
- **Boutique customers (indirectly):** they will not do OTP checkout or click a link, they will send a voice note. If the new flow adds a step for them, they will go to someone else.

**12. One sentence to a friend**
"Looks good for girls selling kurtis on Instagram, but for us wholesale people it is half a tool until it works on our own WhatsApp number with staff logins and Tally export, so I will wait and watch, and meanwhile I keep my Vyapar."

## Round 2 notes

- I now believe I am not alone on staff seats: Arjun and Hiren also want team logins, and Ritu, Devanshi and others want 30 days, not 15. Staff roles stay my gate, and I added two seat types to ask for: "agency, no export" and "CA, read only".
- Charging: flat monthly fee plus Rs 2 to Rs 2.50 per extra order is fine if the page defines what counts (cancelled, exchange, manual, test). The WhatsApp bill must be a separate line at cost with a monthly cap I set. Hiren's template shock and my cousin's Wati seat creep (about Rs 1,800 to Rs 3,500) back this up. Percent of sales is still never.
- Price range unchanged: Rs 0 today, Rs 1,200 to 1,800 a bargain, Rs 2,500 to 3,000 fine if it replaces Vyapar and half a person's work, above Rs 4,000 no. Annual only after three months, 20 to 25 percent off, with a refund if roles or WhatsApp number automation do not arrive. Setup Rs 5,000 to 8,000 one time is fine.
- New asks: invoice series must continue from my current Vyapar number mid-year (Bhalla ji's rule), and "plus 18 percent" stated plainly so my CA can take ITC on the subscription invoice.
- Agree with Meenal on GST thinking, Vikram on caps in writing; disagree with Hiren's "per-order never" and with Rohit's 25 to 30 percent for life agency cut. Still Rs 0 until WhatsApp works on my own number.

## Round 3 notes

- Hiren and I are the wholesale voices here; my 600 boutique buyers never use links, only voice notes and photos, so WhatsApp on my own number is the gate above all else. Per-order fee is fine if capped and defined clearly (no charge for cancelled or RTO orders).
- Concrete plan I would pay for: Rs 1,400 flat including 500 orders, staff logins, Tally export and WhatsApp when it ships; extra orders at Rs 2 each capped at 1,000 a month. Setup Rs 5,000 for catalog upload and Punjabi tuning. Trial minimum 30 days, paid after one full GST month.
- Feature to pay now: WhatsApp assistant on my own number with voice-note handling. Feature to cancel: one wrong carton price quoted to a boutique, data leak, Meta warning on my 12-year-old number, or invoice series break with my CA complaining beyond two months. Agency commission must be setup only, not recurring; Rohit's 25 to 30 percent for life is predatory.

## Round 4 vote

- Growth plan is fair and fits our volume, but WhatsApp on my own number is the deal-maker. I will not pay until it ships and works for a full GST month.
- Tally-ready export and continuous invoice series are non-negotiable for my CA. Once both are live and tested, I move from maybe to yes.
