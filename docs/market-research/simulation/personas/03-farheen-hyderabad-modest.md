# Panelist 03: Farheen Siddiqui, Noor Modest, Hyderabad

## Profile

- **Who:** Farheen Siddiqui, 29, Hyderabad. Runs "Noor Modest" from home, alone, with two small kids (under six).
- **Sells:** abayas, hijabs, prayer sets. Buyers are mostly women in Hyderabad and a few other cities, many first-timers who found a reel or story.
- **Numbers:** about 70 orders a month, average order ₹1,600, so roughly ₹1.12 lakh a month. Half prepaid by UPI (Google Pay), half COD.
- **My own rough sense of money (assumed, not given):** after fabric, tailoring, courier and packing I keep about ₹25,000 to 28,000 a month. Not GST registered (about ₹13 lakh a year is under the goods threshold).
- **Phone only.** No laptop. Instagram, WhatsApp Business app, Delhivery through a local agent (I hand over parcels, the agent gives me the tracking number later), Google Pay.
- **My pain:** most DMs arrive between 10pm and 1am, when I am putting the kids to sleep. I answer in the morning and the buyer has often ordered elsewhere.
- **Tool spend today:** about ₹0. Free Canva, free CapCut, free WhatsApp Business app. The only "growth" money is an occasional reel boost of ₹1,000 to 1,500. ₹500 a month feels like a lot unless it clearly brings sales.
- **Languages:** Urdu, Hindi, English. Buyers write Roman Urdu, Hinglish, some Dakhni, some Telugu, a few in Urdu script, and many send voice notes.
- **What I care about:** replies that are modest and respectful (never cheap, never pushy), and my buyers' privacy (names, phone numbers, addresses, sometimes photos).

## Round 1

### Stage 1. Cold first impression

It is 11:40pm, kids finally asleep, I am scrolling reels and this sponsored post comes up: "Every DM answered. Every order closed in the chat."

**What I think it is:** an auto-reply bot for Instagram, like those "comment LINK and I will DM you" things I see sellers use, but one that also takes the order. "From your real catalog" means I would have to upload my products first. "You approve with a tap" tells me it does not send the order by itself, I check it first. That part I like.

**Would I tap?** Yes. It names my exact problem (DMs that wait) and it says no card. Tapping costs me nothing. Signing up is a different question.

**What I suspect:**
- It is another "growth" tool that sounds great and then asks for money in 15 days.
- "In the buyer's language": my buyers write Roman Urdu, Dakhni and Hinglish mixed with Telugu. Will it get "mereko abaya chahiye, kitna hua" or only textbook Hindi?
- Will it sound cheap or pushy to a woman buying an abaya? Bots usually talk like a chai-stall salesman.
- Does it need my Instagram password? Could my account get blocked?
- Who is CartHedge? I have never heard the name.

**What I need to see next:** a real short video of one chat on a modest-wear shop in Hindi/Urdu, the price in rupees on the very first page (with or without GST), whether it works on WhatsApp too (half my buyers move there), who is behind it and a human I can message, and one or two sellers like me saying it worked.

### Stage 2. Looking around

What I would actually do, in ten minutes, on my phone:

- **Already free in my phone:** WhatsApp Business app has greeting message, away message and quick replies. Instagram has saved replies. Those do not take orders, but they are free, so CartHedge has to beat free.
- **Alternatives and prices.** I searched "Instagram DM auto reply India price". Comparison blogs list ReplyKaro at about ₹99 a month with a free plan (keyword and comment-to-DM replies), ManyChat Essential about ₹1,165 a month with the AI add-on about ₹2,400 more, AiSensy about ₹999, Interakt about ₹2,499, WATI from about $49. Careful: ReplyKaro's own site is one of the sources, so it is selling itself. But it shows the market: a keyword bot is ₹100, a proper WhatsApp tool is ₹1,000 to 2,500. Sources: [postengage.ai comparison](https://postengage.ai/blog/cheapest-instagram-dm-automation-india), [ReplyKaro](https://www.replykaro.com/), [flowgent.ai comparison](https://flowgent.ai/blog/instagram-dm-automation-tool).
- **Reviews of CartHedge:** I searched "CartHedge reviews". Nothing. No reviews, no videos, no seller group talking about it. That is a yellow flag, not a red one, since it is new. I would ask in my sellers' WhatsApp group.
- **WhatsApp charges:** I checked what Meta charges because everyone says "WhatsApp API is costly". Blogs quote, in India, about ₹0.86 per marketing message (about ₹1.02 with GST) and about ₹0.115 per utility message (order updates, OTPs). For my 70 orders, order updates are a few rupees a month. Blasting 500 old buyers an offer would be about ₹500. So Meta's charge is not my worry, the tool's monthly fee is. Sources: [DEV Community guide](https://dev.to/dawnofgenx/the-complete-guide-to-whatsapp-business-api-pricing-in-india-2026-1hbh), [whautomate rate card](https://whautomate.com/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india), [chatmitra](https://chatmitra.com/blog/whatsapp-business-cost-per-message/).
- **What worries me from this look-around:** the cheap tools are keyword bots, the "real" ones are priced for shops bigger than mine, and I could not tell which kind CartHedge is from the ad.

### Stage 3. What I learned from the product files

I read product.txt and the website words. Fair about what is live: the Instagram DM assistant, order board, links, COD confirmation, returns, GST invoices, buyer order page are live. WhatsApp auto-reply on my own number, comment-to-DM, live selling, and photo/story matching are not.

What stood out for my shop:

1. **The plan ladder is built on order volume, my need is a feature.** The thing the ad promises (replies to every DM, in chat, at night) is only in **Pro at ₹1,999**. Growth at ₹999 only turns chats into "ready drafts", it does not reply to the buyer at 11pm. Starter at ₹499 has no AI at all. I do 70 orders a month. Pro gives me 2,000 orders. I would pay for 1,930 orders I will never have, just to get the one feature I need. And no one says if prices include 18% GST.
2. **The "money saved" story is written for someone else.** The RTO calculator and the "150 COD orders at 35% RTO, ₹8,000 to 15,000 lost" example are for bigger COD sellers. I have about 35 COD orders a month and maybe 15% come back. That is about 5 parcels, about ₹200 each (both ways plus packing), so about ₹1,000 a month, and the site's own calculator assumption (down to about 8%) would save me about ₹500. It does not "cover Growth many times over" for me. My real loss is the late-night DMs, and the site hardly leads with that for a small seller.
3. **I like the control.** Auto-confirm is off by default, I approve with a tap, the assistant steps back when I reply myself, there is a "business hours, answer only outside them" option, a polite or warm tone, "aap" or "tum", emoji on or off, and a practice chat where I can test it with my own catalog before buyers see anything. That practice chat is the thing that would decide it for me.
4. **The sample replies worry me.** The "fun" sample ("Yeh kurti har function mein hit hai") and the "warm" one ("Aapke liye rakh doon?") are not how Noor Modest talks. The "polite, aap" sample is closer. I would need a clear way to say what it must never say.
5. **WhatsApp is next, not now.** Buyers see my Instagram, ask one question, then say "WhatsApp number do". Today, order updates and OTPs would go from CartHedge's own number, not mine. A buyer getting an OTP from some unknown number called CartHedge, for a modest-wear shop: some will think it is a scam.
6. **Staff can "view as seller" for support.** It is read-only and recorded, and that is better than nothing, but my buyers' addresses and phone numbers would be visible to people I have never met. Also the AI: product.txt mentions model providers and "AI spend". I want to know whose AI reads my buyers' chats.
7. **Phone only.** I could not tell whether the whole seller app is comfortable on a phone. Bulk CSV import is useless to me (no laptop), and entering about 45 abayas with lengths, colours and photos by thumb would take me a week of nights.
8. **Payments.** The story is built around my own Razorpay. I take money on Google Pay today. The FAQ says "your own Razorpay account or UPI" but I could not tell how a plain UPI payment gets confirmed without me checking screenshots, which is the exact chore I want to drop.
9. **A fake review that looks like me.** In the website strings file, the fallback testimonials include "Farheen A., Modest fashion, Hyderabad, 4 hours saved weekly". I am Farheen S., and I never said that. The file comments say seeded quotes are meant to be hidden until real ones replace them, so maybe the live page never shows it, but if it ever does, that is a fake review and I would walk away. Please delete placeholder testimonials that look like real sellers from the source.
10. **Trial.** The 15 days start at signup. For me, setup is evenings after the kids sleep, so half the trial is gone before the assistant meets a real buyer. And COD results (RTO) show up 10 days or more after dispatch, so 15 days cannot prove the COD confirmation.

### Stage 4. The interview

**1. In your words, what does it do? What confused you?**
It sits behind my Instagram DMs. When someone messages at midnight asking price, size or COD, it answers in her language from my products, collects name, number and address, shows an exact summary, she says "haan", and I wake up to a ready order to approve with one tap. It also gives me an order board, a shop link, a page where buyers track their order, returns and invoices.
What confused me:
- The ad says every DM answered, but only Pro (₹1,999) replies. Growth only drafts. Why so many AI names (sales assistant, order capture, reply assistant)?
- Do prices include GST?
- What counts as an "order" against the quota? (Exchanges are said not to count, good.)
- Razorpay versus my plain Google Pay UPI.
- WhatsApp is half my chats and it is "next".
- The RTO talk feels like it is for bigger sellers.

**2. Top 3 things that excite you, ranked**
1. It replies in seconds between 10pm and 1am. That is exactly when I lose buyers. If I lose even 8 orders a month to slow replies, that is about ₹12,800 of sales. This is the only reason I would pay anything.
2. I hold the switches: tap to approve, auto-confirm off by default, it steps back when I reply, "answer only outside business hours" option, and a practice chat. I can sleep without feeling I handed my shop to a robot.
3. One clean place for orders with the address already collected, no more screenshots and notes, and buyers track their own parcel so "order kahan hai" stops. With two kids and the agent only sending me tracking late, this saves real time.

**3. Top 3 doubts, fears or deal-breakers**
1. **Price versus what I actually get.** The feature I need is ₹1,999, probably plus GST, for 70 orders. That is about 7% of what I keep in a month, to try something I have never seen work. Deal-breaker unless the price is split by feature, or I see orders it closed.
2. **Tone and trust.** One rude, cheap or wrong reply (wrong price, stock I do not have, "hit hai", pushing a woman who said no) becomes a screenshot in the Hyderabad women's seller groups, and my name is on it. Also: does it get Roman Urdu, Dakhni, Telugu mix, voice notes, and "Assalamu alaikum"?
3. **Buyer privacy and strangers.** Names, phones, addresses, sometimes photos. Who sees them (staff "view as seller", the AI company), where are they stored, can I delete one buyer on request. Buyers getting OTPs from an unknown "CartHedge" number. Plus a new company with no reviews: who do I message at 11pm when it breaks, and could Instagram flag my account.

**4. Features missing that you would need**
- Story and reel replies, and "ye wala" screenshots, matched to my products. Most of my DMs are "price?" on a story or reel. **Must have before I pay for the AI plan.** (It is on the "coming" list.)
- Phone-first setup that works on a small screen, plus someone to upload my catalog for me. **Must have before I pay.** Without a laptop, a CSV is useless.
- Plain Google Pay UPI path that confirms payment without Razorpay KYC and without me checking screenshots. **Must have before I pay.**
- Size help by height ("5'3, kaunsa length?") answered from my chart, in the buyer's words. **Must have before I pay** (I would test it in the practice chat).
- A "no return, hygiene item" flag per product (hijab pins, inner caps), and exchange-only for abayas, so the assistant does not promise a return it should not. **Must have before I pay.**
- A "never say" list and a fixed Assalamu alaikum / Walaikum assalam opening. **Must have before I pay.**
- Polite handling of voice notes (at least "I have noted it, Farheen will reply shortly"). **Must have before I pay**, full transcription is nice later.
- Auto-reply on my own WhatsApp number. **Must have before I pay anything near Pro**, nice later if I only pay a small Instagram-only price at first.
- Buyer messages and order page showing "Noor Modest" instead of CartHedge. **Nice later** (I would pay a little for it).
- Replies in Urdu script, and Eid and Ramadan greeting templates. **Nice later.**

**5. Features you would never use**
Reseller pricing (I do not sell to resellers), GST invoices and credit notes (not registered, maybe later), courier handoff (my local agent is not on a courier panel), bulk actions for 100 orders and pick lists (70 orders, I pack myself), live selling (my shop does not show faces and I will not go live), dropped-checkout reminders (chasing a woman who left a cart feels pushy to me), customer risk scoring and the deep insights, and broadcast drops except maybe a rare Eid note to people who said yes.

**6. What you spend today, and how you like to pay**
Tools: about ₹0 a month (Canva free, CapCut free, WhatsApp Business free). Reel boost ₹1,000 to 1,500 in some months. No staff, no agency. Courier is per parcel through my agent, not a tool.
Payment: UPI. I would use UPI AutoPay with a cap and a WhatsApp reminder two days before, and a one-tap cancel, but only after I trust it. No yearly upfront for now.

**7. Price sensitivity** (for Instagram AI replies plus order board, about 70 orders a month, all-in including GST, since that is what leaves my Google Pay)
- (a) So cheap I would doubt it: under ₹199.
- (b) A bargain: ₹399 to 499.
- (c) Getting expensive but I would still pay: ₹799 to 999, only after it has closed orders for me for two months.
- (d) Too expensive: ₹1,499 and up. ₹1,999 is a no for 70 orders.

**8. React to the current price list**
Starter ₹499 fits my order count but has no replying. Growth ₹999 drafts orders but does not answer a buyer at 11pm. Pro ₹1,999 is the only one that does what the ad says, and it is made for a shop thirty times my size. The plan that fits me does not exist: Starter plus the night assistant at about ₹800 to 900 total.
Would I pay after the trial? Only if the assistant closed at least 10 orders (about ₹16,000) I would have lost, and I can show it to my husband in the app. What makes me upgrade: WhatsApp on my own number, story replies working, going past 100 orders in a month. What makes me leave: one rude or wrong reply to a buyer, a buyer complaint about their data, no one answering me at night, a price rise without warning, a surprise charge, an Instagram warning.
Flat, per-order or percent of sales: flat fee, because I can plan around it. A small per-order fee above a generous quota is fine (₹3 over 100 only bites when I am earning). A percentage of sales, never: even 2% of ₹1.12 lakh is ₹2,240 for no change in what I do, and it makes the tool my partner in profit.

**9. Free plan or free trial? Annual?**
A small free plan would win me and I would tell my group: say 20 AI-handled orders a month on Instagram. ReplyKaro has a free plan already. If no free plan, then 30 days, and the clock should start when I connect Instagram and the first DM is answered, not when I sign up. 15 days is too short: setup eats a week of nights, and a COD return shows only after 10 days.
Annual: not at first. After three months of paying, 30% off and money back for the unused months. "Two months free" (about 17%) on ₹8,000 to 10,000 upfront is still a whole month of what I keep.

**10. Add-ons you would actually pay for**
- Done-for-you setup and catalog upload from my Instagram: ₹999 one time, happy. ₹1,500, I hesitate. Above ₹2,000, no.
- Auto-reply on my own WhatsApp number: ₹299 a month on top. If it is included in the plan, even better. ₹499 a month is too much.
- Remove CartHedge branding, so buyers see Noor Modest: ₹99 a month.
- Support on WhatsApp voice notes in Urdu or Hindi between 9pm and 11pm (I cannot take calls with kids): ₹199 a month.
- WhatsApp message packs: I would pay for usage at cost, not a monthly pack. 200 Eid messages is about ₹200 at Meta's rate, that I can accept.
- COD/RTO protection: ₹10 to 15 per COD order, only if it refunds me when a parcel is refused. Otherwise no.
- Extra staff logins, courier booking, more AI replies: no.

**11. Who else influences the decision**
- My husband: asks "kitna lagega, kya fayda, and who sees our customers' details?" He will not like a surprise charge.
- My sellers' WhatsApp group (Hyderabad women running modest-wear and home shops, about 200 of us): two sellers saying "it worked for me" is worth more than any ad. One screenshot of a rude bot and I am out.
- My sister, who clicks my product photos: "does it sound like you?"
- No CA. A cousin helps with tax and would only care if I register for GST.

**12. One sentence to a friend right now**
"Baji, there is a new app that answers my midnight Instagram DMs in Hindi and Urdu and keeps the order ready for me to approve, but the part I need costs ₹1,999, so I will try the free days first and see if it sounds like me before I pay a single rupee."

## Round 3 notes

- **Concrete plan I designed:** Starter ₹399 all-in GST (100 orders) plus optional Night Replies ₹399 (10pm-6am only) = ₹798 total all-in for my use case. This eliminates overpaying for unused quota (70 orders, not 2,000) and makes night replies a feature I can buy separately, not a plan upgrade. WhatsApp messages on a separate prepaid wallet at Meta's rate, not bundled.
- **What makes me pay:** Night replies closing 8+ orders per month I would otherwise lose to slow responses (₹12,800 of sales). What makes me cancel instantly: one rude or wrong bot reply to a buyer, or a privacy breach (buyer data in staff screenshots or with unknown AI companies). Seller verification must be real, not free plans that enable fake pages.
- **Key disagreement:** Hiren says GST extra is fine, but I cannot take credit and ₹1,999 for me means ₹2,359 out of ₹25,000 I keep monthly. Feature-based pricing (Night Replies add-on, Staff logins add-on, WhatsApp add-on) is the only ladder that fits both a 1,200-order Hiren and a 70-order me in one product.

## Round 4 vote

- **Voting Starter at ₹589 all-in GST.** The move of AI replies to Starter (300/mo) solves the bait-and-switch I feared, and ₹589 is inside my "still pay" range once I test the tone and see closed orders in the 30-day trial. The hard caps and price lock matter more than perfect sizing for 70 orders.
- **Deal-maker is WhatsApp on own number at ₹199/mo (not ₹499).** Half my buyers move from Instagram to WhatsApp after one question. At ₹499/mo it is a second plan upgrade and breaks my math. At ₹199, I would add it in month two once I trust the replies.
