# Panelist 16: Pooja Yadav, buyer, Kanpur

## Profile

- **Who:** Pooja Yadav, 28, teacher at a private school in Kanpur. Lives in Kakadeo with husband (private-sector job) and in-laws. Salary about ₹27,000 a month.
- **Role on this panel:** the BUYER voice. I do not buy CartHedge. I meet it through sellers' chats, links, OTP checkout, COD confirmation, the order page, returns and offer messages.
- **Shopping habit:** clothes, artificial and silver jewellery, home items (bedsheets, wall hangings, kitchen organisers) from Instagram sellers, 3 to 4 orders a month, average ₹900, so about ₹3,500 a month. Roughly 3 out of 4 orders are COD. I pay UPI (PhonePe) only to a seller I have already received from once.
- **Phone and language:** mid-range Android (Redmi, 6 GB), Instagram and WhatsApp all day. I type Hinglish in Roman letters, read Hindi in Devanagari without effort, English for school work. I shop after 9 pm, once the copies are checked and the house is quiet.
- **The scar:** two years ago a page called "ready stock Banarasi dupatta set" told me COD was "not available for first order". I paid ₹1,350 on UPI, got a fake tracking number, then got blocked. I filed on cybercrime.gov.in and got nothing back. Since then: COD first, UPI only after trust, and I screenshot everything.
- **Who I ask before buying from a new page:** my staffroom WhatsApp group (6 teachers), my bhabhi, and the comments and "received" story highlights on the seller's page. My husband's rule is "pehle COD hi lena".
- **Money mindset:** I notice ₹30 extra at checkout. A "convenience fee" line is enough for me to close the tab and open Meesho.

## Round 1

### Stage 1. Cold first impression

I am scrolling Reels at 10 pm and this ad comes up: "Every DM answered. Every order closed in the chat. CartHedge replies to buyers in their language from your real catalog, collects the order and confirms it. You approve with a tap."

**What I think it is.** Ye sellers ke liye hai, not for me. It is a bot that sits in a seller's Instagram inbox and answers people like me, then takes the order inside the chat. "You approve with a tap" means the seller taps OK on my order. So the first thing I think of is not "nice tool", it is: aaj ke baad jab main kisi page ko DM karungi, kaun reply karega, insaan ya machine?

**Would I tap.** "Start free trial": no, I have no shop. Maybe I would tap the post itself to read more, because it is about my chats. My cousin Rinki sells sarees from Lucknow, I might forward it to her, but only if I see something that does not look like another "grow your page" scam ad.

**What I would suspect.**
- "Closed in the chat" means pushed to pay quickly, inside a DM, which is exactly where I got cheated. A bot that closes an order faster is also a scammer's dream tool.
- "15 days free, no card": easy for anyone to sign up, including fake pages. That is the thing I would worry about, not the AI.
- Who is CartHedge? Never heard of it.

**What I need to see next.** Not the seller pitch. I want to see what *I* get as a buyer: what the bot's chat looks like, how I know it is a bot, where my money goes, and whether I can still pay COD.

### Stage 2. Looking around like a real person

What I actually did, 10 minutes on my phone:
1. Searched "CartHedge reviews" on Google. Nothing real: no Play Store app, no reviews, no Reddit thread. Only other tools came up, like [Orders App for Instagram DM orders](https://orders.app/instagram-dm-orders). No reviews means I cannot trust it, but it also means it is new, so I do not hold it fully against them.
2. Searched "Instagram seller UPI took money blocked" to remind myself what the red flags are. Same advice my group always gives: [prepaid-only is the pattern, always look for COD](https://righttoinformation.wiki/instagram-store-scam-india), and what to do after [being blocked](https://righttoinformation.wiki/instagram-seller-money-blocked-recovery-india). That is why I care about the token and the "verified" claims.
3. What I already know as alternatives: Meesho (COD, 7-day return, someone to complain to), Myntra and Ajio for brands, and the usual Instagram page with just a UPI QR screenshot. Those QR pages are what a tool like this is trying to replace. Meesho feels safe because the platform is between me and the seller. With CartHedge, the seller's own Razorpay is between us, so only the seller's honesty matters.
4. WhatsApp or Meta charges: not my problem as a buyer. OTP and order updates on WhatsApp cost me nothing.

### Stage 3. What I learned from the founder's files

Reading `product.txt` and the website copy as a buyer, plainly:

**What I liked.**
- The order page: live status, courier and tracking id, cancel, change address, ask for return with photos, all at one link with no app and no password. That ends the "bhaiya order kahan hai" cycle I run every week.
- Returns and exchanges with a OTP-confirmed request and refund recorded per order. A GST invoice with a credit note. A proper bill is proof, something the fake pages never give.
- The bot "never claims to be human". If I ask "bot ho?" it says it is the shop's assistant and that the owner sees the chat.
- Offer messages need an unticked box, STOP in every message, and consent kept on record.
- Pincode and address checks, and the order summary computed by CartHedge, not the AI, so the total in chat equals the total at checkout.

**What worried me while reading.**
- "Payments secured by Razorpay · Seller verified". What is verified? PAN? Address? Or just "signed up"? If a trial account gets that strip, the strip is a lie that scammers will use on me.
- The COD token is "UPI". Is it paid to the shop's Razorpay or to a UPI ID of someone? I got burned by a personal UPI ID.
- The bot only discloses itself *if asked*. Most people will not ask.
- The "haan" rule: "only a yes to the exact summary counts". I type "haan" casually all the time ("haan ok dekhti hoon"). Hopefully the summary card makes it clear.
- COD flow for link orders is three steps (summary, address confirm, intent re-confirm). Too many taps and I will drop off.
- Customer ledger tracks "COD refusals" and "risk flag", and the roadmap talks about a network risk score across sellers. As a buyer that scares me: a hidden score on my phone number that I cannot see or fix. I once refused a parcel because it came 12 days late.
- Dropped-checkout reminder: I verify my number to *check the total*, then a WhatsApp message arrives later. That OTP was for the order, not for marketing.
- Screenshots ("ye wala chahiye") and voice notes are what I send most. Photo/story matching is "coming next", so today the bot may not understand me. Same with comment-to-DM (coming) and WhatsApp auto-replies on the seller's own number (coming, so right now the DM bot is Instagram only; WhatsApp is for order updates and OTPs from CartHedge's own number).

**Live versus coming, being fair to them.** Live: Instagram DM assistant, order summary, link checkout with OTP, COD confirmation with optional token, order page with tracking and self-service, returns and refunds, GST invoices. Coming: WhatsApp on the seller's own number, comment-to-DM, live selling, photo/story matching, and broadcast sending on WhatsApp (so offer messages are not reaching me yet; my answers on those are about what I expect).

### Stage 4. Interview (buyer view)

**1. In my words, what does it do? What confused me?**
It is a bot plus order desk for the seller. I message a page, the bot answers from the seller's real catalog in my language, writes my order as a summary, I say yes, and the seller taps approve. Then I get a link with tracking, return and cancel on it. Everything is "same chat".
Confusing: (a) who is "verified" and by whom, (b) why a *COD* order needs a UPI "token" at all, (c) "you approve with a tap" means the seller decides after I already said yes, so what do I get told if they say no, (d) whether CartHedge keeps my phone number and address for its own use.

**2. Top 3 things that excite me, ranked.**
1. **Reply at night, in Hindi or Hinglish, in seconds.** I order after 9 pm. Most sellers reply next afternoon, by then I have ordered on Meesho. A reply at 10 pm with price, size and COD answer decides which seller gets my money. This alone would move 1 or 2 orders a month back from Meesho to Instagram sellers.
2. **One tracking link with courier name, tracking id and status.** No more asking "bhaiya order kahan hai" and getting a thumbs-up emoji. I also like that it opens inside Instagram, no app.
3. **Return and exchange I can start myself with photos, and a proper bill.** The bill with seller's name and address is evidence if something goes wrong. It is also why my husband would stop saying "Instagram se mat mangwao".

**3. Top 3 doubts, fears or deal-breakers.**
1. **Fake sellers hiding behind a real-looking link.** Trial with no card means anyone can get the pretty link, the Razorpay strip and the "verified" word. If a scammer uses CartHedge, I will blame CartHedge and every seller on it. Deal-breaker unless "verified" is hard (ID, address, bank, phone checked).
2. **The token.** ₹50 to ₹100 to an unknown page in advance is the same thing that burned me. If it goes to a personal UPI or a QR with someone's name, I walk away. Deal-breaker without Razorpay, shop name, refund promise.
3. **A bot with no way out.** Bot misreads, repeats the same question, ignores my screenshot, and pushes the order summary when I am still asking about fabric. I want "owner se baat karni hai" in one tap, and a clear first line saying it is the shop's assistant.
Also: OTP message from a number I do not know (looks like a phishing OTP), hidden COD-risk score, and offer spam.

**4. Features missing that I would need.**
*Must have before I trust a seller on it:*
- "Seller verified" that means something, with the real business name, address and phone on the order page and the payment screen. Say what was checked.
- The bot says up front "main [shop] ka AI assistant hoon, owner bhi chat dekhte hain", not only when asked.
- Token paid only on a Razorpay page with the shop's name. It is subtracted from my COD amount. It comes back to me automatically if the seller cancels or does not ship in 3 days. Say that in one line on the page.
- Order page and WhatsApp messages in Devanagari Hindi, not only English.
- Courier name, tracking id, and expected delivery date on the order page. Refund timeline in days, stated before I ask for the return.
- A way to see and challenge a "risky buyer" flag on my own number.
- Every OTP message says "yeh OTP kisi ko na batayein, sirf order page par daalein" and the link is on a clear domain, not a shortener.
- STOP in every offer message and offers only when I tick yes myself.
*Nice later:*
- Screenshot or reel photo matching ("ye wala"), and voice-note understanding.
- Real photo or video of the actual piece on request ("natural light mein dikhao").
- Seller ratings from past buyers on the store page, and "N parcels delivered".
- No token for repeat buyers after 2 delivered orders.
- Restock alerts on WhatsApp for items I wanted.
- Pay-by-UPI at the door option for COD.

**5. Features I would never use (or would switch off).**
Broadcast and offer messages (I would not tick the box), reseller prices (not me), GST company details, QR code links, the dropped-checkout reminder unless I opted in myself. Anything that asks me to create an account.

**6. What I spend and how I pay.**
Tools: nothing, I am a buyer. My spend is about ₹3,500 a month on orders, ₹30 to ₹40 COD charge on maybe two of them, ₹50 to ₹80 shipping on small ones. I like COD for 3 in 4 orders. UPI (PhonePe) only after I have received one parcel from that seller. I do not save my card on any seller link, I will not. Autopay, annual: not my thing.

**7. Price sensitivity (the price that touches me: token, COD fee, and any fee at checkout).**
- **(a) So cheap I would doubt it:** a token of ₹1 to ₹20. That looks like a "test payment" to grab my UPI details. Also a "verified seller" that costs ₹0 to get.
- **(b) A bargain:** ₹50 token, adjusted in my COD amount, refund guaranteed, from a seller I know. COD charge of ₹20 to ₹30. A prepaid discount of ₹50 or more on a ₹1,000 order, once I trust the seller.
- **(c) Getting expensive but I would pay:** ₹100 token on a ₹1,500 or higher order, from a seller with 2 good deliveries and a visible "received" highlight. COD fee of ₹40.
- **(d) Too expensive:** any token over ₹100, any token that is not adjusted, a token on a ₹500 order, COD fee of ₹50 or more, a "platform fee" or "convenience fee" line (even ₹10 makes me feel Meesho is better).
What makes me buy more from a seller who uses this: reply at night, one true tracking page, return that works, COD available with no token after two good orders, and Hindi. I would go from 3 or 4 orders to maybe 5 a month, mostly by moving Meesho orders back to Instagram sellers.

**8. React to the current price list (as the seller's customer).**
I will never see ₹499/₹999/₹1,999. What I care about is that the seller does not pass it to me. If sellers add a ₹10 to ₹20 fee because of a ₹3 per-order charge, I notice. Flat monthly fee is what I trust, because my price does not change. A per-order fee is fine only if it stays on the seller's side. A percentage of sales makes sellers raise prices or add fees, and I would see that as the platform eating my money. If I were the seller I would keep the plan quiet and never show a "CartHedge fee" to the buyer.
What makes me leave a seller who uses it: a token I did not expect, a bot that will not let me talk to a person, a message on WhatsApp I did not ask for, a parcel without tracking. What makes me stay: it works every time.

**9. Free trial or free plan? Annual?**
As a buyer I am nervous about the free trial with no card, because of fake sellers. I would like a "new seller" label for the first 15 days or first 20 orders, and during trial the seller should not be allowed to collect a token or prepaid UPI, only COD, until their ID is checked. A seller who paid for the year looks more serious to me than one on free trial, so a "paid member" signal would help me. Annual discount does not matter to me directly.

**10. Add-ons I would pay for.**
Nothing for the app. I would pay something only for **protection**: ₹20 on an order above ₹1,000 from a new seller on prepaid, if CartHedge or Razorpay refunds me when the seller disappears. Not ₹50, and never as a hidden charge. ₹0 for WhatsApp updates, I expect those. Courier booking, branding removal, staff logins: not my concern.

**11. Who influences my decision.**
My staffroom group (6 teachers, one of them is always "yeh page fake hai"), my bhabhi, my husband ("COD hi lena"), and the comments and received-parcel highlights on the page. They would ask: paisa kahan jaata hai, wapas kaise aayega, number kis ko milega, "agar seller bhaag gaya toh?".

**12. One sentence to a friend.**
"Agar seller ye use karta hai aur order page par asli naam, address, tracking aur return dikhe toh main COD pe bina dar ke order karungi, lekin agar 50 rupaye advance maange aur 'verified' ka matlab nahi bataye toh main wapas Meesho."

## Round 2 notes

- I now accept a COD token for a first order from a new seller: ₹30 to ₹50 even on a ₹650 order, only if paid on a Razorpay page with the shop's name, adjusted in the COD amount, auto-refunded if the seller does not ship, and dropped after 2 delivered parcels. Devanshi's ₹240 loss per refusal made me see the seller side. Still no token over ₹100 and no token on trial sellers.
- Charging view unchanged: flat monthly seller fee, cap in writing, per-order charge stays on the seller's side. Any "handling" or "convenience" line at my checkout, even ₹10, sends me to Meesho. Percent of sales makes sellers raise my prices.
- A price floor is a trust signal: a ₹99 or free plan that gives the "Seller verified" strip to anyone helps fake pages. Seller plan of ₹499 or more plus a "paid member" label reads safer. Trial sellers: COD only until ID is checked. Free trial no-card is still my biggest fear.
- Agree with Vikram (autopay overage and hidden fees get passed to buyers) and Arjun (price computed in code, human reply pauses the bot, which eased my wrong-total fear). Push back on Shabnam (cheap or free for sellers is not harmless for buyers). Partly with Devanshi: token yes, but late dispatch causes refusals too.
- Still unanswered: what "Seller verified" actually checks, and a way to see and challenge a risk flag on my number.

## Round 3 notes

- Token only works for real sellers with real proof: trial sellers get no token collection (COD only), GSTIN registered sellers get "verified on portal" proof on order page, unregistered get "verified by phone". Trial to paid conversion gate: seller pays to unlock token collection, otherwise link bans after 30 days. This stops fake pages cold.
- Price conflict solved by code, not dispute: order summary computed by CartHedge is final fact, bot quotes are just chat flavor, discrepancies get "seller to confirm" pause. My screenshot, their price, CartHedge's math wins order. Builds trust that numbers are honest.
- My plan that works for buyers like me: Starter ₹799 (100 DM replies, night auto-replies), Growth ₹1,299 (300 replies, includes ₹50 token), Pro ₹1,999 (unlimited, WhatsApp). Zero hidden fees, zero per-order charges passed to me, verified seller badge tied to payment + real check.

## Round 4 vote

- Growth at ₹1,299 is the right middle: verified sellers can afford good night coverage (2,500 replies), token on Razorpay means my money is safe, and no surprise per-order fees hit me.
- One condition: "verified" must check GSTIN and address for real, not just a signup checkbox. If CartHedge truly verifies, fake pages cannot hide; if loose, I go back to Meesho.
