# Panelist 10: Sneha Kulkarni, Sugar & Spoon, Pune

## Profile

- **Who:** Sneha Kulkarni, 34, home baker. Brand: "Sugar & Spoon" (custom cakes, dessert boxes, festive hampers). Kitchen at home in Kothrud, Pune. Husband Amit works in IT in Hinjewadi and handles the money side of the household. Daughter Anvi is 6. Savita tai helps with packing and washing four days a week (about ₹7,000 a month).
- **Languages:** Marathi at home and with older customers, Hindi with most buyers, English for corporate and for Instagram captions. Types Marathi in roman script ("tai, 2 kg cake pahije, udya delivery hoil ka?") more than in Devanagari.
- **Business in numbers:** about 120 orders a month, average order ₹1,800, so roughly ₹2.2 lakh a month in sales. Weekends (Fri to Sun) carry about 65% of orders. Hard cap in her head: 6 to 7 cakes per weekend day plus about 10 dessert boxes. Festive months (Diwali, Christmas, Rakhi, Valentine's) can touch 200 to 250 orders.
- **How orders arrive:** roughly 60% WhatsApp (repeat customers who saved her number), 40% Instagram DMs. Almost every order starts with a reference photo ("aisa cake chahiye").
- **How it works today:** everything made to order with a delivery date and a time slot. 50% advance by UPI to her PhonePe for Business QR, balance on delivery or before. Local delivery by Porter or Uber, charged to the customer. A few corporate bulk orders a month (20 to 60 boxes) that need GST invoices. FSSAI registered, GST registered because of the corporate work.
- **Her evenings:** 7:30 to 11 pm she is on the phone replying to DMs, quoting, fixing date and slot, chasing advances, copying everything into a notebook and a Google Sheet. She says about 3 hours a night. This is the thing she would pay to get back.
- **Tools and money today:** Canva Pro about ₹500 a month, free Zoho Invoice for GST invoices, free WhatsApp Business app (labels, quick replies, catalog), Google Sheet for the order calendar. CA (Joshi kaka) charges about ₹1,000 a month. Occasional Instagram boost of ₹1,000 to ₹1,500. No paid software beyond Canva.
- **Budget:** ₹500 to ₹1,000 a month, but only if it visibly saves her evenings.
- **Temperament:** practical, warm, a little tired. Burned once by a "WhatsApp marketing" tool a friend pushed (₹3,000 paid for a quarter, used twice). Does not trust anything that talks to her customers without her seeing it. Judges tools by one question: will it cost me a cake order or a customer?

## Round 1

### Stage 1. Cold first impression (the sponsored post)

I am scrolling Instagram after putting Anvi to bed, between a reel about ganache and someone's wedding cake, and this ad comes up: "Every DM answered. Every order closed in the chat."

**What I think it is:** a chatbot that answers my Instagram DMs and takes the order. Like those "comment PRICE and get a link" tools but a bit smarter. "From your real catalog" tells me it is made for people who sell things with a fixed price list, like kurtis and bags. My cakes do not have a catalog in that sense. Every cake is a conversation: how many kg, which flavour, eggless or not, photo reference, message on the cake, which date, which slot, can you deliver to Baner.

**Would I tap:** yes, probably, because it says 15 days free and no card, and because the line "every DM answered" is exactly my 11 pm problem. I would tap and look, not sign up. If it had said "enter card details" I would have scrolled on.

**What I would suspect:**
- It is for fashion sellers and someone just changed the words. I have seen this before.
- A bot talking to my customers in my name. If it says "yes, delivery tomorrow 5 pm" and I cannot do it, I am the one who disappoints a mother on her child's birthday.
- Does it understand Marathi? Hindi and English I can assume. "Pahije", "udya", "kiti kg lagel" is how my customers type.
- Instagram only? Most of my orders come on WhatsApp, not Instagram. If it does not work there, it fixes the smaller half of my inbox.
- Price is not in the ad. Free trial and then a surprise.
- Could my Instagram account get banned for using automation? Two home baker friends have had accounts restricted.

**What I need to see next:** a 30 second video of one real cake order in Marathi or Hindi, from "reference photo" to "advance paid, date and slot booked". The price page. Whether it works on WhatsApp. Who is behind it, and one baker or sweet shop using it, not a boutique.

### Stage 2. Looking around

What I would actually check, short:

- **Cheaper alternatives I know of:** Instagram auto-DM tools like ReplyKaro start around ₹99 a month in India, but they are keyword reply tools ("comment PRICE, get a link"), not order takers ([ReplyKaro](https://www.replykaro.com/instagram-automation/india)). WATI's single user pay-as-you-go is ₹999 with message credits, and the proper plans are in dollars ([WATI pricing](https://www.wati.io/en/pricing/)). Zoko is about $49.99 a month, so over ₹4,000, and Shopify-first ([Spur comparison](https://www.spurnow.com/en/blogs/best-whatsapp-api-provider-india)). There are order-management tools for home bakers in the US at about $10 a month ([Homegrown](https://findhomegrown.com/blog/order-management-tools-home-baker)) but nothing Indian that I know of, and none that does Marathi.
- **WhatsApp charges:** from 1 July 2025 Meta bills per message in India, and as per the guides I read: marketing about ₹0.86, utility about ₹0.115, plus 18% GST ([AiSensy](https://aisensy.com/pricing), [MyOperator](https://myoperator.com/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india-2026)). So a festive hamper offer to 300 old customers is about ₹300 plus GST for one blast. Fine for me twice a year. Order updates at ₹0.115 each is nothing. The thing I will remember is that the tool's price is not the only price.
- **Razorpay:** the ad lines will say "your own Razorpay". I checked: Razorpay standard is about 2% plus 18% GST, so 2.36% ([Razorpay UPI charges explained](https://razorpay.com/blog/upi-charges-explained-mdr-vs-platform-fees/), [SoftwareSuggest](https://www.softwaresuggest.com/blog/razorpay-payment-gateway-charges/)). On my ₹2.16 lakh a month that is about ₹5,000 a month if everything goes through it. My PhonePe QR costs nothing. So "CartHedge takes no cut" is true but is not the whole story.
- **Reviews:** I searched "CartHedge Instagram DM sales assistant India reviews" and found nothing about it, only lists of other DM tools ([search results](https://creatorlanehq.com/blog/best-instagram-dm-automation-tools-india)). New product, zero public reviews. That alone makes me want a trial and a reference, not a yearly plan.
- **Ask the group:** I would post in my "Pune Home Bakers" WhatsApp group (about 180 members) and ask if anyone has used it. Nobody will have.

### Stage 3. What I learned from the product and the website

I read the product definition and the website words. The honest reading, as a baker:

**What it is:** a sales assistant for Instagram DMs (auto-reply in the buyer's language from the seller's catalog, collects item, address and payment, shows an order summary, the seller approves with one tap), plus an order board, a branded link and storefront with OTP checkout, GST invoices, a customer ledger and alerts. The money goes to my own Razorpay account.

**What matches my business:**
- "Custom link: type item and price in ten seconds" and "made-to-order seller, no catalog needed". Finally one line that is about me.
- Options up to three groups (size, flavour, finish) with price per combination. Weight x flavour x eggless fits a cake menu.
- GST invoices with the buyer's GSTIN for corporate orders, numbered each financial year.
- Customer ledger with notes and order history. I could keep "allergic to nuts" and birthdays there.
- Alerts on WhatsApp when an order or a chat needs me. Practice chat to test the assistant before customers see it. Business hours with an away message.
- The assistant never invents a price or a date and hands over when unsure. That is what I want.

**What is clearly built for someone else:**
- The whole headline story is COD and RTO (cash on delivery refusals). My orders are advance-paid and made to order. Nobody refuses a birthday cake. The calculator, the "RTO saved" meter, COD confirmation and token, COD ceiling, returns and exchanges, reseller prices, back-in-stock waitlist, size charts: none of it is for me.
- The testimonials are a Jaipur juttis seller, modest fashion and silver jewellery. The founder's own file calls them seed fallback, so they are placeholders and not real customers. No baker anywhere.

**What I looked for and did not find:**
- A delivery date and time slot at checkout, and a cap on how many orders per day or per slot. This is my whole business.
- A 50% advance and balance later. What I see is a ₹50 to ₹100 "token" for COD and full payment by UPI or card.
- A calendar or "tomorrow's bake list" view by delivery date. The board is by status (new, confirmed, packed, shipped), not by day. The pick list is for shelves.
- Reference photo handling. The AI "photo and story replies" is on the coming list and is for matching catalog items, not for reading a cake design.
- FSSAI licence number on the invoice and the store page. Food sellers must show it.
- Local rider booking (Porter and similar). "Courier handoff" is for parcel couriers, and my cakes do not go by courier.

**Live versus coming, fairly:**
- Live: Instagram assistant, board, links, checkout, GST invoices, alerts, ledger.
- Coming: assistant on my own WhatsApp number, comment-to-DM, photo matching, live selling. Today, WhatsApp messages (order updates, OTPs) go from CartHedge's number, not mine, so my customer would get a message from an unknown number instead of "Sugar & Spoon". That worries me with repeat customers who trust my number.

**The price list against my budget:**
- Starter ₹499 for 100 orders then ₹3 each: with 120 orders that is about ₹559. It gives me a board and links. A notebook with a QR.
- Growth ₹999 gives AI order drafts, GST invoices, broadcasts. The 500 order quota is wasted on me.
- Pro ₹1,999 is the plan with the automatic DM replies, which is the exact thing the ad promised. That is double the top of my budget. The ad sells the ₹1,999 plan.

### Stage 4. The interview

**1. In your words, what does it do? What confused you?**

It sits behind my Instagram DMs and answers buyers in their language from my product list, collects the order details and shows a summary, and I approve with a tap. It also gives me one shop link with checkout, an order board and GST invoices. What confused me: (a) the ad says "every DM" but the automatic replies are only on the ₹1,999 plan and only on Instagram today, while most of my orders are on WhatsApp; (b) "real catalog" and "COD" everywhere, nothing about made-to-order, dates or advance; (c) "regional scripts" but I cannot see Marathi named; (d) why the status messages come from CartHedge's WhatsApp number and not mine.

**2. Top 3 things that excite me, ranked, and why**

1. **Fast first reply and order details captured from the chat, in my customer's language.** My evenings go to "kiti kg? flavour? date? address?" asked 300 times a month. If it asked those, put it in a ready card and I approve in the morning, I get 1.5 to 2 hours a night back. That is the only reason I would pay.
2. **Custom link plus payment with proof.** Type "2 kg truffle, photo cake, ₹3,200", send it, the customer pays the advance and I do not wait for a screenshot or doubt "maine kar diya". My biggest daily irritation is "advance aaya kya?".
3. **GST invoice for corporate orders from the order itself.** I make Zoho invoices by hand today. Auto invoice with the client's GSTIN, numbered, ready for the CA, saves me 20 minutes per corporate order and an argument with an accounts department.

**3. Top 3 doubts, fears or deal-breakers**

1. **It books a date or slot I cannot make, or quotes a price wrongly.** One wrong promise on a birthday cake and that customer is gone and tells ten people. I need a hard rule it cannot break: my capacity, my lead time, my blocked days.
2. **It does not work on WhatsApp, or works from someone else's number.** More than half my orders are on my own WhatsApp. If my regular customers get a message from "CartHedge" they will think it is spam or a scam.
3. **The real cost is above the sticker.** The price is not ₹499. It is ₹999 for the plan that has invoices, ₹1,999 for the auto replies, plus Razorpay at about 2.36%, which could be ₹5,000 a month for me versus a free PhonePe QR. Also a possible Instagram account restriction. Smaller fear: Marathi gets mangled and makes me look careless.

**4. Missing features I would need**

| Day-wise calendar and "tomorrow's bake and delivery list" (what to bake, which slot, which address) | **Must have before I pay** |
| Custom order fields: reference photo upload, message on cake, weight, flavour, eggless, allergies, free-text notes, carried from chat to link to board | **Must have before I pay** |
| Marathi (including roman Marathi) in the assistant, proven in the practice chat | **Must have before I pay** |
| FSSAI number on invoices and the store page | **Must have before I pay** |
| Assistant on my own WhatsApp number, and buyer messages sent from my number | **Must have before I pay for the AI part**. The board and invoices alone I can use without it, so I would only pay the Growth price for those. |
| Cancellation rule tied to the delivery date (advance non-refundable inside 48 hours, refundable before) | Nice later (I can write it in the policy text) |

**5. Features I would never use**

RTO savings meter, COD confirmation with token, COD charge and ceiling, returns and exchanges (a cake cannot come back), reseller pricing, back-in-stock waitlist, size charts, courier handoff to parcel couriers, live selling. Dropped-checkout reminders maybe, but I would not miss them.

**6. What I spend today, and how I like to pay**

- Software: Canva Pro about ₹500 a month. Everything else free (WhatsApp Business, Google Sheets, Zoho Invoice).
- CA: about ₹1,000 a month (not a tool, but invoices are part of that).
- Staff: Savita tai about ₹7,000 a month, nothing for admin.
- Marketing: about ₹1,000 to ₹1,500 in Instagram boosts in a good month.
- So real "app" spending is about ₹500. Anything I add has to beat what my evenings are worth.
- Payment: UPI, with autopay only after I trust it. No yearly upfront in the first year. Card is Amit's, not mine.

**7. Price sensitivity for the plan I would need, ₹ per month**

I would need: board, custom links, date and slot, advance, GST invoices, and the assistant on WhatsApp. Say that is a Growth-style plan.

- (a) So cheap I would doubt it: under ₹200. A tool that takes my orders for the price of two coffees would feel unserious.
- (b) A bargain: ₹399 to ₹599 all in.
- (c) Getting expensive but I would still pay: ₹799 to ₹999, if it clearly saves my evenings and has date, slot and advance.
- (d) Too expensive: ₹1,299 and above. ₹1,999 is not a conversation. Prices incl. or excl. GST: please tell me, 18% on ₹999 is ₹180 and that changes my answer.

**8. Reaction to the price list**

- **Which plan fits:** on features, Growth ₹999 (invoices, drafts). On budget, Starter ₹499, which fits my 120 orders at about ₹559 but gives me a board and links only. On the ad's promise, Pro ₹1,999, which I will not pay.
- **Would I pay after the trial:** only Starter or Growth, and only if date, slot and advance are there by then. If they are not, I stay on notebook plus WhatsApp and say "check back".
- **What makes me upgrade:** the assistant on my own WhatsApp, working in Marathi, for under about ₹1,299 a month in total. Also a festive month when I am drowning and need the help.
- **What makes me leave:** one botched cake order, my customers getting a message from an unknown number, messy GST invoices, or the Razorpay fees eating more than the tool saves.
- **Flat fee vs per-order fee vs % of sales:** flat fee, I trust it. A small per-order overage like ₹3 is fine, because I can see it. A percentage of sales never: my average order is ₹1,800, so even 1% is ₹18 an order and about ₹2,160 a month. That is more than the whole tool. The tiers by order quota suit fashion sellers with ₹800 orders and big volume. For me, order count is low and ticket size is high, so the quota matters less than the features locked inside each tier.

**9. Free plan or free trial? Annual discount?**

- **Trial length:** 15 days is too short. My business runs on weekends and festivals. I need at least one full month, including a busy weekend, to see if it holds. 30 days, or "first 30 orders free", would be fair.
- **Free plan:** a small free plan (say 20 orders a month, board and links only) would get me in the door, and I would pay when I want invoices and the assistant.
- **Annual:** only after 3 months of using it. 2 months free (about 17%) would tempt me, 20% off would convince me. Not before, because my cash comes in lumps (Diwali is big, June is dead).

**10. Add-ons I would actually pay for, with a price**

- Assistant on my own WhatsApp number (when it exists): ₹300 to ₹500 a month on top of a plan of about ₹800 to ₹1,000.
- Done-for-you setup (upload my 40 designs, flavours, weights, price grid and rate card): ₹1,000 to ₹1,500 one time. I would pay this happily, as I do not have the evenings to do it.
- WhatsApp message pack for festive offers: priced near Meta's rate, about ₹1.10 a message all in. A pack of 300 for about ₹350 twice a year is fine.
- Phone support in Hindi or Marathi in festive weeks: ₹200 a month, or ₹500 for a Diwali week pack.
- Removing the CartHedge branding from buyer pages: ₹150 to ₹200 a month. Buyers should see Sugar & Spoon. A tiny "powered by" in the footer is fine, a CartHedge logo on checkout is not.
- Extra staff logins: two free (me and Amit or Savita tai), ₹100 for each extra.
- Porter or Borzo booking from the order: ₹10 per booking if it saves me the rider messages. I would not pay a monthly fee for it.
- COD or RTO protection: no.

**11. Who else influences the decision, and what they would ask**

- **Amit (husband):** "What is the total, including GST and the gateway fee? Can you export the data if you stop? Who sees the customer phone numbers?" He is the one who reads the terms.
- **Joshi kaka (CA):** "Is the GST invoice proper (HSN, CGST and SGST, numbering)? Does CartHedge's own bill carry GST so you can claim it? Is FSSAI on it?"
- **Savita tai:** she packs. "Can I see tomorrow's list on one screen, in Marathi, without logging in?"
- **The Pune Home Bakers group:** "Who uses it? Did it ever send a wrong date? What does it cost after the offer?" Two of them have been burned by a tool each, so they will talk.

**12. One sentence I would say to a friend about CartHedge right now**

"It looks like a good DM bot for kurti sellers, with a cake-shaped hole in it: until it understands delivery date, slot and 50% advance, and works on my own WhatsApp in Marathi, I am watching it, not paying for it, but I will try the trial."

## Round 2 notes

- I now believe the first blocker is WhatsApp on my own number (eight other panelists share it, per their Round 1 posts), with date, slot and 50% advance second. Product file says WhatsApp will use coexistence (I keep the Business app), but it is still "coming", so I want a live demo before I move anything.
- Pricing view: tiers by order count suit low-ticket sellers; high-ticket, low-volume sellers like me, Mousumi and Shabnam need the assistant priced by features. I proposed one plan near ₹899 with replies included and a 150 order quota, a stated AI chat allowance, WhatsApp costs passed at Meta's rate itemised, and a written monthly cap with an alert at 80% of quota. Current range: bargain ₹399 to ₹599, fine up to ₹999 with date, slot and advance, ₹1,999 still a no.
- New idea from Pooja's post: a booking card sent in my name (date, slot, advance paid, balance due, FSSAI number) so buyers trust paying an advance to a home baker; buyers must never see a fee line.
- Agree with: Mousumi and Lakshmi (WhatsApp first, ₹1,999 too high), Vikram on a written cap (but I disagree that printed ₹3 overage is the old chatbot trick), Pooja on no buyer fees. Disagree with: single-price thinking; Tanya and Arjun find ₹1,999 too cheap, so a separate high-volume drop plan should carry that, not the small-seller plans.

## Round 3 notes

- Lakshmi raised the Razorpay trap: I confirmed 2.36% is ₹5,100 a month, more than the tool. Product says UPI ID option with "reported payment" alert exists, but I have not seen it tested. Until WhatsApp on my own number is live, I am not testing payment at all.
- Proposed Growth Plus at ₹899 all-in for sellers like Mousumi and me (25 to 120 orders): auto-replies, 150-order quota with cap and 80% alert, date-slot-advance as the core differentiator. Tanya and high-volume sellers need their own drop plan, not squeezed into small-seller tiers.
- Confirmed gate with Pooja: without date, slot, 50% advance, FSSAI on invoice and Marathi WhatsApp working live, I cannot use it, price is irrelevant. 30-day trial needed because my business runs on weekends and festivals. When those are shipped, I will test Razorpay and UPI, then decide on payment gateway.

## Round 4 vote

- Growth plan at ₹1,299 is my vote; add-on WhatsApp at ₹499 makes the real cost ₹1,798, inside my range. I am "maybe" on payment until I see a live demo of WhatsApp replies in Marathi with date, slot, and advance working without CartHedge's name showing to my customers.
- Safeguards matter most: the hard cap rule (bill never exceeds next plan) is what keeps me from worrying about surprise fees. The 30-day trial and ₹2 per-order overage with 80% alerts are solid. Everything I asked for in Rounds 2 and 3 (date, slot, 50% advance, FSSAI on invoice, price lock) is here; WhatsApp on own number is the gating item.
