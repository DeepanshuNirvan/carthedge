# Panelist 13: Rohit Sharma, Indore (agency / reseller channel)

## Profile

- **Who:** Rohit Sharma, 33, Indore, Madhya Pradesh. Hindi first, English for work. Runs a small digital agency that sets up WhatsApp and Instagram automation (AiSensy, Interakt, WATI) for local businesses and online sellers.
- **Business in numbers:** about 40 clients. Roughly 16 are Instagram-DM-led sellers (fashion, jewellery, home decor, food and handicrafts), 9 are local retail (sweets, namkeen, sarafa jewellers), 7 are coaching or clinics, the rest are real estate and miscellaneous. Team is Rohit, Ankit (junior, ₹14,000 a month) and Pooja (part-time freelancer for creatives and client WhatsApp support, ₹8,000 a month).
- **Income:** setup fees ₹5,000 to ₹15,000 (about 2 new clients a month, ~₹18,000), management retainers ₹2,000 to ₹5,000 on about 26 clients (~₹78,000), BSP partner commissions about ₹13,000. Around ₹1.1 lakh coming in, about ₹80,000 after staff and tools. GST registered (clients and CA firms asked for invoices).
- **What he knows:** which tools small sellers sign up for and quietly abandon, what support they phone him about at 10 pm, how AiSensy, Interakt and WATI price and what margin they give partners.
- **Would recommend CartHedge only if:** there is a real partner program (recurring commission or wholesale), a multi-client dashboard, a white-label option or referral income, and support that actually answers.
- **Mood:** curious, a bit tired of "AI" pitches, practical. Judges everything by "can I sell this to a seller and not get the 11 pm call".

## Round 1

### Stage 1. Cold first impression

Scrolling Instagram at night, I see the sponsored post: "Every DM answered. Every order closed in the chat. CartHedge replies to buyers in their language from your real catalog, collects the order and confirms it. You approve with a tap."

**What I think it is:** an AI chatbot for Instagram DMs that also takes the order. Sounds like ManyChat plus an order form plus some "AI" sticker. It only talks about DMs, so I assume Instagram, not WhatsApp. My clients live on WhatsApp, so that is my first doubt.

**Would I tap?** Yes, but as an agency owner checking a competitor or a possible tool to resell, not as a seller. A seller would scroll past unless the ad showed a real chat.

**What I'd suspect:**
- Every AI bot ad says "answers in their language". Hinglish with spelling mistakes is where these bots break. Show me.
- "From your real catalog": my sellers don't have a clean catalog. They have photos in Instagram posts. Who uploads it?
- "15 days free, no card" is normal. The real price comes after.
- No name, no team, no clients on the ad. Who is behind it and who answers when it breaks?

**What I'd need next:** pricing page, a 60 second demo video of a real Hinglish chat, whether it works on WhatsApp, and whether there is a partner page. If there is no partner page I close the tab.

These are vendor or blog numbers, I always check the BSP's own page before quoting a client.

### Stage 4. The interview

**1. In your words, what does it do? What confused you?**
It reads a seller's Instagram DMs in Hinglish, answers from the catalog, collects item, size, address and payment, shows an exact order summary, and the seller approves with a tap (or auto-confirms). Then a buyer link handles payment, tracking, returns, and the seller gets a board, GST invoices and alerts. COD gets a re-confirm step to cut RTO.
What confused me: (a) the ad says every DM answered, but auto-replies are only Pro, and only on Instagram. (b) what exactly counts as an "order" for the 100 order quota: a cancelled one, an RTO, a draft I never approved? (c) is the ₹499 plus GST or including? (d) who is it for, a seller with 20 products or 500? (e) there is no page anywhere for agencies like me.

**2. Top 3 things that excite you, ranked, and why for your business**
1. **COD re-confirmation plus the system-calculated order summary.** My fashion and jewellery clients bleed on RTO. If I can say "we cut your refusals and the app shows the rupees saved", I can charge a ₹9,000 setup for it and retain the client. Concrete, demonstrable, not AI hype.
2. **One tool instead of three I stitch together.** Today for a small seller I join AiSensy plus a Razorpay link plus a Google Sheet plus manual invoices. One workspace with orders, buyer tracking page, returns and GST invoices means less setup time for me and fewer "order kahan hai" calls reaching me.
3. **The seller stays in control.** Approve with a tap, practice chat, pause when the owner replies, handover for bargaining and complaints. My biggest fear is an AI promising a wrong price to a client's customer. These controls let me demo it to a nervous seller and sleep.

**3. Top 3 doubts, fears or deal-breakers**
1. **No agency layer = I can't sell it.** No partner program, no multi-client login, no white-label, no referral tracking. As it is, I'd be giving CartHedge free customers, and the BSPs pay me 20% recurring for the same effort. This is the deal-breaker for recommending it, not for a seller buying it.
2. **Support and reliability.** When the AI says something wrong at 2 am, or Instagram's token breaks, the seller calls me, not you. I don't know the team size, there are no reviews, and the testimonials are placeholders. I need a named person on WhatsApp, a response time in writing, and a message from you when Meta breaks something, before I tell my clients.
3. **Pricing shape and the Instagram-only gap.** The headline feature costs ₹1,999, which is more than AiSensy Basic, while ₹99 to ₹699 Instagram tools exist. Per-order overage is a surprise-bill risk for sellers who budget monthly. And half my clients do their selling on WhatsApp, where this does nothing yet.

**4. Features missing that you would need**
- **Partner program with written terms (recurring commission or wholesale price, tracked referral link or code, monthly payout with GST invoice and TDS certificate).** MUST HAVE before I pay or promote.
- **Multi-client dashboard: one login, switch between client workspaces, see orders, alerts and "needs you" chats across all clients, add Ankit and Pooja with limited roles per client, give the client their own view.** MUST HAVE before I pay.
- **Reliable partner support: named contact on WhatsApp, reply inside 2 hours Mon to Sat 10 to 7 IST, Hindi, an incident message when Meta or Instagram breaks, a status page.** MUST HAVE before I pay.
- **AI audit log and kill switch: every message the AI sent, searchable, and one tap to pause the AI on all my client workspaces.** MUST HAVE (this is how I handle "AI ne galat bola").
- **Workspace template or clone: copy tone, policies, FAQ, handover rules from one client to the next.** MUST HAVE (otherwise setup time kills my margin).
- **Catalog import that works for non-technical sellers: from Instagram posts, Shopify or Woo, or a photo ZIP plus a Google Sheet. A CSV is not enough.** MUST HAVE, because the AI is only as good as the catalog.
- **WhatsApp auto-replies on the seller's own number (on the roadmap).** MUST HAVE before I push it to most clients. I can start with the Instagram-led 16.
- **Billing control: option for the client to pay CartHedge directly, or for me to pay in bulk and invoice the client; transferable workspace if the client leaves me.** MUST HAVE.
- **White-label (my logo, my domain, no CartHedge branding, branded buyer pages).** NICE LATER, but it is what makes a retainer feel like mine, and AiSensy offers it.
- **Monthly client report I can forward under my name (orders, RTO saved, replies handled).** NICE LATER, but it justifies my ₹3,000 management fee.
- **Comment-to-DM (roadmap).** NICE LATER for the pitch, though it is what the ₹99 tools are sold on, so it wins demos.
- Google Sheet or webhook export of orders. NICE LATER.
- Hindi UI for the seller app. NICE LATER, helps older shop owners.

**5. Features you would never use**
Live selling (none of my clients does Lives). Reseller price tier for most of my clients. Label details like MRP and maker address for my service clients (coaching, clinics). GST credit notes for the many clients who are unregistered. The storefront for sellers who already have a Shopify or just want to stay on chat.

**6. What you spend today on tools, staff, agencies; how you like to pay**
My own side: staff ₹22,000 (Ankit ₹14,000, Pooja ₹8,000), tools about ₹7,000 a month (Canva, Google Workspace, Zapier/Make, domain and hosting, my own BSP demo accounts), and I put ₹10,000 to ₹15,000 into Instagram and Google ads to find new clients, separate. Clients pay their BSP directly: ₹1,000 to ₹3,200 a month, plus Meta message charges, and my management fee of ₹2,000 to ₹5,000. I pay for tools by UPI AutoPay under ₹2,000 and by company credit card for bigger ones (GST input credit). I prefer monthly. I would pay annual only if the discount is real and the client has already paid me.

**7. Price sensitivity**
For one typical Instagram seller of mine (100 to 300 orders a month, needs automatic replies):
- (a) so cheap I'd doubt it: under ₹299. At that price I assume it's a toy or runs on a risky scraper.
- (b) bargain: ₹799 to ₹999 with auto-replies included.
- (c) getting expensive but I'd still pay: ₹1,499 to ₹1,999, only for a COD-heavy seller where the meter shows ₹8,000 plus saved.
- (d) too expensive: ₹2,500 and above for a seller under 500 orders. ₹1,999 is already the ceiling.

As an agency, per client workspace wholesale (I mark up, they see the list price): (a) under ₹300, (b) ₹600 to ₹800, (c) ₹1,000 to ₹1,200, (d) ₹1,500 and above, since a seller compares with AiSensy at ₹1,500 and I need at least ₹500 margin. A flat agency platform fee that includes white-label and 10 workspaces: ₹2,999 to ₹4,999 a month is a bargain, ₹9,999 and above is too much until I have 15 live clients.

**8. React to the current price list**
- **Which plan fits:** for my Instagram sellers, only Pro does the headline job. Starter at ₹499 has no AI, so for my clients it is an order-links tool and they will compare it to a Google Form. Growth is a strange middle: it captures orders from DMs but does not reply. The jump from ₹999 to ₹1,999 is where the ad's promise sits.
- **Would I pay after the trial:** for 3 or 4 COD-heavy clients, yes, if support answers. For the rest, not at ₹1,999.
- **What makes me upgrade:** the RTO meter showing real rupees saved, and a seller hitting 100 orders. **What makes me leave:** a surprise bill, one wrong price sent to a buyer, Instagram token breaking with no one to call, silence on support, or a competitor offering 30% recurring.
- **Flat vs per-order vs %:** flat fee I trust. Per-order is acceptable only with a clear definition of "order", a notice at 80% of quota, and a cap, because sellers budget monthly. At 300 orders Starter becomes ₹499 plus ₹600 = ₹1,099, more than Growth for 500 orders, which feels like a trap. Percent of sales never, my clients would walk and so would I.

**9. Free plan or free trial? How long? Annual?**
Trial for a seller is fine, 15 days. But catalog upload and client approvals take me a week, so for agencies I'd want a 30 day partner sandbox with unlimited demo workspaces, so I'm not burning one trial per prospect. A tiny free-forever tier (say 30 orders) would also let me demo every prospect. Annual: 2 months free (about 17%) is okay for sellers. For me to push annual to my clients I'd want 20% off and pro-rata refund if they churn.

- Auto-replies on the seller's own WhatsApp number: ₹500 to ₹799 a month per client.
- Extra staff logins: free for my team seats, ₹99 to ₹149 per extra seat for client staff.

- Priority support on call and a partner manager: ₹1,500 to ₹2,000 a month for me, if it truly means a human in under 2 hours.
- Done-for-you setup and catalog upload: I do this myself and charge ₹5,000 to ₹15,000. I'd want CartHedge to leave this to partners and pay me for the setups I do, not sell setup to my clients.
- More AI replies: depends on whether there is a cap. If there is, a clear top-up per 1,000 replies, not a hidden limit. Please tell me.
- COD/RTO protection (roadmap) and courier booking: pass-through with a visible per-order fee, I'd pay ₹5 to ₹8 per protected COD order if it works.

## Round 2 notes

- Own-number WhatsApp is now my first gate, ahead of the partner program: about nine of the 16 named it. I can still start with my 16 Instagram-led clients, but I won't push CartHedge to the rest until it ships.
- COD confirm alone is weak. Devanshi's Shiprocket test (35% to 32%) means I will only sell it bundled with pincode check and a pay-online discount, and only after my own RTO numbers. It is no longer my number one excitement.
- Pricing view: flat fee only. Overage needs a written "order" definition, an alert at 80% and a hard stop unless the seller taps. Meta WhatsApp charges in a separate prepaid wallet at pass-through. AI reply count per plan must be stated, with a top-up per 1,000. Price range unchanged: seller ₹800 to ₹1,000 with auto-replies (ceiling ₹1,999); agency wholesale ₹600 to ₹1,200 per workspace; agency platform fee ₹2,999 to ₹4,999.
- Partner economics: 20% of a ₹499 plan is ₹100, so I won't push Starter. I need recurring commission on the seller's plan with GST invoice and TDS certificate, a named WhatsApp contact, and a multi-client login. Nothing in the group moved this.
- Agree with Hiren (overage trap), Vikram (surprise-bill fear, no face) and Devanshi (COD confirm needs more). Disagree with Meenal that the CA should get no commission and the client the discount, since agencies carry the support.

## Round 3 notes

- Proposed CartHedge Partner Pro (₹4,999 per month, 10 white-label workspaces, partner support SLA): multi-client login is the non-negotiable gate. Without it, I stay testing on 2 clients; with it, I push all 40.
- Defended 20% recurring commission to Meenal: agencies carry real support burden (11 pm calls, Meta incidents). GSTIN loop is clean if seller gets own GSTIN on CartHedge invoice and I invoice commission separately. This is partnership, not conflict of interest.
- Disagreed with Vikram on per-order overage: works if designed right (clear definition, 80% alert, written cap, seller manual approval instead of autopay). Both want protection, just different paths. Also accepted his closure risk and proposed 24-month capped terms instead of recurring for life.

## Round 4 vote

- Chose Brand/Partner at ₹5,999: AI in Growth fixes bait-and-switch, hard cap on overages is the safeguard that matters most, partner program now exists in writing.
- Blocker remains: partner plan vague on multi-client login scope and white-label commitment; WhatsApp on seller's own number must launch within 60 days or I cannot push to my 24 WhatsApp-led clients.
