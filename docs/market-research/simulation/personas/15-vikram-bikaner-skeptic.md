# Panelist 15: Vikram Sethi, Sethi Bhujia Bhandar, Bikaner (the burned skeptic)

## Profile

- Age 45. Owns "Sethi Bhujia Bhandar", a namkeen and sweets shop in Bikaner with an online side that ships bhujia, papad, sev and mithai boxes across India (a lot of it to Marwari families in Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Kolkata and Surat).
- Online numbers: about 200 orders a month, average order about ₹900, so roughly ₹1.8 lakh a month online. Mostly prepaid (UPI to a PhonePe business QR, some Razorpay payment links on big orders, about 15 percent COD). Diwali, Rakhi and Holi months go to 500 to 700 orders.
- Today: WhatsApp Business app on one phone, plus Instagram page (about 6,000 followers) that his son Rohan posts on. Helper Bablu (₹10,000 a month) packs boxes and also answers WhatsApp for about three hours a day. Couriers: DTDC, India Post, some Shiprocket via a nephew.
- GST: registered, under the composition scheme (CA Mohta ji says he cannot take input credit, so any software GST is a pure cost to him). FSSAI licence holder.
- Tool history (five years): Dukaan (paid a year upfront, support vanished after payment), Bikayi (app shut down or pivoted, store links died), a WhatsApp chatbot vendor (₹1,500 a month became ₹3,800 once "message credits" and "per conversation" charges appeared). Went back to WhatsApp Business and a phone.
- Fears: subscriptions that auto-grow, hidden charges, startups that shut down, data lock-in, support that goes silent after payment. Will only consider a tool with clear pricing in writing, easy data export, refund terms, a real phone number that gets answered, and proof the company will still exist in two years.
- Language: Hindi, Marwari-flavoured Hinglish ("bhai sahab", "paisa to dena hi hai par samajh ke", "chhota sa bhi charge likha hona chahiye").
- Influencers: wife Sunita (packing, kitna kharcha, customer phone numbers safe hain?), son Rohan 24 (will test it, says "Papa try karo"), CA Mohta ji (GST on the subscription, invoice in his GSTIN), helper Bablu (worried for his job), younger brother and shop partner Dinesh (money).

## Round 1

### Stage 1. Cold first impression (Instagram sponsored post)

Ye lagta hai Instagram ke liye ek aur chatbot jo DM ka jawab dega aur order le lega. "Order closed in the chat" bada claim hai. Mere customer 80 percent WhatsApp par aate hain, Instagram par kam, to pehla sawal hi ye ki ye WhatsApp par chalega ya nahi.

Would I tap? Haan, par sirf ye dekhne ke liye ki price kitna hai, aur company ka phone number aur pata kahan likha hai. Sign up nahi karunga. "15 days free, no card" achha lagta hai, kyunki card maangte to turant band kar deta. Par "no card" ke baad kya hota hai, wo main jaanta hoon: trial khatam, phir "plan lo warna store band". Dukaan ne bhi pehle aise hi pyaar se bulaya tha.

What I would suspect:
- Ad mein price nahi. Jo price chhupata hai uski pichhli baat bhi chhupi hogi.
- Koi naam, chehra, company, shehar nahi. Startup hai to kitne din chalega?
- "You approve with a tap" matlab akela AI order nahi leta, aur ye theek hai, par phir "closed in the chat" thoda zyada bol diya.

What I need to see next: pricing page with the full rupees (GST milakar), phone number jo uthaye, company ka naam aur pata, refund aur data export ki baat, aur kisi ek khane-peene wale seller ka asli review.

### Stage 2. Looking around

- Searched "CartHedge reviews / scam or genuine": nothing about CartHedge came up at all, only generic Instagram DM scam articles and other tools ([search results incl. Instagram DM scams, righttoinformation.wiki](https://righttoinformation.wiki/instagram-store-scam-india)). Matlab koi review nahi, koi purana customer nahi. Naya hai ya chhupa hua. Red flag number one for me.
- Checked what Dukaan costs now since I already know it: Starter about ₹299 and Growth about ₹999 a month, yearly saves about 35 percent, and reviews still say support is hit or miss and some sellers got hit by 4 to 5 percent charges ([Kripesh Adwani review](https://kripeshadwani.com/dukaan-review/), [SelectHub](https://www.selecthub.com/p/ecommerce-platforms/dukaan/)). So the price list of CartHedge is in the same range as Dukaan, and I already know how that story ended for me.
- Checked WhatsApp charges, because the chatbot vendor burned me on this. One blog says Meta charges about ₹0.86 per marketing message (about ₹1.02 with GST), ₹0.115 per utility message, and from 1 Oct 2026 service messages after the first 1,000 free a month also cost ₹0.115 ([EngageLab](https://www.engagelab.com/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing), [DEV Community India guide](https://dev.to/dawnofgenx/the-complete-guide-to-whatsapp-business-api-pricing-in-india-2026-1hbh)). If I message 2,500 old customers at Diwali that is about ₹2,500 in Meta charges alone. Who pays that, me or them? This is exactly where "extra charges" sneak in.
- Worry list before even opening it: auto-renewal, hidden message credits, what happens to my links if I leave, can I take my customer list out, who answers the phone.

### Stage 3. After reading product.txt and the website copy

Jo achha laga:
- Buyer ka paisa seller ke apne Razorpay mein jata hai, "CartHedge takes no cut". Ye pehli baar kisi ne seedha likha.
- "Download all store data as one file" aur "delete account, 30 din mein data erase, beech mein login karo to sab wapas". Lock-in ka darr isse kam hota hai.
- Har subscription payment par GST invoice. CA ko pasand aayega.
- Instagram par official login, password share nahi.

Jo khatka:
- Ad mein jo dikhaya (auto-reply, order close) wo plan table ke hisaab se sirf Pro ₹1,999 mein hai. Growth mein sirf "ready drafts". Ad Pro ka dikha rahe hain aur "starts at ₹499" ka mahaul bana rahe hain.
- Auto-reply abhi sirf Instagram par live hai. Meri mukhya jagah WhatsApp hai aur wo "coming next" hai. Matlab main aaj jo cheez khareedunga wo meri zaroorat ki nahi.
- "Orders above the quota are added to the next autopay charge". Ye bilkul wahi hai jo chatbot wale ne kiya. Variable autopay mera deal-breaker hai.
- After trial "the store simply pauses", aur expired par "seller APIs and the public storefront both stop". Ek din paisa late hua to mere grahak ke links mar jayenge? Diwali ke din?
- "Custom plan priced by CartHedge staff". Matlab jo marzi bolenge.
- "Cancel anytime" likha hai par refund ki ek line nahi.
- Contact page par form hai aur "we reply within a working day". Phone nahi, "Talk to a human" bhi email jaisa. Footer mein "Admin" login ka link public dikh raha hai, ajeeb lagta hai.
- Testimonials (Jaipur juttis, Hyderabad modest fashion, Rajkot jewellery) ek bhi khane wala nahi, photo nahi, number nahi. Copy file mein khud likha hai ki ye "seed fallback" hain. Agar live site par ye dikhte hain to nakli hain, aur mujhe jaise log pakad lete hain.
- RTO savings meter, COD calculator: mere liye bekaar, mera maal zyadatar prepaid hai.

### Stage 4. Interview

**1. What does it do? What confused you?**
Instagram ke DM mein customer ko jawab deta hai Hinglish mein, mere catalog se, order ki details jama karta hai, summary dikhata hai, main ek tap se approve karta hoon. Saath mein ek storefront link, board, GST invoice, return, customer ledger. Confusion: (a) ye WhatsApp par kab chalega, (b) kaun sa plan chhodkar ad wala kaam milta hai (Pro), (c) price GST ke saath hai ya alag, (d) quota ke upar ka paisa kab aur kaise katega, (e) free trial ke baad store band hone ka matlab.

**2. Top 3 excitements, ranked**
1. Buyer ka paisa seedha mere Razorpay mein, koi cut nahi, aur poora data ek file mein nikal sakta hoon. Mujhe control chahiye, ye wahi deta hai.
2. Order ka link aur tracking page. "Bhaiya parcel kahan hai" ke 15 to 20 phone roz aate hain, Bablu ka time jaata hai. Ye aaj live hai.
3. Raat ko aane wale baar baar ke sawal ("1 kilo bhujia kitne ka", "Mumbai kitne din", "ghee wali mithai hai?") ka Hindi mein jawab, WhatsApp par. Par ye tabhi jab WhatsApp par chale. Abhi isse excited hona jhooth hoga.

**3. Top 3 doubts / deal-breakers**
1. Company ka bharosa: koi chehra, pata, phone, saal, paying customer nahi dikha. Dukaan aur Bikayi mein paise gaye, ab teesri baar nahi.
2. Hidden aur variable charges: per-order fee, quota ke upar autopay mein judna, WhatsApp message ka Meta charge kaun dega, AI ka kharcha, "custom plan". Mujhe poora mahine ka maximum likha hua chahiye.
3. Mere WhatsApp number ka risk. Agar apna number automation par chalaya aur Meta ne block kiya to meri poori dukaan ka number gaya. Coexistence ka kya matlab hai, jo ghalti hui to zimmedar kaun.
(Deal-breaker bhi: payment late hone par links aur store turant band, aur support ka phone nahi.)

**4. Missing features I would need**
- WhatsApp auto-reply on my own number actually live and working, Business app phone par bana rahe: **must have before I pay**.
- Real phone support (Hindi bolne wala insaan, time likha hua, number likha hua), har paid plan mein: **must have before I pay**.
- Pricing mein sab likha: GST shamil ya alag, mahine ka upper cap, message charges ka rule, aur refund (bina chaalaaki ke, bache hue din ka paisa wapas): **must have before I pay**.
- Data export Excel/CSV mein (customers, orders, products, photos), aur kharidne se pehle test karne ko: **must have before I pay**.
- FSSAI licence number storefront, invoice aur packing slip par (khane ke seller ke liye zaroori): **must have before I pay**.
- Weight-wise shipping (250g, 500g, 1kg, 5kg ke slab, state ke hisaab se). Abhi sirf flat fee aur "free above amount" dikha, mera maal weight se bikta hai: **must have before I pay**.
- Daily order limit (kitchen ki capacity), dispatch ka din, Diwali pre-order date, aur kuch pincodes par mithai na bhejna: **must have before I pay** (limit aur dispatch date), shelf life / best before dikhana: **nice later**.
- Payment late hone par 7 din ki grace, aur band hone par bhi data aur links read-only chalte rahein: **must have before I pay**.
- Hindi seller app ya kam se kam Hindi labels, Bablu ke liye: **nice later**.
- Gift order (alag address, message likha hua, rate chhupa hua) aur office hamper ke bulk order ka B2B invoice: **nice later**.
- Tally export: **nice later**.
- Plan ko 1 to 2 mahine ke liye pause karna bina data khoye (off-season): **nice later**.

**5. Features I would never use**
RTO savings meter aur calculator, COD confirmation token (mera maal 85 percent prepaid), size chart, colour options with photos, exchange flow (khane ka saaman exchange nahi hota, bas "transit mein kharab hua" ki shikayat), live selling, story/photo matching, reseller pricing (shayad kirana ke liye kabhi, abhi nahi), "remove branding" ke paise.

**6. What I spend today, and how I pay**
Software par lagbhag ₹0 (WhatsApp Business free, Razorpay sirf jab link banta hai, 2 percent ke aaspaas). Bablu ₹10,000 mahina (jisme replies ka hissa lagbhag ₹3,000). Courier customer se ya box mein jod kar. Paying style: UPI se, har mahine haath se, jab main chahun. Autopay mein sirf fixed amount, ek limit ke saath, aur wo bhi pehle 6 mahine ke baad. Saal bhar ka advance dobara nahi dunga pehle saal mein.

**7. Price sensitivity (₹/month, flat, GST milakar, about 200 to 300 orders, WhatsApp automation actually working)**
(a) So cheap I would doubt it: below ₹199.
(b) A bargain: ₹399 to ₹599.
(c) Getting expensive but I would still pay: ₹999 to ₹1,200, only if it clearly saves Bablu's replying time.
(d) Too expensive: ₹1,500 and above for my 200 orders. Pro ₹1,999 with 2,000 orders quota is made for someone else. Wo Bablu ki aadhi tankhwah hai.
Today, with Instagram-only: I would pay ₹0 to ₹499 and that too after a month of watching.
(GST: composition mein mujhe credit nahi, to ₹999 ka matlab mere liye ₹1,179.)

**8. React to the price list**
- Starter ₹499: 200 orders par ₹499 + 100 x ₹3 = ₹799 aur GST alag, aur GST invoice isme nahi. Bekaar.
- Growth ₹999: 500 orders ka quota mere liye theek (200 normal, Diwali mein 600, overage ₹250 hi). GST invoice, offers, broadcast isme hain. Ye mere hisaab ka plan hai. Par ismein sirf "drafts" hain, auto-reply nahi.
- Pro ₹1,999: ad wala kaam yahin hai, par mere 200 orders ke liye 10 rupaye per order padta hai. Nahi.
- After trial would I pay? Aaj ki haalat mein nahi. Do shart: WhatsApp par chale, aur likhit mein cap aur refund.
- What makes me upgrade: sirf WhatsApp auto-reply jab proven ho. What makes me leave: koi bhi bina bataye charge, support ka phone na uthana, ya links band ho jaana.

## Round 2 notes

- I now believe a written cap is not enough: the overage cap must also sit inside the Razorpay autopay mandate (max amount) and there must be a "stop at limit, do not charge extra" switch. Changed my mind after Hiren's Interakt template-charge story matching my chatbot-vendor story.
- Price range unchanged: Rs 0 to 499 today (Instagram-only), Rs 999 to 1,200 flat, GST included, once WhatsApp auto-reply on my own number is proven. Composition scheme means Rs 1,999 is really Rs 2,359 to me. Trial 30 days, after Diwali (8 Nov). Free plan no, annual only after 6 months monthly.
- Disagree with Ritu (free plan: free-plan companies like Bikayi die) and Rohit (25 to 30 percent lifetime agency commission ends up in my bill and clouds his advice; one-time setup fee Rs 1,500 to 2,500 is fine). Partly disagree with Hiren on per-order fees (flat Pro is right for him; the quota-overage autopay is the trap).
- Agree with Meenal (no company name, GSTIN or address on the site; show price GST-inclusive, composition clients get no credit) and with Lakshmi and Sneha (food sellers need weight slabs, daily cap, FSSAI; flat shipping fee only). Own-number WhatsApp risk and coexistence still unproven; I will not test before Diwali.

## Round 3 notes

- Proposed Growth plan: ₹1,299 flat, 500-order quota, ₹2 per order overage but CAPPED at ₹1,499 per month with SMS alert at 80%. This works for small food sellers like me, Lakshmi, and Sneha; Hiren's Pro ₹1,999 is for 500+ baseline orders. Per-order fee without a hard cap is the chatbot trap, overage inside autopay even worse.
- Deal-breaker shift: whichever plan CartHedge picks, must include (1) named phone support with registered address (not email within-a-day), (2) 7-day grace period before links stop if payment late, (3) written FSSAI and invoice requirements for food sellers, (4) no token or overage on prepaid-only orders. Composition GST must show all-in price on the site; annual discount is optional, not forced. Trial 30 days, scheduled after Diwali (8 Nov), not "anytime".
- Will not move if WhatsApp own-number still "coming". Rohit's "named person on WhatsApp before I tell a client" is my litmus test. Also fake verified pages on free trial will destroy buyer trust for real sellers like me; need guarantee (not just label) that a CartHedge link is real, or my margin gets chewed by refusals and negative reviews from scam exposure.

## Round 4 vote

- Growth at Rs 1,299 is my plan, hard cap safeguard is right, but WhatsApp as +Rs 499 add-on makes total Rs 1,799 (Rs 2,124 GST), exceeding my budget. Voting "maybe" until WhatsApp is included or grace period for late payments is in writing.
- Phone support and data export are promised, but still text promises. The one change I need: Rs 1 line on the trust page saying "7 days read-only if payment is late, then stop"—proves they understand Diwali season and won't kill my store links.
