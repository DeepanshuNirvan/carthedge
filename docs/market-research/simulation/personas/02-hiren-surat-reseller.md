# Panelist 02: Hiren Patel, Surat (saree and dress-material supplier to resellers)

## Profile

- **Who:** Hiren Patel, 44, owner of Shree Hari Fab, Surat. Gujarati first, Hindi fluent, reads English slowly (uses Google Translate on long English pages, skips anything that looks like a legal paragraph).
- **Business:** Supplies sarees and dress material to about 900 resellers across India through 12 WhatsApp groups. Catalogue goes out as PDFs and photo dumps. Small Instagram page on the side (a few thousand followers, maybe 5 to 10 percent of orders start there).
- **Numbers:** About 1,200 orders a month, average order ₹750 (roughly ₹9 lakh a month in sales), 80% COD, about 30% RTO. His margin per order is only about ₹90 to ₹110, so RTO (forward plus reverse shipping plus packing, about ₹150 each) eats roughly ₹50,000 a month.
- **Team and process:** 3 staff (about ₹12,000 to ₹14,000 each) copy orders from WhatsApp into a paper register and then Excel. An elder brother-in-law partner handles the godown and dispatch. His CA files GST (he is registered, so he claims GST credit on tool bills).
- **Past with tools:** Tried Interakt for two months, stopped because template message charges came as a shock. Uses Shiprocket for couriers, Excel, Google Drive, Canva for catalogue covers.
- **Money style:** Cost-conscious to the paisa, negotiates everything, likes paying yearly upfront if the discount is big (30% or more), distrusts per-order fees because of his volume, will not give an auto-debit mandate to a new vendor.
- **Wants:** A separate price for resellers, margin tracking, fewer RTOs, staff doing less copying.
- **Voice:** English with Hinglish and a little Gujarati ("su chhe", "bhai", "saheb", "ketla"). Blunt, practical, thinks in rupees per month and rupees per order.

## Round 1

### Stage 1. Cold first impression (Instagram sponsored post)

My first thought: "Ye bhi koi chatbot hai Instagram ke liye." It says it answers DMs in the buyer's language from my catalog and takes the order. Okay, good for a boutique with 40 DMs at night. My orders do not come like that. My orders come from 12 WhatsApp groups and from resellers sending a photo with "ye wala 4 piece, COD, address neeche". Instagram is the small side.

Would I tap? Yes, one tap, because "15 days free, no card" costs me nothing and I am always looking for something to stop my 3 boys copying orders. But I tap with my guard up.

What I would suspect:
- "Free, no card" is how Interakt also started, and then the message charges came. After 15 days what do they ask, and what else gets billed on top?
- "In their language": do they mean Gujarati, or only Hindi and English? My resellers write in Gujarati, Hinglish and sometimes Marathi.
- "Every order closed in the chat" which chat? Instagram only? WhatsApp?
- AI "replies to buyers" is a risk. If it quotes a wrong price to a reseller, I lose money, and my resellers are touchy.
- Who is behind it? Nothing on the ad tells me. Is this a Surat/Gujarat company or a college project?

What I need to see next: the pricing page first (before any feature), a line that says WhatsApp works, Gujarati language, and one real customer who does wholesale or reselling that I can call.

### Stage 2. Looking around (before signing up)

I did only what I would actually do: search for what it costs me, and check how Meta's WhatsApp charges work, because that is what burned me.

1. **Meta's WhatsApp rates (India, 2026).** A few BSP blogs say marketing messages are about ₹0.86 each, utility and authentication about ₹0.115 each, all plus 18% GST. One site says that from 1 October 2026 even service replies get charged after the first 1,000 free per number (I have not verified this, only one blog says it, so I would ask CartHedge directly). Sources: [MyOperator](https://myoperator.com/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india-2026), [EngageLab](https://www.engagelab.com/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing), [Whautomate](https://whautomate.com/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india).
   My Interakt shock was exactly this: broadcasting to about 900 resellers many times a month. 900 messages at ₹1.02 with GST is about ₹920 per broadcast. Ten broadcasts is over ₹9,000. The tool plan fee was the small part.
2. **Alternatives and their cost.** Interakt starts about ₹999 a month for the Instagram-only plan and ₹2,799 to ₹3,799 for the proper WhatsApp plans, plus roughly 12% markup over Meta's rates. WATI is around ₹4,000 and up and charges per agent seat. A cheap Instagram-only tool called ReplyKaro says ₹99 a month. Sources: [CampaignHQ on Interakt](https://blog.campaignhq.co/interakt-pricing-india-2026), [OdoReach comparison](https://www.odoreach.com/blog/wati-vs-interakt-vs-odoreach-pricing), [ReplyKaro](https://www.replykaro.com/instagram-automation/india). These are blog claims and competitor sites, so I treat the numbers as rough.
3. **Reviews/"scam?" check.** Nothing about CartHedge in the places I looked, so no reviews either way. A new brand with zero public reviews is a yellow flag for a man who pays upfront. I would ask for a reference call.
4. **What worries me before even signing up:** WhatsApp charges (again), whether my 900 resellers' numbers are safe (my resellers are my business), and whether it works for resellers who order for their own customers (dropship), because most tools assume a retail seller and a retail buyer.

### Stage 3. After reading product.txt and the website copy

Things I noticed in the files:
- The big promise is Instagram DM automation. For WhatsApp, "automating the seller's own number" is still coming next. Broadcasts on WhatsApp also wait on that.
- Starter has no reseller pricing and no GST invoices. Those start at Growth. The Instagram auto-reply starts at Pro.
- The testimonials in the website file are marked "seed fallback only", so those are placeholders, not real customers. I read that as: no reference customers yet.
- The product definition lists future ways to earn: a take-rate on payments, a ₹3 to ₹8 spread on courier shipments, a premium on RTO protection. The website says "takes no cut". I want that written in my contract.
- The savings calculator assumes COD confirmation takes refusals down to 8%. For my kind of buyer I think 30% to 20% is believable, 8% is not.
- Language strip shows Hindi, Tamil, Bengali, Telugu, Kannada, Hinglish, English. I do not see Gujarati named anywhere.
- Account is one email and password. I see no staff logins.

### Stage 4. The interview

**1. In your words, what does it do? What confused you?**

It sits behind Instagram DMs: AI answers buyers from my catalog, collects item, size, address, shows an order summary, and I approve with a tap. Around that it gives an order board (new, confirmed, packed, shipped, delivered, RTO), a shareable store link with product, cart and custom links, a phone OTP checkout, COD confirmation, GST invoices, returns and refunds, a customer ledger, packing slips and a pick list, and an RTO "money saved" meter.

What confused me:
- Everything is told through Instagram. My orders are on WhatsApp groups. What do I actually get today for WhatsApp? Only status updates and OTPs from CartHedge's own number.
- Gujarati. Is it supported or not?
- Reseller price: how does the store know a person is my reseller before showing the price? Do I tag 900 numbers one by one?
- My resellers order for their own customers (dropship). The buyer who verifies OTP is the reseller, but the COD amount and delivery address belong to the reseller's customer. Does the ledger and COD flow understand that?
- Does an RTO or cancelled order count in the monthly quota? Does the trial include Pro?
- Is ₹1,999 plus GST or including?
- Messages "from CartHedge's number": who pays Meta's charge, and do my resellers' customers see CartHedge's name instead of mine?

**2. Top 3 things that excite you, ranked, and why for your business**

1. **One link with reseller price plus one order board.** It replaces PDF catalogue and photo dumps in 12 groups, and the order lands in the board instead of my 3 staff copying it into a register and Excel. If even half my resellers order through the link, I could let one staff go or give him dispatch work. That is about ₹13,000 a month.
2. **Bulk actions, packing slip with COD amount, and pick list.** Select 100 orders, print slips, print the pick list. At 40 to 60 orders a day this is real hours saved, and mistakes in picking drop.
3. **COD confirmation before dispatch.** My RTO bill is about ₹50,000 a month. If I cut RTO from 30% to 20% that is about 120 fewer returns, roughly ₹18,000 a month saved. That alone is more than the price. But see my doubt about dropship below.

**3. Top 3 doubts, fears or deal-breakers**

1. **WhatsApp is not live.** My business is WhatsApp. Paying full price for an Instagram bot while my real channel is "coming next" is wrong. Deal-breaker for paying yearly or paying Pro just for the AI part.
2. **Hidden or growing charges.** Meta message charges (Interakt again), per-order fee above quota during Diwali and wedding season (my orders can double), and the future "take-rate" and courier spread written in the founder's file. I need one all-in price in writing and a hard cap on WhatsApp spend.
3. **Dropship does not fit the way it is built.** Resellers do not want me to contact their customers, and they do not want my name on the parcel or the tracking message. If CartHedge sends my-branded OTP, COD confirmation and tracking to the end customer, my resellers will think I am stealing their customer and leave. Also, OTP every time for a reseller who sends 15 orders a day will kill adoption.

**4. Features missing that you would need (must have before I pay / nice later)**

Must have before I pay:
- WhatsApp on my own number (auto-reply, order capture from reseller chats). This is the product for me.
- Blind dropship: reseller's name (or none) on the packing slip, the end customer's address, COD amount to collect = reseller's selling price (not my price), messages to the end customer sent in the reseller's name or sent to the reseller instead.
- Reseller account: login once, saved address book of their customers, many orders in one go, and a reseller ledger (advance, credit, what they owe me). Margin shown to both of us (reseller's margin and mine).
- Cost price field and margin report per product and per reseller. Right now I only see price, reseller price and compare-at. Margin tracking needs cost.
- RTO per reseller: RTO rate, reverse shipping debited to their ledger, "stop COD for this reseller" switch. The ledger counts COD refusals per buyer, but my buyer is the reseller.
- Bulk import of my 900 resellers with the reseller tag, so nobody tags by hand.
- Staff logins (3 of them) with limits: they should not see my cost, margin or revenue.
- Bulk courier booking and label print, with COD remittance tally (Shiprocket, Delhivery or similar).
- Gujarati for replies (Hinglish is fine). Hindi screens for my staff.
- A written all-in price and a cap on any pass-through charges.

Nice later:
- Quantity slabs and minimum order quantity (1 pc, 6 pcs, 12 pcs rates).
- Design-number search and mass "sold out" so I can update 200 designs fast.
- Reseller one-tap photo download without my watermark, or with their own watermark, for posting on their page and status.
- Bulk order upload from a reseller's Excel sheet.
- Tally and GSTR-1 export (my CA will ask).
- Gujarati app screens (Hindi first is enough).
- Group-friendly sharing: catalogue cards in WhatsApp groups with a good link preview.

**5. Features you would never use**

Comment-to-DM, live selling, photo and story matching, the "fun" tone and emoji settings, back-in-stock waitlist, the practice chat, dropped-checkout reminders, RTO insurance and the AI insights panel. The Instagram assistant I would keep switched off for the first months.

**6. What you spend today, and how you like to pay**

- Staff: about ₹40,000 a month for 3 data entry boys (₹12,000 to ₹14,000 each).
- Tools: about ₹1,500 a month (Google Drive 200 GB, Canva, a PDF tool). Excel is already on the computers.
- Interakt: stopped after 2 months. I paid about ₹999 plus roughly ₹4,000 to ₹6,000 in message charges in the worst month.
- Courier: Shiprocket, per shipment, not a tool bill.
- How I pay: UPI or NEFT, yearly upfront if the discount is 30% or more. Monthly by UPI if no discount. No autopay with a vendor I do not know for at least 6 months. I need a GST invoice because I take input credit.

**7. Price sensitivity for the plan I would need, in ₹/month**

(Plan I need: about 1,200 orders, reseller price, GST invoices, WhatsApp working. All prices excluding GST, because I claim credit.)
- (a) So cheap I would doubt it: under ₹600 for 1,200 orders and all this.
- (b) A bargain: ₹1,200 to ₹1,500 flat, WhatsApp order updates included.
- (c) Getting expensive but I would still pay: ₹2,500 to ₹3,000, but only if WhatsApp is live and I actually remove one staff (₹13,000 saved).
- (d) Too expensive: ₹4,000 flat, or any plan where Diwali pushes the bill past ₹5,000 with per-order fees.
- For today's live features only (no WhatsApp automation): I would pay about ₹500 a month, no more.

**8. React to the current price list**

- I do the maths: Starter ₹499 plus 1,100 extra orders at ₹3 is ₹3,799. Growth ₹999 plus 700 extra at ₹2.50 is ₹2,749. Pro at ₹1,999 covers 2,000 orders, so Pro is the cheapest for me, and it is the plan that carries the Instagram AI which I barely use. The list pushes volume sellers into Pro. Fair enough, but I notice it.
- Which plan fits: Pro on quota, because reseller pricing and GST invoices start at Growth and I cross 500 orders on day 12.
- Pay after trial? Only if, during the trial, at least 30 to 50 resellers place real orders on the link and the dropship (reseller price, COD amount, no-contact) works. If resellers say "WhatsApp pe hi bolenge", I leave.
- What makes me upgrade: Diwali, wedding season (2,500 to 3,000 orders). I would want an overage cap or a seasonal bundle, not ₹2 for every extra order.
- What makes me leave: surprise Meta charges, a price rise after I commit, weak support in Hindi or Gujarati, resellers not adopting, courier or COD tally mismatch.
- Flat vs per-order vs percent of sales: **flat fee only.** Per-order fees scale against me. My margin per order is about ₹100. ₹2.50 is 2.5% of my profit on every order, including RTO orders that earn nothing. Percent of sales is a no: 1% of ₹9 lakh is ₹9,000. A flat fee I can plan, negotiate and put in my books.
- Also: price lock for 12 months in writing, and "no take-rate, no courier spread" in the agreement, since the founder's file says they are planned later.

**9. Free plan or free trial? How long? Annual discount?**

- 15 days is too short. My 900 resellers cannot be trained in 15 days. I need 30 days. A free plan with about 50 orders a month would also let me test slowly, but a longer trial suits me better.
- Annual: yes, tempting, but the discount must be big. 2 months free (about 17%) is not enough. I want about 33% (4 months free): ₹1,999 a month becomes about ₹16,000 for the year. I would start by asking 40%. And only after WhatsApp is live, plus a refund of the unused months if they do not deliver WhatsApp in 90 days.

**10. Add-ons I would actually pay for**

- **WhatsApp message packs:** prepaid wallet, real balance visible, hard cap, no surprise. Marketing messages at about ₹1.10 to ₹1.20 each including GST (Meta's own is about ₹1.02 with GST). Above ₹1.50 I walk. Utility messages (order updates, OTP) should be inside the plan.
- **Auto-replies on my own WhatsApp number:** the main reason for me to pay. I would prefer it inside Pro. If separate: about ₹500 a month on top.
- **Extra staff logins:** 3 included in the plan. Extra ones ₹100 each per month, not the WATI "seat tax" style.
- **Blind dropship / reseller white-label (their name on slip and messages):** ₹500 a month. This one protects my reseller relationships.
- **Courier booking:** no monthly fee. They can keep ₹2 a shipment only if the rate is at least ₹5 below my Shiprocket rate.
- **COD/RTO protection:** flat monthly risk scoring, about ₹500. Per-order I do not want. A real RTO guarantee that refunds shipping, I would study at about ₹4 to ₹5 per COD order, but only with a written payout rule.
- **Done-for-you setup and catalog upload:** yes. 2,000 designs plus 900 resellers imported plus staff training in Gujarati: ₹3,000 to ₹5,000 one time. Free if I take the annual plan.
- **Priority support on call in Hindi or Gujarati:** ₹500 a month. My staff will call, not email.
- **Removing CartHedge branding:** for the reseller white-label only, covered above. For my own link, I do not care.
- **More AI replies:** no.

**11. Who else influences the decision, and what they would ask**

- **My brother-in-law and partner:** "Kitna kharcha, kitne mahine me wapas, aur staff ko nikalna padega kya?"
- **CA:** GST invoice format, credit notes, how the monthly report goes into Tally, whether CartHedge's own invoice is proper for input credit.
- **Senior staff (the one who runs the register):** "Hindi ya Gujarati me hai? Excel me export hota hai? Mere pe kaam badhega ya kam?" If he hates it, nothing works.
- **Top 20 resellers who send me 40 percent of orders:** "Mera customer ko aapka message na jaye. Mera naam parcel pe. Mera bhav lock rahe." They decide whether the link ever gets used.

**12. One sentence to a friend right now**

"Instagram ka bot achha hai, par apna kaam WhatsApp pe hai, jab tak WhatsApp pe nahi chalega aur bill fix nahi hoga, tab tak free trial me bas reseller link aur order board check karunga."
