# Panelist 09: Lakshmi Narayanan, Paati's Kitchen, Chennai

## Profile

- **Who:** Lakshmi Narayanan, 52, home food seller. Brand: "Paati's Kitchen" (mango thokku, avakkai, lemon and garlic pickles, idli and sambar podis, murukku, adhirasam, Diwali sweets boxes). Cooks in her own kitchen in Mylapore, Chennai. Husband Narayanan, retired from Indian Bank, handles the bank, UPI statements and anything with the word "KYC". Son Karthik, 24, works at an IT company in Siruseri, runs the Instagram page on weekends. Daughter Divya lives in Singapore and is the reason half the NRI families found her. Kamala akka (₹7,000 a month) and Selvi (₹6,000 a month, mornings only) help with cooking, jarring and packing.
- **Languages:** Tamil at home and with most customers, English for NRI families and anyone outside Tamil Nadu. Types Tamil in English letters ("oru kg mango thokku venum, Coimbatore ku anupa mudiyuma?"). Older customers send voice notes in Tamil, and she answers them the same way.
- **Business in numbers:** about 150 orders a month, average order ₹700, roughly ₹1.05 lakh a month in sales. Peak months (Diwali, Pongal, Tamil New Year, mango pickle season April to June) go to 300 to 400 orders. Prepaid only, no COD, because it is food and a refused parcel is a dead parcel. Udyam and FSSAI registered, not GST registered (turnover under the limit), CA is paid about ₹5,000 a year.
- **How orders arrive:** about 85% on WhatsApp (her number is printed on every jar label), about 10% from Instagram (Karthik), the rest phone calls. Many repeat buyers. A big share of them are Chennai families buying for children abroad, parcels sent to India addresses or sometimes to a cousin in Bengaluru or Hyderabad.
- **How it works today:** customer messages "1 kg mango thokku, 500g garlic pickle, 2 podi, Tirunelveli 627001". She works out the price from memory and a paper rate card, works out the courier charge by weight and pincode from ST Courier and DTDC slabs, replies with a total and her PhonePe for Business QR. Customer pays and sends a screenshot, she checks the bank SMS, notes it in a notebook, Kamala akka packs, Karthik or her husband books the courier on Saturday. Customers pay the courier charge. Courier is ST Courier for Tamil Nadu and south India, DTDC for the rest of India.
- **Her evenings:** about 3 hours a day on WhatsApp (answering the same "price enna", "engey order", "Coimbatore ku courier evlo" questions, typing totals, chasing screenshots). Messages from the US and UK come in at 2 to 4 a.m. her time and are answered at 7 a.m., and she suspects some of those buyers order from someone else in between.
- **Tools and money today:** free WhatsApp Business app (greeting, away message, labels, 6 quick replies), PhonePe for Business QR (free), a paper rate card, a notebook, a Google Sheet Karthik keeps. Total spend on software: about ₹0. Real costs: Kamala akka and Selvi ₹13,000 a month together, CA ₹5,000 a year, jars, labels and packaging.
- **Budget:** thinks in terms of "one helper's four days". Will pay about ₹500 to ₹1,000 a month for something that clearly removes the WhatsApp typing and does not lose an order. Pays by UPI only. Dislikes cards and does not keep one linked to anything.
- **Temperament:** warm, sharp with numbers, patient but not gullible. Has seen a "free WhatsApp bulk sender" that Karthik's friend sold her once, got her number reported and cost her a week of messages. Judges a tool by: "will it embarrass me in front of my customers, and will my number be safe?" Trusts voices she can call on the phone.

## Round 1

### Stage 1. Cold first impression (the sponsored post)

I am on my phone after lunch, going through Karthik's Instagram page to see what he posted about the new avakkai, and this advertisement comes between two reels: "Every DM answered. Every order closed in the chat."

**What I think it is:** some machine that answers customers' messages and takes the order. "DM" I know, that is Instagram messages, Karthik says it often. "Closed in the chat" I read as WhatsApp chat, because that is where my orders are. "In their language", I want to know which languages. "From your real catalog", I do not have a catalog, I have a paper rate card and the small WhatsApp Business catalog that Karthik made once. "You approve with a tap" is good, nothing goes out without me seeing it.

**Would I tap:** yes, I would tap. "15 days free, no card" decides it, and the first line is exactly my 7 a.m. problem. I would not enter anything, I would just look, and then I would show Karthik in the evening. If it asked for a card I would not go further.

**What I would suspect:**
- It is for Instagram only and for people selling clothes. Everything on Instagram is clothes.
- A scam. These days every advertisement with "free trial" is either a scam or a subscription that goes on and on. I do not know this company name.
- A machine speaking to my customers in my name. If it says "yes, will reach before Diwali" or gets the weight wrong, I am the one who answers to the aunty in Adyar.
- Tamil. Hindi and English they will manage, but my people write "eppo varum", "enna rate", "anuppidunga".
- Who is behind it. A company name, a Chennai address, a phone number I can call.

**What I need to see next:** one line saying "WhatsApp", one real Tamil conversation, the price in rupees, and who to call.

### Stage 2. Looking around

I did not look for long. Karthik was sitting next to me, so we did three searches.

1. **"CartHedge reviews".** Nothing. Not one review. Search shows other tools (Interakt, others) but no mention of this name. That is not a scam sign by itself, it only means it is new, but I cannot ask any other seller. [Search results](https://www.spurnow.com/en/blogs/best-whatsapp-api-provider-india). One result was a Meta announcement from May this year, "Business AI on WhatsApp for small businesses in India" ([about.fb.com](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/05/introducing-business-ai-on-whatsapp-for-small-businesses-in-india/)). I did not read all of it. Karthik says Meta's own thing might answer customers for free or cheaply, and that he will read it properly. So my first question to the founder is: why should I pay you when Meta itself is doing this on WhatsApp?
2. **WATI and Interakt prices.** Karthik says these are what the "WhatsApp API" people use. WATI Growth is about ₹2,499 a month, Interakt starts around ₹999 a month, and both also add a small charge on every conversation on top ([comparison](https://dev.to/dawnofgenx/whatsapp-business-api-vs-wati-vs-interakt-vs-aisensy-what-should-an-indian-smb-actually-choose-3i8b), [WATI vs Interakt](https://xobito.com/blog/wati-vs-interakt-complete-comparison-for-2026)). I stopped there. For me ₹2,499 is out. ₹999 is the number I measure everything against.
3. **Meta's WhatsApp charges.** The rate card in India is about ₹0.86 for a marketing message (like a "Diwali sweets are open" broadcast) and about ₹0.12 to ₹0.15 for an order-update message, all plus 18% GST, and the pages say that from 1 October this year even replies are charged after the first 1,000 free each month ([rate card summary](https://whautomate.com/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india), [AiSensy](https://aisensy.com/pricing)). Different pages show ₹0.115 and ₹0.145 for the same thing, so I will not quote either. My own math: 150 orders and 5 updates each is about 750 small messages, a couple of hundred rupees at most. A festival broadcast to 600 old customers is about ₹600. That I can live with. What I will not accept is the tool taking ₹3 or ₹4 per message.

Other things I already know: my friend Revathi (sweets, Anna Nagar) uses only the free WhatsApp Business app with quick replies and says it is enough. Karthik says "the free app cannot take orders by itself, Amma, that is the difference". I also worried about my number, because once my number goes on the "API", I hear the Business app on my phone stops working. That I will ask.

### Stage 3. What I learned from the product and the website

I read the product definition and the website words. Honest summary, in my own words:

**What matches me:**
- Options per product with their own price and stock. So "Mango thokku" with 250g, 500g, 1 kg each having a price is supported. Good.
- The order summary is worked out by the system, "not the AI". I like this a lot. My biggest mistakes are in my own arithmetic (once I undercharged courier on a 5 kg parcel to Bengaluru, lost ₹180).
- A pick list for many orders and packing slips with address. In Diwali Kamala akka and Selvi can pack from a printed list instead of me reading out from the notebook.
- Repeat buyers: "last address offered", "ek aur chahiye" type reorder. My NRI families order the same parcel again and again to the same house.
- UPI intent that opens GPay or PhonePe. Good, no cards. There is also a "UPI ID" setting and a "reported UPI payment" alert, so maybe I can use my own PhonePe without Razorpay (to ask).
- Practice chat in settings where I can pretend to be a customer. I would test in Tamil before any real customer sees it.
- Tamil is shown on the website ("இது எவ்வளவு?"), and "regional scripts" are claimed. Nothing about Tanglish or voice notes, and my customers use both.
- Autopay with UPI AutoPay. I like that kind of payment.
- Data export in one file and erase on request. I keep my customer list as my asset, so good.

**What does not match me or is not ready:**
- **WhatsApp on my own number is "coming next", not live.** Today only Instagram works. My orders are on WhatsApp. Today the product does not do the main thing I want. Order updates and OTPs go out from "CartHedge's own WhatsApp number", so my customers would get a message from a number they do not know, not from Paati's number printed on the jar. For older aunties that looks like fraud.
- **The AI auto-reply is only in Pro at ₹1,999.** Growth at ₹999 has "AI order capture from DMs" which becomes drafts. For 150 orders a month I would be paying for a 2,000-order plan. Per order that is about ₹13.
- **Shipping is a flat fee, and "free delivery above an amount".** I see a pincode check (valid pincode, serviceable) but no weight slabs, no zones. My courier charge depends on total weight and where it goes. This is half of my work.
- **Stock is per variant.** I cook in batches, e.g. 40 kg of mango thokku. A 1 kg jar uses four 250g worth. I would have to count separately.
- **Everything is about COD and RTO and kurtis.** The calculator, the money savings meter, the testimonials, the reseller price. For a prepaid food seller this is the wrong conversation, and I felt that the product was not made with me in mind. The core loop might still work for me.
- **Razorpay.** Buyer money goes to "your own Razorpay account". I do not have one. Narayanan will ask about KYC, FSSAI, website URL and what Razorpay keeps per payment. I believe around 2% but I have not checked it. CartHedge "takes no cut", but if Razorpay takes 2% on ₹1 lakh that is ₹2,000, more than the plan itself.
- **Courier:** "courier handoff" is Pro and is for aggregators. ST Courier is a regional courier. I do not think they are on an aggregator. I need to enter the tracking number myself.
- **15-day trial.** With Diwali in early November, a trial now would run in my busiest weeks, while the WhatsApp piece is not even ready.
- **Broadcasts** need customers who have "opted in". My 600 old customers saved my number years ago but never replied "START". The message to ask them for consent is itself a message. How do I do that?
- **Festival rush:** nothing for "last order date for Diwali delivery", daily cap (I can pack 60 parcels a day, not 100), or a closed banner. "The assistant never invents dates", so it will hand over any delivery question to me, which is what I do not want during Diwali.
- **Returns and exchange flow:** food is not returned. I only need "jar leaked in the courier, send photo, I will resend" in a simple way.

### Stage 4. The interview

**1. In your words, what does it do? What confused you?**
It is a helper that sits behind your chat. A customer writes, it answers from your price list in the customer's language, gets the item, weight, name, address and then the payment, shows the customer one exact summary, and the order comes to me as a card that I approve with one tap. Then the customer gets a link to pay and track, and every status update goes in the same chat. Plus a board for all orders, packing slips, GST invoices, and numbers. What confused me: first, "DM" and "chat", which means Instagram and which means WhatsApp. It took me a while to understand that WhatsApp is "next" and only Instagram is "today". Second, the plans: "AI order capture" in Growth and "AI sales assistant" in Pro, and I do not clearly see which one answers customers by itself without me. Third, "Razorpay in your name", do I need to open a new account. Fourth, does a repeat order from the same customer count as another "order" for the 100 or 500 limit.

**2. Top 3 things that excite you, ranked, and why for your business.**
1. Orders taken on my own WhatsApp number while I sleep or cook, in Tamil and English (when it is live). My NRI customers message in US and UK daytime, which is my midnight. This is real money lost now. It also gives me back my 3 hours.
2. Exact order summary with weight prices and total worked out by the system. Fewer mistakes, fewer "madam you charged me wrong" arguments, no more screenshots of payments to chase. If shipping by weight is added, it is the best thing in the whole product for me.
3. Festival rush tools: pick list, packing slips, bulk status change for up to 100 orders, repeat address autofill. In Diwali I do 300 to 400 orders in three weeks and today I am writing parcels from a notebook at 11 p.m.

**3. Top 3 doubts, fears or deal-breakers.**
1. My WhatsApp number. It is on every label. If setup moves it onto an API and my Business app and chat history stop working, or if Meta blocks the number for sending too many messages, I lose my whole business in one morning. Deal-breaker: I must keep using my phone app on the same number, and I must be told clearly what can go wrong.
2. The machine saying something wrong to my customers in Tamil or promising a date. Food, festival, relatives abroad waiting for a box on Diwali day. One wrong "will reach on time" and I lose a family for ten years. Deal-breaker: no promise on dates or availability without my rule, and I must see everything and be able to take over in one tap.
3. Value and trust. The thing I need is on the most expensive plan, the thing I need most is not built, it is a new company with no reviews, and it needs Razorpay, which has its own charges. Also messages going out from an unknown CartHedge number. Who do I phone if something breaks on a Diwali Sunday, and will that person speak Tamil?

**4. Features missing that you would need (specific to your business). Mark each "must have before I pay" or "nice later".**
- WhatsApp auto-replies on my own number, with the Business app still working on my phone: **must have before I pay.**
- Shipping charge by total weight and destination (Tamil Nadu, South, rest of India, a slab table I fill in from ST Courier and DTDC rate cards), added to the order summary: **must have before I pay.**
- Tamil, and Tamil in English letters, understood and replied properly; plus voice notes turned into text: **must have before I pay** (at least Tanglish; voice notes can be a second step but half my older customers use them).
- Festival controls: last-order date for Diwali delivery, daily order cap, "closed for today" message: **must have before I pay** (a simple cut-off date and cap is enough).
- Deliver to a different address than the buyer, with a gift message ("from Divya, Singapore"): **must have before I pay.**
- Enter ST Courier or DTDC tracking number by hand and send it to the buyer on WhatsApp: **must have before I pay.**
- Messages to customers from my own number, not from a CartHedge number: **must have before I pay** (otherwise the order updates look like spam).
- A UPI option that works with my own PhonePe QR or UPI ID without opening a Razorpay account, with a simple "I have received payment" tap: **must have before I pay**, or else clear Razorpay help.
- One stock pool in grams across 250g, 500g and 1 kg: **nice later.**
- Shelf life, "best before" and batch line on the product page and packing slip: **nice later.**
- Import my old customers and ask them to opt in properly before the first festival broadcast: **nice later** (but I will want it by Pongal).
- Pause the plan in slow months, or pay only for the busy months: **nice later.**
- Abroad: shipping and payment for NRIs themselves: **nice later.**
- Tamil screens in the app: **nice later.**

**5. Features you would never use.**
COD confirmation and the RTO savings meter (I do not do COD at all), reseller pricing, GST credit notes and invoices for now (not GST registered), returns and exchange flow (food is not taken back), live selling, dropped-checkout reminders (my customers do not browse, they message), comment-to-DM, and the "AI insights" panel. Instagram features I will leave to Karthik.

**6. What you spend today on tools, apps, staff or agencies for this work (rough ₹ per month), and how you like to pay.**
Tools: ₹0. WhatsApp Business app is free, PhonePe for Business QR is free. Staff: Kamala akka ₹7,000 and Selvi ₹6,000, about ₹13,000 a month, and that is for cooking and packing, not messages. CA about ₹400 a month. Karthik helps on weekends for free. Instagram boost: ₹500 once, never again. I pay by UPI only. UPI AutoPay I accept if I can see the limit and cancel from my own app. Cards I dislike and I will not keep one on file. Yearly upfront by UPI only after I have used it for 3 months.

**7. Price sensitivity for the plan you'd need, in ₹/month (all-in, GST included, for WhatsApp auto-replies and about 150 to 400 orders a month).**
(a) So cheap I would doubt it: below ₹200.
(b) A bargain: ₹500 to ₹700.
(c) Getting expensive but I would still pay: ₹1,200 to ₹1,500, and only if it handled most of my chats on its own through the festival months.
(d) Too expensive: ₹2,000 and above for every month. That is more than Kamala akka's wages for 8 days, for a thing that does not pack a single jar.
For Instagram-only, as it is today, I would pay ₹0 to ₹300, and honestly only if Karthik asks for it.

**8. React to the current price list.**
Which plan fits me: by volume Starter or Growth (150 orders). By features Pro (auto-reply). I want the AI replies, but the 2,000-order quota is made for someone with ten times my orders. Starter with 150 orders is ₹499 + 50 × ₹3 = ₹649, no AI. Growth is ₹999 and the AI only makes drafts. Pro is ₹1,999. I would like a plan with the auto-reply and a small quota, for ₹999 including GST, then I could go to Pro in Diwali.
Pay after the trial: only if WhatsApp on my own number is live and I have tested it with Tamil and my own customers. Otherwise no, not one rupee, I would go back to my free app.
What makes me upgrade: festival months crossing the quota, auto-reply on WhatsApp, broadcasts to old customers.
What makes me leave: one wrong price or weight reaching a customer, my number in trouble, a Diwali Sunday with nobody to phone, a price rise after I get used to it, or Meta's own free thing doing the same job.
Flat fee vs per-order fee vs % of sales: flat fee, I trust it, I can plan. Per-order fee is acceptable if it is small (₹2 to ₹3) but in the festival my bill becomes a surprise, so I would like a cap. A percent of sales never: my profit is about 35%, so 2% of sales is nearly 6% of my profit and it grows exactly when I work hardest.

**9. Free plan or free trial? How long? Annual discount?**
A free tiny plan (say 30 orders a month) is the honest way for me, because I could try with my regular customers. If it must be a trial, 15 days is too short, and not before Diwali. 30 days, starting from the day WhatsApp is connected, not from sign-up. Annual: yes after 3 months of use, and 2 months free (about 17%), 20% off would make me do it. Paid by UPI. And money back if Meta blocks my number.

**10. Add-ons you would actually pay for, with a price.**
- WhatsApp festival broadcast messages: about ₹1 to ₹1.25 each including GST (the Meta rate is about ₹0.86 plus GST). 600 customers, 4 times a year, yes. More than ₹1.5 a message, no.
- Auto-replies on my own WhatsApp number: should be inside the plan. If it is an add-on, ₹300 to ₹500 a month on top of a ₹499 plan, not more.
- Done-for-you setup (35 products with 3 weights each, shipping table, Tamil replies tuned with my words): ₹1,500 to ₹2,500 once, yes, and I would want it before Diwali.
- Priority phone support in Tamil: ₹200 to ₹300 a month, yes, especially in festival months.
- Courier booking with ST Courier or DTDC rates: ₹3 to ₹5 per parcel, nice later.
- Extra staff logins: Karthik and my husband should be free, up to 3 logins.
- Remove CartHedge branding: ₹0, I do not care.
- COD and RTO protection: no.
- More AI replies: only if no surprise. I want a clear number up front, not a "your replies are over" message in the middle of Diwali.

**11. Who else influences the decision and what they'd ask.**
- Narayanan (husband): "Who are these people? Where is the office? What does Razorpay take? If the company shuts down, what happens to my customers' list?" He decides about anything with a bank account.
- Karthik (son): "Amma, why pay when Meta is giving a similar thing? Is the Business app safe on the same number? Is the API on the official Meta route?" He will test the whole thing before I touch it.
- CA Subramanian: "Are you crossing the GST limit? Does the software issue the right invoice if you register next year?"
- Divya (daughter, Singapore): "Will my friends in Singapore be able to order and pay, and will the OTP reach a foreign number?"
- Kamala akka and Selvi: they will only use a printed list, no app, no screen. If the pick list is not simple, they will go back to the notebook.
- Revathi and my home-chef WhatsApp group: they will ask "did it cost you a customer?" before they ask anything else.

**12. One sentence you'd say to a friend about CartHedge right now.**
"It could take my WhatsApp orders in Tamil while I sleep, but today it only works on Instagram and does not know how to charge courier by weight, so I will wait and watch till it works on my own number."

I will push for auto-reply with a small quota, counted in orders and never in replies, at about ₹999 all-in. Still no rupee until WhatsApp works on my own number; product text says Meta coexistence will keep my Business app working, but nobody here has proven it on an old number.
- **New fear from Meenal:** where my customers' phone numbers, addresses and chats go when the AI reads them (NRI families, Divya's Singapore friends). A written data note is now "must have before I pay"; Narayanan will ask for it too.
- **Pricing stance:** flat fee best. Per-order fee only with a cap I set, an alert at 80% and the cap on the invoice (Starter would swing from ₹499 to about ₹1,399 in Diwali). Meta WhatsApp charges passed at cost plus GST, no markup, estimate shown before each broadcast. Quote all-in with GST, since I am not GST registered and cannot claim credit. Range unchanged: ₹500 to ₹1,000 a month is comfortable, up to ₹1,200 to ₹1,500 only if it handles most chats through festivals; annual only after 3 months and one festival, UPI only.
- **Open question on payments:** Razorpay's about 2% (roughly ₹2,100 a month on ₹1.05 lakh) is more than the plan; the product's UPI ID setting plus "reported UPI payment" alert might let me use my own PhonePe, to confirm with the founder.
- **Agree with:** Vikram (overage on autopay is the old chatbot game, wants cap in writing), Sneha (Razorpay "no cut" is not the full story), Hiren on Meta charges being the shock, but disagree with his "GST extra is fine". Rohit: would hire a partner for Tamil tuning and setup at about ₹2,000 once.

## Round 3 notes

- **Concrete plan I would design:** 150 orders base + auto-reply + 100 AI chats per month at ₹999 all-in with GST, with a Diwali bump to ₹1,499 for 300 orders. Per-order fees are acceptable if capped in writing, with alert at 80%, cap shown on invoice, and hard stop. The trap is silent autopay overages, not the fee itself.
- **Feature that would make me cancel:** (1) WhatsApp own-number coexistence not proven live on old numbers before I join, or (2) Razorpay 2% is not replaceable by the UPI ID setting with my own PhonePe. If Razorpay is locked in, the effective cost makes every plan too expensive. These two are deal-breakers.
- **Disagree with:** Rohit's 25 to 30% lifetime commission (his margin rides my bill, and if CartHedge shuts down my commission rents to nothing), and Hiren's "GST extra is fine" (I am not registered, so 18% is real money, not tax planning). Change of mind: I accept per-order cap if written down and hard-capped in Razorpay mandate, not paper.
