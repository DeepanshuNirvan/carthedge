# Panelist 07: Kabir Khanna, SoleStreet.del (Delhi)

## Profile

- 23, Delhi. Runs "SoleStreet.del", a sneaker and streetwear reseller, 60k Instagram followers.
- Sells in drops on Instagram. About 400 orders a month in bursts, average order ₹4,500 (roughly ₹18 lakh GMV a month). Mostly prepaid UPI, almost no COD.
- During a drop he gets 500+ DMs in an hour and loses sales because he cannot reply fast enough.
- Today: a ManyChat-style comment-to-DM tool (paid in dollars, on his card) and a Google Form for orders.
- Needs speed, exact stock per size, waitlists, raffles, comment-to-DM.
- Tech-savvy, pays by card, will pay ₹1,000 to ₹3,000 a month if it clearly saves drop-day sales, hates clunky UI. Speaks Hinglish and Gen-Z slang.
- Assumed colour for the panel (not from the brief): a friend/partner (Aman) packs and ships, two college juniors help with DMs on drop days, the family CA handles GST.

## Round 1

### Stage 1. Cold first impression (the sponsored post)

Scrolling reels, ad pops up: "Every DM answered. Every order closed in the chat." My first thought: ManyChat with AI stuck on top, aimed at Indian sellers. It replies to people, takes the order, confirms it, and I just "approve with a tap". Okay, that is basically what I need on drop night, so I stop scrolling.

Would I tap? Yes. 15 days free and no card means zero risk, and I am already shopping for something better than my dollar-priced tool. I would tap on the free trial, not on the claim.

What I suspect:
- "Every DM answered" is a big line. Every founder says it. At 500 DMs an hour, does it actually hold up, or does it die at 50?
- "In their language from your real catalog" sounds like it was built for kurti and saree sellers. Do sneakers with UK/US/EU sizes and limited pairs work, or only "pink wali kurti"?
- Instagram might ban or restrict my account if a bot goes wild. I have 60k followers, I cannot risk that.
- Nothing about comment-to-DM, which is what I actually use daily.
- It is an ad, so there is a budget behind it, but I have never heard of CartHedge. No reviews in my feed.

What I need to see next: a real demo of a drop (many DMs, last pair of a size, UPI link in the chat), pricing, whether comment-to-DM and waitlists exist, and who is behind it.

### Stage 2. Looking around like a real person

What I checked, short version:
- **ManyChat (what I pay now):** new accounts see Pro at $39/month (or $29 billed yearly) for 2,500 contacts, extra contacts cost more. With dollar conversion, card forex markup and GST it comes to about ₹3,000+ on my statement. Source: [Manychat pricing 2026](https://www.featurebase.app/blog/manychat-pricing) and [Chatarmin on the March 2026 change](https://chatarmin.com/en/blog/manychat-pricing).
- **Indian comment-to-DM tools:** these are cheap, ReplyKaro from ₹99, FlowDM ₹299 flat with UPI, QuickDM free and ₹399 Pro, LinktoDM from ₹699, Linqin from ₹499. So "comment-to-DM" alone is a commodity. CartHedge has to win on the order desk and stock, not on that. Sources: [FlowDM](https://flowdm.in/instagram-dm-automation-india), [Best DM automation tools in India](https://whosedm.com/blog/best-instagram-dm-automation-tools-in-india-2026-honest-comparison), [ReplyKaro](https://www.replykaro.com/instagram-automation/india).
- **Instagram DM limits:** this is my real worry. Some sources say automated DMs are capped at 200 per hour per account, which would queue the rest of my 500 DMs, and another says Meta documents 100 messages per second and private replies to comments 750 an hour, with 200/hour being vendor-side pacing. They contradict each other, so I want CartHedge to tell me straight. Sources: [Instagram DM rate limits explained](https://instantdm.com/blog/instagram-rate-limit-2026-5000-dms-per-hour), [Spur on DM automation rules](https://www.spurnow.com/en/blogs/instagram-dm-automation-rules), [Creatorflow on API limits](https://creatorflow.so/blog/instagram-api-rate-limits-explained/). Also the 24-hour window: after 24 hours since the buyer's last message I cannot DM them, which matters for waitlists and raffle results. Source: [24-hour window guide](https://www.keyapi.ai/blog/instagram-messaging-api-policy/).
- **WhatsApp charges (for drop alerts to past buyers):** Meta bills about ₹0.86 per marketing message and ₹0.115 per utility message, plus 18% GST. 3,000 past buyers would be roughly ₹3,000 per blast. Source: [WhatsApp API pricing India 2026](https://whautomate.com/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india) and [DEV guide](https://dev.to/dawnofgenx/the-complete-guide-to-whatsapp-business-api-pricing-in-india-2026-1hbh).
- **Reviews of CartHedge:** I searched, nothing. No reviews, not on any "best Instagram DM tool in India" list. That does not mean bad, it means new, so I treat it as unproven.
- Alternative of hiring someone: I already pay two juniors on drop days, and they cannot reply to 500 DMs either.

### Stage 3. What I learned from the product files

Read product.txt and the website copy. Sorting live versus coming, fairly:

Live and relevant to me: Instagram DM assistant with auto-reply, order capture, order summary in chat, auto-confirm switch, handover; options per product (size) with their own stock and SKU, sold out at zero, stock back on received returns; order board with bulk actions (up to 100), packing slips and a pick list by item and size; prepaid payment link in chat via my own Razorpay (no cut); GST invoices; returns and exchanges (a one-tap size exchange is a big deal in sneakers); customer ledger; insights; alerts on WhatsApp; back-in-stock waitlist (Pro).

Not live: comment-to-DM, live selling, photo and story matching, auto-replies on my own WhatsApp number, WhatsApp broadcasts. No raffle anywhere, not even on the roadmap. Nothing about stock hold during payment, scheduled drop go-live, or per-buyer purchase limits.

Plan reading: Growth (₹999) only creates drafts from DMs. Automatic DM replies, in-chat summary and auto-confirm are in Pro (₹1,999), plus courier handoff and waitlists. So for me Pro is the only plan that does what the ad says.

### Stage 4. The interview

**1. In your words, what does it do? What confused you?**
It is a chatbot plus order desk plus mini storefront for Instagram sellers. A buyer DMs "9 UK hai? price?", the AI answers from my catalog, takes name, address and payment, shows an exact summary, and the order lands on a board. I approve or it auto-confirms. Then the buyer gets a link to pay and track.
What confused me: (a) "approve with a tap" by default, because at 500 DMs I cannot tap 200 times, I would run auto-confirm on, so how does prepaid payment and stock hold work then? (b) is stock reserved from the moment the buyer says "haan" or only when they pay? (c) do unpaid drafts count towards my 500 or 2,000 order quota? (d) the site is full of kurtis and COD and RTO, which are not my world.

**2. Top 3 things that excite you, ranked, and why for my business**
1. AI replies with exact stock per size, in seconds, and puts the UPI link in the chat. This is where I lose money. If I lose even 30 orders a drop at ₹4,500, that is ₹1.35 lakh per drop. Everything else is secondary.
2. Order board with bulk packing slips and a pick list by size. After a drop I pack 100+ pairs, and I currently do it from a Google Form and screenshots. A pick list by size would save Aman hours.
3. Prepaid via my own Razorpay with auto-confirm on payment, no screenshot checking, no cut of my sales. Plus one-tap size exchange and GST invoices, which would be the honest runner-up because sneaker returns are mostly wrong size.

**3. Top 3 doubts, fears or deal-breakers**
1. Drop-day scale and overselling. 500+ DMs in an hour, Instagram sending limits, AI response time, and two buyers saying "haan" for the last pair of size 9. One oversell on a hyped drop and I eat refunds plus angry comments. Deal-breaker if there is no proper stock hold with a timer.
2. Comment-to-DM, waitlist alerts and raffles are not live. That is half my drop. Until then I keep ManyChat, and I will not pay for two tools for long. Also, can two tools coexist on one Instagram account without fighting over messages?
3. Trust and AI mistakes: wrong UK/US/EU size conversion, saying "legit" or "box with bill" when I did not say it, or Instagram flagging my account. And a brand-new company with zero reviews running my whole drop night. If it is down at 8 pm during a drop, I lose a day's sales. I want an uptime promise and a status page.

**4. Features missing that I would need**
- Comment-to-DM with keyword triggers ("comment SIZE"): **must have before I pay** (really, before I cancel ManyChat).
- Stock hold with timer and auto release during payment, per size, with no oversell under load: **must have before I pay**.
- Proof of throughput: a clear answer on how 500+ DMs in an hour get answered, and a drop-day queue view: **must have before I pay**.
- Waitlist that actually notifies, on WhatsApp since Instagram's 24-hour window blocks late DMs: **must have before I pay** (otherwise waitlist is just a list of numbers).
- Bulk approve or auto-confirm for prepaid only, so I am not tapping 200 times: **must have before I pay**.
- Per-buyer limit of one pair per phone or address for hyped drops (resellers and bots hoard): **must have before I pay** for hyped releases, nice for normal ones.
- Raffle mode (pick winners from comments or entries, follow-gate): **nice later**, but I would pay for it.
- Scheduled drop go-live (stock and replies open at 8:00 pm sharp) and "only 2 left" in replies: **nice later**, I can flip stock manually at first.
- Size chart with UK/US/EU conversion built in: **nice later**, the assistant must not guess sizes though.
- Staff logins with roles (my two juniors): **nice later**, but I need 3 logins at least.
- Shiprocket or Delhivery label export for bulk upload: **nice later**, CSV export is enough to start.
- A slang tone ("bro", "fire", streetwear voice) instead of only polite, warm or fun: **nice later**.

**5. Features I would never use**
COD confirmation flow and the RTO savings meter (I barely have COD), the reseller price tier, custom made-to-order links, regional language replies (my buyers type English and Hinglish), and "aap or tum" settings. Fine that they exist, but the marketing leans on them and I do not care.

**6. What I spend today**
- ManyChat-style tool: about ₹3,000 a month on my card (dollars, forex markup and GST).
- Google Form: free. Razorpay and UPI fees on payments, which I will pay anyway.
- Two juniors helping with DMs on drop days: roughly ₹4,000 a month (about ₹500 per drop each, 3 to 4 drops).
- Billing and GST invoices through my CA's setup: around ₹300 a month, not sure of the exact number.
- Total tooling plus DM help: about ₹7,000 to ₹7,500 a month.
How I like to pay: card with autopay, monthly at first. UPI AutoPay is fine too. Yearly upfront only after it has survived a few drops. I want a proper GST invoice with my GSTIN, because I take input credit, so GST on top is not a big deal for me, but show it clearly.

**7. Price sensitivity for the plan I would need (Pro-type, with auto-replies and comment-to-DM)**
- (a) So cheap I would doubt it: under ₹700 a month. ₹99 to ₹299 tools exist, but those are dumb auto-DM bots. An AI plus order desk at that price means it will not survive a drop.
- (b) A bargain: ₹1,500 to ₹2,000.
- (c) Getting expensive but I would still pay: ₹2,500 to ₹3,500, but only after it has proven itself on a hyped drop.
- (d) Too expensive: ₹4,500 flat and above. Anything as a percentage of sales is out.

**8. Reaction to the current price list**
Pro at ₹1,999 is the plan for me, since only Pro has auto-replies, in-chat summary and auto-confirm, waitlists and courier handoff. Growth (₹999) gives me drafts only, which is useless at drop speed. Starter at ₹499 with 100 orders is not for me (400 orders means ₹900 in overage on top, so it is worse than Growth anyway). ₹1,999 lands inside my budget, and replacing ManyChat plus the form makes it a straight swap in cost.
Would I pay after the trial? Not on day 16 unless comment-to-DM is live or has a firm date. I would probably pay for one or two months to run drops through the DM assistant, keep ManyChat in parallel, and cancel whichever fails.
What makes me upgrade: more staff logins, WhatsApp, raffle and drop tools, a higher reply cap. At 400 orders a month I will not touch the 2,000 limit. What makes me leave: one oversell on a hype drop, wrong size or price sent to a buyer, Instagram disconnecting mid-drop (token expiry), slow support at night, or ManyChat-style comment features never shipping.
Flat versus per-order versus percent: flat fee, I trust it, because I can plan. A per-order fee of ₹2 on 400 orders is ₹800, fine, as long as unpaid drafts and abandoned chats do not count as orders. A percentage of sales I would reject outright: even 0.5% of ₹18 lakh is ₹9,000, and it punishes me for having a good drop.

**9. Free plan or free trial? Annual plan?**
Trial is fine, but 15 days is short because I do 3 to 4 drops a month, and a trial window could miss the one big drop. I want 30 days, or the trial pauses until I have run two drops. A small free plan (say 30 orders a month) would also let me test with a mini drop, nice to have. Annual: I would only consider it after 2 to 3 months. 15% off does not move me, 2 months free (about 17%) is okay, 25% yes. Never before it has proven itself.

**10. Add-ons I would actually pay for**
- Drop pack (comment-to-DM, waitlist alerts, raffle) if not included in Pro: ₹500 to ₹800 a month. ₹999 is too much given ₹99 to ₹299 competitors.
- Priority drop-day support (call or WhatsApp reply within 10 minutes during a drop): ₹1,000 a month. One bad drop night costs more than that.
- Extra staff logins beyond 3: ₹150 to ₹200 per seat a month.
- WhatsApp message packs for drop alerts and waitlist pings: at about Meta cost plus a small margin, up to ₹1.50 per marketing message all in. 5,000 messages is about ₹7,500, so packs matter.
- Auto-replies on my own WhatsApp number: ₹500 a month, nice later.
- Remove CartHedge branding: ₹300 a month, low priority.
- More AI replies: I would rather have unlimited. If it is capped, a pre-bought top-up of about ₹200 per 1,000 replies, with auto top-up so it never cuts out mid-drop.
- Done-for-you catalog upload: no, I do my own drops. COD/RTO protection: no. Courier booking: only if the rates match or beat my current Shiprocket rates.

**11. Who else influences the decision**
- Aman, my partner who packs and ships: "does the pick list work by size, and if the bot confirms the wrong size do we eat the return cost?"
- Our CA: "can you issue GST invoices in our format, and does CartHedge's own invoice show GSTIN so I can claim ITC? Are the buyer payments going straight to our Razorpay?"
- The two juniors: "will we still get paid for drop day, and can we get logins?"
- My dad, who funds the stock: "AI wala? Paisa barbaad mat kar." He only cares if the ₹ number is smaller than what I spend now.

**12. One sentence to a friend**
"Bro, it's like ManyChat plus a proper order desk with stock per size at Indian pricing, but comment-to-DM and waitlists aren't live yet, so trial it on a small drop, and if it oversells even once on a hype drop, delete it."

## Round 2 notes

- Now believe: stock hold with a timer is the real gate, not the approve tap (Pro already has optional auto-confirm). Most of the panel lives on WhatsApp, so my comment-to-DM, hold timer and raffle will queue behind it. I will ask for a written date.
- New wants from the group: AI disclosure with one-tap "talk to owner" routed to my juniors (staff logins), a verified-seller check against cloned drop pages, a real company address and status page on the site.
- Price range unchanged: flat ₹1,999 to ₹3,000, up to ₹3,500 only after a proven hype drop. Drop-day priority support separate at about ₹1,000 a drop. Per-order fee above quota is OK (₹2) only if unpaid drafts do not count and there is a cap and alert, never a hard stop mid-drop. Percent of sales never.
- WhatsApp costs: prepaid wallet, price shown before send, up to ₹1.50 all in per marketing message, hard cap, never hidden in autopay. AI replies: unlimited on Pro, or ₹200 per 1,000 top-up with auto top-up; a small free plan (30 orders) with a reply cap is fine.
- Agree with Hiren on template-shock fear and Pooja on AI disclosure. Disagree with Tanya on paying ₹5,000 to ₹12,000 flat, and with Shabnam benchmarking against the ₹99 keyword bots.

## Round 3 notes

- Concrete plan locked: "Drop Kit" at ₹2,000 flat, 400 orders, 1,000 AI replies per month, optional auto-confirm on prepaid only, stock-hold timer (15 minutes, no oversell), one staff login. Add-on "Drop Pack" at ₹800 for comment-to-DM, raffle, and scheduled go-live. Both plans need written Razorpay hard-cap, prepaid WhatsApp wallet (no autopay surprise), and a rollover buffer for Friday spikes.
- Make-or-break features: stock hold with countdown timer (cancellation trigger if it fails mid-drop with an oversell); comment-to-DM with a written ship date by August 2027 (cancellation trigger if it slips past that). Make me pay: proof of 500+ DM throughput on real drop days, a status page, and AI disclosure with one-tap staff-login routing to the juniors.
- Tanya's spike-mode insight shifted my pricing view: I'm not running parallel tools on drop day, so her ₹12k support-bundled plan doesn't fit reseller unit economics. I'd split support: base plan flat ₹2,000, drop-day priority support as a ₹1,000 add-on per drop (real work). Disagreement stands: D2C (400k followers, ₹5k-12k budgets) is a different customer than sneaker resellers (60k followers, 400 orders, ₹2-3.5k budgets).

## Round 4 vote

- Voting Pro at ₹2,999 because it replaces my ₹7k tooling spend, but "maybe" not "yes" until comment-to-DM ships or I get a written date by 60 days, and stock hold must be live day one. Price lock and hard-cap safeguards are the win.
- WhatsApp on own number at ₹499 is an instant add-on. Drop pack at ₹999 per day is too steep for comment-to-DM and raffle; ₹500-800 per month makes the math work. Trust page and support phone in Hindi/English shifts me from "new company risk" to "serious."
