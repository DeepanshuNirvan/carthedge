# Round 2: the group discussion

All 16 panelists have introduced themselves in a shared group. Now you talk to each other, like a busy WhatsApp group of sellers (plus an agency owner, a CA, a skeptic and a buyer) who were all shown the same new product.

## Steps

1. **Remember who you are.** Read your own persona file in full first. That is you, and everything in it is what you already know and think about CartHedge.
2. **Read the group.** Read every post in `C:/Users/deepanshunirvan/Desktop/Deepanshu/carthedge/docs/market-research/simulation/rounds/r1/` (16 files, one per panelist).
3. **Talk.** Write 3 to 5 separate group messages, each addressed to a named panelist (`@Name`) and replying to something specific they said. Mix these moves the way a real person would:
   - agree and add your own experience with numbers,
   - push back when you think someone is wrong, naive or too generous,
   - share how you handle the same problem today,
   - ask a sharp question,
   - correct a wrong assumption about CartHedge (check `C:/Users/deepanshunirvan/Desktop/Deepanshu/carthedge/product.txt` if unsure).
   At least one message must argue about **how CartHedge should charge**: flat fee vs per-order fee vs % of sales, free plan vs trial, how many AI replies a plan should include, who pays WhatsApp message costs, annual discounts. At least one message must say **what changed in your mind** after reading the others (or why nothing did).
4. **New ideas welcome.** If someone's post sparks a feature you'd need or a fear you hadn't thought of, say it.

## Rules

- Stay in character: language flavour, money habits, temper, loyalties. Disagreement is good; don't converge just to be nice.
- 220 to 380 words in total. No em dashes.
- Format each message as `**Your name → @Their name:** message`.
