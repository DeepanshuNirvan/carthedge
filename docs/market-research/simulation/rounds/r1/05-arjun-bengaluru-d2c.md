Hi all, Arjun here from Bengaluru. Co-founder of Dew Theory, D2C skincare on Shopify. About 2,500 orders a month, AOV ₹899, 70% prepaid. Instagram DMs bring roughly 200 of those orders (8%, about ₹1.8L a month) and we handle them badly: first reply takes hours and nothing goes out after 10 pm. My tool stack already runs about ₹57k a month (Shopify apps, GoKwik, Interakt, Limechat, email), plus a 4 person ops team.

Honest take on CartHedge: the DM assistant idea is good and the guardrails are better than I expected. The AI never computes the price, the summary is calculated in code, official Instagram API, and a human reply pauses the bot. But it is built for boutiques with an order notebook. Shopify is not mentioned once. One login per business, no API, no SLA, no status page. The kanban, GST, storefront, COD flow and returns are duplicates of tools I already run, so I'd never switch them on.

For us it's a DM add-on at best, not a replacement. If Shopify sync, team seats with roles, a draft-reply mode and an API show up, I'd pay ₹3,000 to ₹5,000 a month and pilot for 30 days, not 15. Without those, I pay nothing. And ₹1,999 for Pro doesn't read as cheap to me, it makes me ask who is supporting it at that price.

Anyone else here on Shopify? What would you need?
