Namaste all, Meenal Joshi here, CA from Ahmedabad. I do GST returns and books for about 60 small online sellers (fashion, jewellery, food): 34 regular GST, 9 composition, 17 below threshold. They call me before they buy any app, so my yes or no travels.

Honest take: the GST thinking is better than I expected. Invoices restart each financial year, CGST/SGST or IGST from the delivery address, bill of supply for composition, a credit note for every refund. Buyer money goes straight to the seller's own Razorpay, so no TCS or e-commerce operator question. I like that.

But today I would not recommend it. No GSTR-1 or Tally export yet, only "coming". No company name, GSTIN or address on the site. I also want in writing where my clients' customers' phone numbers and chats go when the AI reads them. And I cannot tell when the invoice is issued, or what happens to it when a COD parcel comes back RTO. Gujarati is not in the language samples either.

Price, plus GST: ₹499 to ₹799 is a bargain for my clients, ₹1,299 to ₹1,499 is my limit, ₹1,999 plus 18% is hard under 300 orders. No commission to me, give the discount to the client. Fix the above and I will test on 3 clients.
