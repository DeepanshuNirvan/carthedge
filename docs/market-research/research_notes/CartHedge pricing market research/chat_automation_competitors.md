# Chat automation and AI chat-commerce competitors: pricing for Indian sellers (as of Oct 2026)

Research date: 4 Oct 2026. About 30 searches and fetches. Official pricing pages were used where they could be fetched. Interakt, ManyChat, Bik.ai and SleekFlow either blocked the fetch (403 or cert error) or did not render prices, so those vendors rely on third-party sources, which are marked [3P]. Many [3P] sources are competitor blogs (Chat Mitra, RichAutomate, ReplyKaro, Kwikzy, CreatorFlow) and may be biased. Prices exclude 18% GST unless stated.

## Q1. Current plans, limits, seat pricing and annual discounts

### Takeaway
Indian WhatsApp BSP inboxes cost ₹999 to ₹3,500/month at entry level (Interakt, AiSensy, BotSpace, DoubleTick, Gallabox Basic). WATI is the premium outlier at ₹4,999/month. Instagram-only DM tools are much cheaper: free to about ₹1,600/month, with a race to free (Zorcha went free in Aug 2026, ManyChat cut its free tier to 25 contacts in Mar 2026). D2C-focused AI platforms (Bik, LimeChat) are sales-led and do not publish prices.

- The dense Indian price band for a small-seller WhatsApp + Instagram inbox is ₹999 to ₹1,500/mo (BotSpace Pro, Interakt Starter, AiSensy Basic). ₹2,500 to ₹3,500 is "growth" tier. ₹5,000+ is D2C or team tier (WATI, Gallabox Essential+).
- Annual discounts cluster at 10% to 20% for Indian BSPs (AiSensy 10%, WATI about 17%, Gallabox 20%). ManyChat gives about 18% to 30%.
- Instagram-only automation is being commoditised to free (Zorcha, SuperProfile, LinkDM free tier). CartHedge cannot charge for comment-to-DM alone. The value has to sit in the order desk, the storefront and AI closing the sale.
- Seat caps (WATI Growth 3, Gallabox Basic 3) and ₹750 to ₹5,520 per extra seat are a common upsell lever. Small sellers with 1 to 2 operators rarely hit them.

## Q2. How each vendor passes on Meta WhatsApp charges

### Takeaway
Most Indian BSPs use a prepaid wallet and add a 10% to 30% markup on Meta's per-message rate. AiSensy charges about 26% over Meta. BotSpace and Zoko (Plus and above) advertise zero markup. Meta charges service messages from 1 Oct 2026, beyond 1,000 free per number per month, at the utility rate. That directly raises the cost of AI DM replies on WhatsApp. Instagram messaging carries no Meta fee.

### Cited Findings

**Meta base rates (India)**
- Meta has billed per message (not per conversation) since 1 Jul 2025. The current rate cards are dated "effective July 1, 2026". Click-to-WhatsApp ads open a free 72-hour window covering all message types. — [Meta developer docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing)
- India marketing went from ₹0.7846 to ₹0.8631 per message (about +10%) effective 1 Jan 2026. Utility and authentication stay at about ₹0.115. Add 18% GST, so marketing is about ₹1.0185 with GST. — [search summary of Authkey/Chat Mitra/RichAutomate](https://authkey.io/blogs/whatsapp-pricing-update-2026/); [Chat Mitra](https://chatmitra.com/blog/whatsapp-business-cost-per-message/)

**New: service-message charging from 1 Oct 2026**
- Service messages and utility templates sent inside the 24-hour customer-service window used to be free. From 1 Oct 2026 they are charged at the destination's utility rate after 1,000 free service messages per business phone number per month. The allowance resets monthly with no rollover. Messages in the free entry-point window and user-sent messages stay free. — [360dialog](https://360dialog.com/blog/whatsapp-service-message-charging-october-2026/); [Gupshup support](https://support.gupshup.io/hc/en-us/articles/62362400519705-WhatsApp-Service-Messages-Pricing-w-e-f-01-Oct-2026); [YCloud](https://www.ycloud.com/blog/whatsapp-api-message-pricing-update-effective-october-1-2026)
  - Conflict: the Meta developer page I fetched still said "Utility templates delivered within an open customer service window are free". It may not be updated yet, or my fetch may have hit the pre-October text. — [Meta developer docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing)
  - AiSensy's page confirms India service messages at ₹0.145 after 1,000 free per month, effective 1 Oct 2026. — [AiSensy pricing](https://aisensy.com/pricing)

**Vendor pass-through**

| Vendor | Model | Marketing ₹/msg | Utility / auth ₹/msg | Markup vs Meta base |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AiSensy | Prepaid; ₹10 minimum balance to run automation | 1.09 | 0.145 (service 0.145 after 1,000 free) | about 26% on both |
| Interakt Starter [3P] | Not stated | 0.970 | 0.160 / 0.129 | see note |
| Interakt Growth [3P] | Not stated | 0.958 | 0.150 / 0.128 | see note |
| DoubleTick [3P] | Not stated | 0.88 | not found | about 2% |
| BotSpace | Prepaid wallet remitted to Meta | Meta rate | Meta rate | 0% ("We do not make any money from this") |
| Zoko Starter | Prepaid | Meta rate + $0.015 per conversation | Meta rate + $0.015 | flat fee |
| Zoko Plus and above | Prepaid; "follows Meta's rate card" | Meta rate | Meta rate | 0% within plan allowance |
| WATI | Per-message; country rate cards | not shown | not shown | not disclosed |

- **AiSensy**: billing is "pre-paid" and a minimum ₹10 balance is needed for automation. Markup is my inference from ₹0.8631 and ₹0.115 base rates. — [AiSensy pricing](https://aisensy.com/pricing)
- **Interakt** [3P]: rates per [CampaignHQ](https://blog.campaignhq.co/interakt-pricing-india-2026). LandInChat instead states the markup as "+25% on Meta rates" for Starter, "+15%" for Growth and "+10%" for Advanced. — [LandInChat](https://landinchat.com/pricing-comparison/interakt)
  - My arithmetic: ₹0.958 is about 11% over ₹0.8631, but about 22% over the pre-Jan-2026 ₹0.7846. The published rates may predate the Jan 2026 Meta increase.
  - Utility ₹0.150 to ₹0.160 is about 30% to 39% over ₹0.115.
- **DoubleTick** [3P]: rate per [RichAutomate](https://richautomate.in/blog/aisensy-price-hike-may-2026-alternatives-india). The same source lists Interakt and MSG91 at ₹0.88 and RichAutomate at ₹0.85.
- **BotSpace**: prepaid wallet; Meta fees are collected and remitted directly to Meta. Subscriptions are postpaid. Wallet balances are non-refundable. Its page notes that Meta does not charge for Instagram messaging. — [BotSpace pricing](https://www.bot.space/pricing)
- **Zoko**: all charges are prepaid. — [Zoko pricing](https://www.zoko.io/pricing)
- **WATI**:
  - The pricing page only says "Per message charges vary for marketing, utility & authentication", links to country rate cards, and discloses no markup. — [WATI pricing](https://www.wati.io/pricing/)
  - The ₹999 pay-as-you-go pack buys about 500 messages, an implied average of about ₹2/message. — [WATI pricing](https://www.wati.io/pricing/)
- **Gallabox**: "WhatsApp conversation fees" are billed separately per country, and no markup is shown on the page. — [Gallabox pricing](https://gallabox.com/pricing)
- **ManyChat**: WhatsApp fees are separate from subscriptions. From 1 Oct 2026 it also bills service messages beyond 1,000 per month per number. — [Featurebase](https://www.featurebase.app/blog/manychat-pricing)

### Inferences
- The going market markup for Indian SMB BSPs is about 10% to 26% on marketing messages and up to about 40% on utility. "Zero markup / at Meta cost" is a credible, differentiated claim: only BotSpace and Zoko make it explicitly.
- The 1 Oct 2026 service-message charge matters for an AI DM sales assistant on WhatsApp. A seller with more than 1,000 replies a month per number now pays about ₹0.115 + GST per reply to Meta, or ₹0.145 via AiSensy. Instagram DMs carry no Meta fee, which makes IG-first AI replies structurally cheaper.
- Prepaid wallet plus a small minimum recharge (AiSensy ₹10) is the norm. Non-refundable wallets (BotSpace) are a likely friction point.

### Gaps
- I could not fetch Interakt's official rate card or WATI's India rate card, so markup figures for those two are third-party or inferred.
- No minimum-recharge amounts were found for Interakt, WATI or Gallabox.
- The Meta developer page and BSP announcements conflict on service-message charging. Confirm against Meta's live India rate card.

## Q3. AI agent pricing

### Takeaway
AI is priced in three ways:
- **Bundled credits inside the plan**: WATI, Gallabox, ManyChat Pro.
- **Flat add-on with a message allowance**: AiSensy, ₹1,350/mo for 1,000 AI messages.
- **Per outcome**: BotSpace ₹10 per resolution, Zoko $0.09 per resolution, and Zoko's sales agent at 3% of order value.

The cheapest real AI tier for an Indian seller is about ₹999 to ₹1,350/mo (BotSpace Pro or the AiSensy AI add-on), plus usage.

### Cited Findings
- **BotSpace**:
  - AI agent "Rex" costs ₹10 ($0.30) per resolution. It includes a knowledge base, tone of voice, human handover and intent detection.
  - AI Copilot: free for 10 conversations per member per month, then ₹750/seat/mo for unlimited use.
  - AI Insights (CSAT, sentiment): ₹8,800/mo. — [BotSpace pricing](https://www.bot.space/pricing)
- **Zoko**:
  - Each AI agent costs $24.99/mo.
  - "Guru" (FAQ) and "Wismo" (order tracking) cost $0.09 per resolution after 100 free.
  - "Sello" (sales agent) costs **3% of order value after 100 free orders**. — [Zoko pricing](https://www.zoko.io/pricing)
- **AiSensy**: AI Agent Builder ₹1,350/mo (₹1,215/mo yearly) including 1,000 AI messages, which is about ₹1.35 per AI message. It adds 500 free test messages, knowledge-base Q&A, long-term memory and tool calling. Chatbot builder is a separate ₹2,500/mo. — [AiSensy pricing](https://aisensy.com/pricing)
- **WATI**: bundled AI credits of 2,000 (Growth), 5,000 (Pro) and 20,000 (Business) per month, usable for Wati and the "Astra" AI agent. AI Copilot covers 1 agent on Growth, 2 on Pro, and unlimited on Business. Voice AI is Business only. Extra AI credits are "custom quantity pricing". — [WATI pricing](https://www.wati.io/pricing/)
- **Gallabox**: 500 (Basic), 2,000 (Essential) and 8,000 (Advanced) AI credits per month, auto-deducted for AI replies and translations. Extra credits come in 5,000-unit packs, with no price shown. AI agents on WhatsApp and Instagram require Essential (₹6,999/mo, or ₹5,599 annual) or above. — [Gallabox pricing](https://gallabox.com/pricing)
- **ManyChat**: AI is included from Pro ($39/mo, or $29 annual). The old $29/mo AI add-on is legacy. — [Featurebase](https://www.featurebase.app/blog/manychat-pricing)
- **DoubleTick**: "AI agents included in all plans". No credit or overage details are shown. — [DoubleTick pricing](https://doubletick.io/pricing)
- **Zorcha** [3P]: DMs are unlimited and the meter is AI credits and team credits on paid tiers (Pro ₹1,199/mo annual). — [Kwikzy](https://www.kwikzy.com/blogs/instagram-automation-tools-india-under-1000)
- **Interakt**: [LandInChat](https://landinchat.com/pricing-comparison/interakt) says "custom flows and chatbot AI add-ons are billed separately", but no price was found.

### Inferences
- Per-resolution AI pricing in India anchors at about ₹7.5 to ₹10 (Zoko $0.09; BotSpace ₹10). Per-message AI anchors at about ₹1.35 (AiSensy).
- Zoko's "3% of order value" sales agent is the closest analogue to CartHedge's AI DM sales assistant. It sets a precedent for GMV-linked AI pricing, though it is in USD and aimed at Shopify brands.
- A small seller doing 300 AI-closed chats a month would pay about ₹3,000 at BotSpace's ₹10 per resolution. A flat ₹999 to ₹1,499 AI-inclusive plan with a fair-use cap would undercut it and is easier to understand.

### Gaps
- Prices not found: WATI and Gallabox extra-credit packs, the Interakt AI add-on, Bik.ai and LimeChat AI pricing, and DoubleTick AI limits.
- What one "AI credit" equals (per reply or per conversation) at WATI and Gallabox is not defined on their pages.

## Q5. Target segment and recurring complaints

### Takeaway
There are two tiers:
- **Small Instagram/WhatsApp sellers**, on INR self-serve plans at ₹99 to ₹1,500: BotSpace, AiSensy, Interakt Starter, Zorcha, ReplyKaro and other Instagram tools.
- **D2C / Shopify brands**: Zoko, Bik, LimeChat, WATI Business, Gallabox Advanced, and SleekFlow for regional SMB teams.

Complaints centre on real bills far above the headline price (message fees plus add-ons), hard cancellation, slow support and template rejections.

### Cited Findings
- **Small sellers**:
  - BotSpace is Pune-built with native INR and Razorpay billing, and priced from ₹999 by engaged contacts. — [BotSpace pricing](https://www.bot.space/pricing); [Chat Mitra](https://chatmitra.com/chatmitra-vs-botspace/)
  - Interakt sells a ₹999 Instagram-only Starter. — [CampaignHQ](https://blog.campaignhq.co/interakt-pricing-india-2026)
  - Only ReplyKaro and UnlockDM, among the Instagram tools reviewed, accept UPI/Razorpay. LinkDM is USD-only. — [UnlockDM](https://www.tryunlockdm.com/blog/instagram-dm-automation-india-2026); [Kwikzy](https://www.kwikzy.com/blogs/instagram-automation-tools-india-under-1000)
- **D2C / Shopify brands**:
  - Zoko is sold as a Shopify app ("cart recovery on WhatsApp"). — [Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/whatsapp-button-chat)
  - BIK targets "eCommerce brands". — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/264836/Bikayi/)
  - LimeChat targets D2C, BFSI, automotive and real estate with chat and voice AI. — [LimeChat landing](https://www.limechat.ai/landing-page-q2-2026)
- **WATI complaints** [3P + review aggregators]:

### Inferences
- The recurring pain is unpredictable total cost: subscription plus marked-up per-message fees plus seat, channel and AI add-ons. A single all-in INR price with Meta fees at cost and a visible wallet is a direct answer to the top complaint.
- No vendor in this set bundles a storefront and order desk with the AI DM agent at the ₹999 to ₹1,500 small-seller price point. The closest are BotSpace (inbox + AI) and SuperProfile (storefront + AutoDM, monetised by a 5% to 10% sales fee).
