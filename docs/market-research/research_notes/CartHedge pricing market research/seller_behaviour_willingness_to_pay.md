# Indian Instagram/WhatsApp-first small sellers: behaviour, economics, willingness to pay (as of Oct 2026)

Research date: 4 Oct 2026. Older data is marked with its year. Several sources (Reddit, Quora, Business Standard, qz.com) returned HTTP 403 to the fetch tool; where a figure comes only from a search-result summary rather than a fetched page, it is flagged "(snippet)".

## 1. Market size: social-commerce GMV, number of sellers, growth, category mix

### Takeaway
Headline "social commerce" estimates vary 10x because definitions differ: the old Bain forecast ($16–20B by 2025) counted reseller/social platforms like Meesho, while Redseer's Nov 2025 report sizes chat-led selling by offline-first MSMEs, the segment CartHedge targets, at only US$1.4–2.2B GMV in CY2024, growing 40–50% a year to 2029. The seller base is huge (55–60M retail MSMEs), but no credible public count of Instagram/WhatsApp sellers exists.

### Cited Findings
- **Redseer, Nov 2025 (Shiprocket IPO industry report):** "digitally enabled social commerce" (WhatsApp and social-media chat sellers who need enablement or logistics help) was ₹116–183 billion (US$1.4–2.2B) GMV in CY2024. It is expected to grow at a 40–50% CAGR from CY2024 to CY2029P. — [Redseer Report on India E-commerce Enablement Opportunity, 30 Nov 2025](https://sr-website-01.shiprocket.in/sr-website/IndustryReport-MhCbDH.pdf)
- Same report: direct commerce (brand websites) was ₹581–747B (US$7–9B) GMV in CY2024, at a 20–25% CAGR. The total addressable market for e-commerce enablement platforms was ₹7.7–8.4 trillion (US$92–101B). — [Redseer/Shiprocket](https://sr-website-01.shiprocket.in/sr-website/IndustryReport-MhCbDH.pdf)
- Same report: India has 80–85 million MSMEs, of which 55–60 million are retail MSMEs (31 Dec 2024). Trade/retail MSMEs make up 64–68% of domestic retail, and offline retail is still about 93% of total retail (CY2024). — [Redseer/Shiprocket](https://sr-website-01.shiprocket.in/sr-website/IndustryReport-MhCbDH.pdf)
- Same report, describing the purchase journey: smaller sellers lack the know-how to build a website and "rely on direct communication to receive orders". The journey "takes orders via direct messages and payments typically made through UPI apps", and "customers frequently confirm payments via screenshots". Redseer names conversational commerce (WhatsApp Business, Instagram DMs, AI chatbots), community commerce (WhatsApp groups), live commerce and vernacular chatbots as the trends. — [Redseer/Shiprocket](https://sr-website-01.shiprocket.in/sr-website/IndustryReport-MhCbDH.pdf)
- Same report: there were 230–250M online shoppers in CY2024, projected at 338–403M by CY2029. About 46% of online domestic retail GMV came from Tier-2+ cities (CY2024). — [Redseer/Shiprocket](https://sr-website-01.shiprocket.in/sr-website/IndustryReport-MhCbDH.pdf)
- **Bain + Flipkart, How India Shops Online 2025:**
  - E-retail GMV was about $60B in 2024, growing 10–12% (down from 20%+ in earlier years), and is projected at $170–190B by 2030.
  - India had over 270M online shoppers in 2024. Gen Z is 40% of e-retail shoppers.
  - 60% of new shoppers since 2020 came from Tier-3 or smaller cities, and 60% of new sellers since 2021 came from Tier-2 or smaller cities.
  - Trend-first fashion is projected at $8–10B by 2028. Hyper-value commerce grew from about 5% of GMV (2021) to over 12% (2024).
  - Source: [Bain, How India Shops Online 2025](https://www.bain.com/insights/how-india-shops-online-2025/)
- One search summary said India's online retail "concluded 2025 with GMV of about $65–66B, growing 19–21%". That is arithmetically inconsistent with $60B in 2024 at 10–12% growth, so treat it as unverified (snippet). — [Bain HISO 2025 PDF](https://storiesflistgv2.blob.core.windows.net/stories/2025/03/how_india_shops_online_2025.pdf)
- **Older forecasts (2020–2022), which conflict with each other:**
  - Bain–Sequoia (2020): social commerce of $16–20B by 2025, up from $1.5–2B in FY20 (a 55–60% CAGR). — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/social-commerce-in-india-poised-to-hit-16-bn-gmv-by-2025/)
  - Redseer (2020): social commerce of only $5.6–7B by 2025, or 4–5% of e-commerce. — [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2020/12/social-commerce-india-could-touch-7b-dollar-2025-redseer)
  - A "$70B social commerce opportunity by 2030" headline (Bain, 2022; title only, not fetched). — [Consultancy.in](https://www.consultancy.in/news/3478/indias-70-billion-social-commerce-opportunity-by-2030)
- **Unicommerce India D2C Report (Apr 2026), category order growth in FY2026:** Health & Pharma +48%, Beauty/Personal Care +41%, FMCG +32%, Fashion +21%, Home Furnishings +19%. Tier-2/3 cities drove 66% of incremental order volume. The data covers 410M shipments from 6,000+ brands. — [Unicommerce D2C Report 2026](https://unicommerce.com/india-d2c-report-2026-april/)
- **WhatsApp Business in India (vendor blogs only; no primary Meta figure found):**
  - About 15M "active" WhatsApp Business accounts in India.
  - 480M+ cumulative WhatsApp Business app downloads in India (2025).
  - "~80% of small businesses in India and Brazil use WhatsApp".
  - Sources: [AiSensy](https://m.aisensy.com/blog/whatsapp-statistics-for-businesses/), [Wapikit](https://www.wapikit.com/blog/global-whatsapp-business-statistics-2025)
- **Meta (Jun 2026):** "more than one billion active threads with businesses on WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram every day". — [WhatsApp Business blog, Conversations 2026](https://whatsappbusiness.com/blog/introducing-meta-business-agent-ai/)

### Inferences
- For pricing models, use Redseer's US$1.4–2.2B (CY2024), growing 40–50% a year, as the "chat-led MSME seller" GMV. Bain's $16–20B is a different, platform-inclusive definition and should not be used to size CartHedge's segment.
- Growth is concentrated in Tier-2/3 cities and in beauty, health and fashion. Vernacular support and COD handling are table stakes, not premium features.

### Gaps
- No credible public count of Instagram-only or WhatsApp-only sellers in India. The 15M WhatsApp Business figure is vendor-blog sourced and unverified.
- No source found for the category mix of social commerce specifically (fashion, jewellery, home, food, beauty shares). Category data above is for D2C brands (Unicommerce), not DM-first sellers.
- KPMG and Meta-commissioned India social-commerce reports for 2025–26 were not found within the search budget.

## 2. Seller economics: orders, AOV, COD share, RTO rates, cost of an RTO

### Takeaway
COD is still about half of orders even at the 2025 festive peak, and COD orders return to origin (RTO) at 2–5x the prepaid rate (roughly 20–26% for COD vs under 2–10% for prepaid; 58% for COD at festive peak). Each bounced ₹1,000 COD order costs a seller roughly ₹200–250, so cutting RTO is the most quantifiable value CartHedge can sell.

### Cited Findings
- **Unicommerce India D2C Report (Apr 2026, FY2026 data):**
  - Overall RTO was 39.2% at the November festive peak, 25.6% in January, and 21.0% in March for optimized brands.
  - In the festive quarter, COD return rate was 58% vs under 15% for prepaid.
  - Three operational levers separated the 39% brands from the 21% brands: prepaid incentives, pin-code-based courier routing, and address verification at checkout.
  - Source: [Unicommerce](https://unicommerce.com/india-d2c-report-2026-april/)
- **GoKwik Diwali 2025:** 47.58% COD vs 52.42% prepaid; festive prepaid share rose from 37.44% to 52.42% in one year. Full-year COD remains higher and still dominates Tier-3. — [Angadi Labs benchmarks (compiling GoKwik), 25 Jun 2026](https://www.angadilabs.com/blog/india-fashion-ecommerce-benchmarks)
- **Unicommerce Diwali 2025:** COD order volume grew 22% YoY vs prepaid +26%. Tier-II and III cities were about 55% of orders, and Tier-II led growth at +28% YoY. — [MediaBrief](https://mediabrief.com/unicommerce-reports-24-order-volume-growth-during-2025-diwali-festive-season/)
- **COD vs prepaid RTO by year**, compiled by Angadi Labs from vendor data:
  - FY23 (Unicommerce): COD 20.9% vs prepaid 5.8%
  - 2024 (Unicommerce): COD ~24% vs prepaid ~10%
  - FY25 (Shipway): COD 26% vs prepaid under 2%
  - Industry average is 20–25%; well-run operations get to 15–20%, the best to 10–15%.
  - Source: [Angadi Labs](https://www.angadilabs.com/blog/india-fashion-ecommerce-benchmarks)
- **GoKwik on industry RTO:** "average RTO rate of about 20–25% and goes up to nearly 40%" depending on category, state or pincode (snippet). — [Inc42 / GoKwik](https://inc42.com/features/early-stage-d2c-brands-can-scale-faster-and-profitably-with-kwik-cod-app-on-shopify-gokwiks-vineeta-vora/)
- **Cost of an RTO:** "For a ₹1,000 COD order that bounces, brands lose an estimated ₹200 to ₹250 once return freight, inspection, and refund processing are counted" (GoKwik estimate). — [Angadi Labs](https://www.angadilabs.com/blog/india-fashion-ecommerce-benchmarks)
- **Fashion D2C AOV, compiled from GoKwik and ClickPost:**
  - ₹1,869 (festive 2024)
  - ₹1,586 for new-age D2C fashion (FY2026)
  - ₹1,854 for conventional fashion (FY2026)
  - ₹1,309 for non-festive Tier-1 (Q4 FY25)
  - Benchmark range: ₹1,500–1,900.
  - Source: [Angadi Labs](https://www.angadilabs.com/blog/india-fashion-ecommerce-benchmarks)
- **Return rates:** Indian apparel/fashion return rates run 25–40%; across India overall, 10.4% of orders (FY23, Unicommerce). — [Angadi Labs](https://www.angadilabs.com/blog/india-fashion-ecommerce-benchmarks)
- **Redseer:** single-vendor logistics providers often lack "RTO prediction, insurance, early COD advances", which is why MSMEs move to aggregators. — [Redseer/Shiprocket](https://sr-website-01.shiprocket.in/sr-website/IndustryReport-MhCbDH.pdf)

### Inferences
- Value framing for a ₹999/mo plan: at about ₹225 lost per bounced ₹1,000 COD order, avoiding 4–5 RTOs a month covers the plan.
- A seller doing 100 COD orders a month at a 25% RTO rate loses roughly ₹5,000–6,000 a month (illustrative, using GoKwik's ₹200–250 per order).
- CartHedge's COD confirmation, OTP and prepaid-nudge features map directly onto the three levers Unicommerce identified (prepaid incentives, address verification, routing).
- Festive months (Oct–Nov) are when RTO peaks (39%) and when sellers feel the pain most. That is the best acquisition window for COD/RTO messaging.
- DM-first sellers probably sit at the worse end of these benchmarks: more first-time buyers, no checkout-level address verification. This is an inference; no data on DM-first sellers was found.

### Gaps
- No public data on monthly order volume by seller size band for Instagram/WhatsApp sellers. All order, AOV and RTO data above is D2C brands on Shopify-type stacks, not DM-first micro-sellers.
- No Delhivery or Shiprocket category-level COD/RTO split for jewellery, food or home was found. Shiprocket's DRHP KPIs (for example COD share of its shipments) were not extractable from the industry-report section.
- A vendor-blog claim that small sellers "earn ₹15,000–₹30,000/month" from WhatsApp and Facebook groups is unsourced and excluded.

## 3. Software buying behaviour of Indian micro/small businesses

### Takeaway
The anchor for Indian micro-business software is about ₹3,500–5,000 a year for billing apps, sold annually with heavy "discount from list" framing, money-back guarantees and a CA seat. Sellers balk at bigger tickets: Bikayi's move to ₹18–30k a year pushed 90% of buyers onto EMI, and its revenue then collapsed. Meta has also just set a ₹0 anchor for AI DM replies.

### Cited Findings
- **myBillBook pricing (fetched Oct 2026):**
  - Plus: ₹3,490/yr (struck-through ₹4,833), or ₹291/mo.
  - Pro (most popular): ₹3,990/yr (struck-through ₹7,982), or ₹333/mo. Includes SMS/WhatsApp marketing, 500 messages a year.
  - Pro Max: from ₹570/mo, 2 businesses and 3 users.
  - Every plan includes access for "1 user + 1 CA".
  - Terms: "up to 20% off" on yearly plans, "up to 65% savings on multi-year plans", a free trial via the app, and a "7 days money back guarantee". Pays by card, net banking, UPI or wallet.
  - Source: [myBillBook pricing](https://mybillbook.in/pricing-plans)
- **Vyapar** (snippet; not fetched, so verify): Desktop+Mobile Silver ₹4,399/yr (list ₹7,499); Gold ₹4,799/yr (list ₹9,099); entry plans about ₹700–3,400/yr, priced per device. — [SoftwareSuggest Vyapar](https://www.softwaresuggest.com/vyapar)
- **Bikayi (2022, a WhatsApp-store builder for small sellers):**
  - Annual subscription rose from ₹5,000 (Jan 2021) to ₹15–18k (mid-2021) to ₹30,000 (Mar 2022).
  - "up to 90% of the customers opted for the EMI-based subscription" at ₹18,000.
  - After EMI was stopped, monthly revenue fell from ₹1.9 Cr (Apr 2022) to ₹9.5 lakh (Jul 2022).
  - About 2.8M of a claimed 7M merchants were removed.
  - On unit economics: "Selling a subscription plan at INR 5,000 meant that Bikayi could only end up with positive unit economics if those customers purchased the digital marketing credits as add-ons."
  - Source: [Inc42, 28 Jul 2022](https://inc42.com/features/bikayi-in-disarray-startup-hit-by-fraud-allegations-seller-exodus/)
- **Dukaan (snippet, 2022):** "discontinued the free plan completely and crossed $1.7 Mn" ARR in January 2022. The same coverage notes "merchants won't say no as long as solutions are free, but once they start charging, things are expected to change". — [Srijith Padmesh, The Investment Thesis: Dukaan](https://srijithpadmesh.substack.com/p/dukaan)
- **Meta Business Agent (3 Jun 2026):**
  - Answers questions, recommends catalogue products, "closes sales", qualifies leads, escalates to humans, and sends morning briefings on missed conversations.
  - Runs on WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram and Business Suite.
  - "Getting started is free. In the coming months, businesses will access the agent through our paid subscription offerings."
  - "More than one million businesses are already using a Meta Business Agent".
  - Source: [WhatsApp Business blog](https://whatsappbusiness.com/blog/introducing-meta-business-agent-ai/)
  - It was tested in India, Mexico and Brazil for about two years before global rollout. — [CNBC, 3 Jun 2026](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/03/meta-business-agent-is-zuckerberg-latest-effort-to-diversify-from-ads.html)
  - Its India predecessor, "Business AI", was free for all WhatsApp Business app users, supported Indian languages, and answered FAQs, pricing and shipping questions (snippet). — [SiliconIndia](https://www.siliconindia.com/news/general/meta-launches-ai-support-tool-for-whatsapp-businesses-in-india-nid-240554-cid-1.html)
- **UPI AutoPay:**
  - NPCI circular OC-223 (7 Oct 2025) makes AutoPay mandates viewable and portable across UPI apps, with a compliance deadline of 31 Dec 2025.
  - A central portal, upihelp.npci.org.in, lets users see and cancel mandates.
  - Sources: [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/news/npci-set-to-make-upi-autopay-mandates-portable-across-apps-report-12458130), [Essara guide](https://essara.space/guides/what-is-upi-autopay-how-it-works)
- **AutoPay failures:** "UPI autopay revocations hit 20 mn per month on low customer balance" (headline, Sep 2025; body not fetched). — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/finance/news/upi-autopay-revocations-hit-20-mn-monthly-over-low-customer-balances-125090700500_1.html)
- **Practitioner opinion, not a survey:** "₹999/month emerges as the sweet spot" for Indian micro-SaaS. Typical tiers are ₹299–499, ₹999–1,999 and ₹2,999–4,999 a month. — [rajeshrnair.com (blog)](https://rajeshrnair.com/blog/software/saas/micro-saas-ideas-india)

### Inferences
- **Monthly price is a hard sell.** The comparable a seller already pays for is about ₹300–400 a month (billed annually) for billing software, and Meta gives basic AI DM replies away free. CartHedge must justify any price above about ₹500/mo with outcomes Meta's free agent does not cover: the order desk, COD/RTO control, storefront and payment tracking.
- **Annual billing with EMI or monthly payment is how this market buys.** The Bikayi pattern shows sellers will commit to an annual price only if they can pay monthly. UPI AutoPay monthly mandates are the modern equivalent of EMI, but expect failed debits from low balances: build retries and a grace period.
- **The CA is a built-in stakeholder.** myBillBook gives a free CA seat on every plan, so GST and invoice exports for the CA could be a retention hook.
- **Expect Meta's paid tier to set the AI-agent price ceiling** once announced. Monitor it.

### Gaps
- No Vyapar, Khatabook, myBillBook, Zoho, Google-KPMG or NASSCOM survey with quantitative willingness-to-pay, trial-to-paid conversion or churn-reason data for Indian micro-businesses was found within budget.
- No data on UPI AutoPay adoption specifically for B2B or SaaS subscriptions. A "926M AutoPay transactions in Nov 2025 vs 530.5M in Nov 2024 (top-10 banks)" figure appeared in a search summary but could not be tied to a source URL, so it is excluded.
- No quantitative data on the role of family members or agencies in purchase decisions. CA involvement is evidenced only by product design (myBillBook's CA seat).

## 4. Real seller voices: complaints and wishes

### Takeaway
Reddit, Quora and Play Store were not reachable (HTTP 403 or blocked), so verbatim seller voice comes only from Shopify app reviews by Indian merchants and press quotes. The recurring themes are fake or fraudulent COD orders, RTO, cancellations, unresponsive tool support, and "price?" DM overload.

### Cited Findings
- On an Indian COD order-confirmation (OTP) Shopify app (reviews fetched Oct 2026; all from apps.shopify.com/cod-order-confirmation-1/reviews):
  - foottex, India (12 Mar 2026): the app "reduced fake orders, cancellations, and RTO issues".
  - Vinod Cookware, India (13 Feb 2024): it combats "fraudulent COD orders through #MetaAds". This suggests Meta ads drive fake COD orders.
  - Mai Soli, India (13 Jul 2023): "Our rate of cancellation has gone down", and it converted COD orders to prepaid.
  - Meadbery, India (9 Oct 2024): the app has not addressed "evolving challenges faced by Indian Shopify sellers, particularly concerning RTO and COD issues".
  - Shop The Arena, India (24 Apr 2025): "app has not been working for 10 days and no response yet".
- **Bikayi merchant (2022):** "If my customers cannot even add products to cart, what good is WhatsApp and Facebook marketing?" Another seller complained of missing analytics after spending ₹12,000 on marketing credits. — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/features/bikayi-in-disarray-startup-hit-by-fraud-allegations-seller-exodus/)
- **"price?" DM overload (vendor guide, not a survey; snippet):** sellers "post a product, get 30–50 DMs asking 'price?' and spend the next 3 hours typing the same answer", and buyers who wait more than about 10 minutes "move on to the next seller". — [StitchMagic guide](https://stitchmagic.in/guides/sell-on-instagram-india)
- **Redseer** names integration complexity, limited payment options, credit access, and fragmented multi-vendor operations as key MSME challenges. — [Redseer/Shiprocket](https://sr-website-01.shiprocket.in/sr-website/IndustryReport-MhCbDH.pdf)

### Inferences
- Fake COD orders from ads, cancellations and RTO dominate the voice of paying sellers. Tools that verify COD buyers (OTP or WhatsApp confirmation) earn praise.
- Tool support responsiveness is a churn trigger ("10 days and no response").
- "Price?" DM fatigue is widely asserted by vendors but not evidenced by a primary source here. Validate it in CartHedge's own seller interviews.

### Gaps
- No verbatim Reddit (r/IndianEntrepreneur, r/smallbusinessindia), Quora, YouTube or Play Store lines could be captured. Both Reddit domains and Quora returned 403 or were blocked.
- No primary evidence was found on late-night replying, payment chasing (UPI screenshot fraud) or language struggles. Redseer only notes buyers "confirm payments via screenshots". These need first-party interviews.

## 5. Buyer behaviour: COD, trust, scams, OTP, prepaid incentives, WhatsApp fatigue

### Takeaway
Buyers are moving to UPI: Gen Z is over 90% UPI, and festive prepaid passed 50% in 2025. Distrust of unknown sellers still keeps COD around half of orders, especially in Tier-2/3. Buyers are saturated with WhatsApp business spam (96% get it daily) and 59% block senders, so consent-gated, sparse broadcasts are essential.

### Cited Findings
- **LocalCircles WhatsApp spam survey (reported 4 Feb 2026):**
  - 42,000+ respondents across 324 districts.
  - 96% receive spam or promotional WhatsApp messages daily; only 4% receive none.
  - More than half get 1–3 a day and 30% get 4–7.
  - Share getting 8 or more a day is reported as 19% (vs 11% in 2023) by Storyboard18, but as 11% by Business Standard. These conflict.
  - "59% say they block senders, but spammers simply return with new numbers."
  - The "bulk of spam is pushed through WhatsApp Business".
  - Top spam categories: real estate and financial services (71% each), healthcare, jobs, personal and beauty services, gaming, telecom.
  - Sources: [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/amp/social-media/whatsapp-spam-hits-96-of-users-in-india-reveals-survey-amid-sc-heat-on-meta-88863.htm), [Business Standard (snippet)](https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/whatsapp-spam-promotional-messages-daily-users-india-digital-privacy-126020400369_1.html)
- **LocalCircles 2023:** 95% of WhatsApp users received spam daily. — [BusinessToday, Feb 2023](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/story/whatsapp-users-beware-survey-shows-95-users-get-spam-messages-daily-371263-2023-02-23); 51% got 4 or more spam messages a day — [LocalCircles](https://www.localcircles.com/a/press/page/spam-sms-survey)
- **LocalCircles dark-patterns study (Jun–Sep 2025):**
  - 77,000 responses from 334 districts.
  - 75% of e-commerce users frequently face drip pricing, including undisclosed "Cash on Delivery Fee".
  - About 29% had an order shipped COD or pay-later after cancelling at the payment stage.
  - Source: [BW Businessworld](https://www.businessworld.in/article/localcircles-says-75-indian-ecom-users-face-drip-pricing-575259)
- **Payments:** over 90% of Gen Z digital transactors prefer UPI. Gen Z is 40% of e-retail shoppers. — [Bain HISO 2025](https://www.bain.com/insights/how-india-shops-online-2025/)
- **Festive 2025 payment mix:** prepaid share rose from 37.44% to 52.42% YoY (GoKwik). COD orders grew 22% vs prepaid 26% (Unicommerce). — [Angadi Labs](https://www.angadilabs.com/blog/india-fashion-ecommerce-benchmarks), [MediaBrief](https://mediabrief.com/unicommerce-reports-24-order-volume-growth-during-2025-diwali-festive-season/)
- **Prepaid incentives** are one of the three levers separating 21%-RTO brands from 39%-RTO brands. — [Unicommerce D2C Report 2026](https://unicommerce.com/india-d2c-report-2026-april/)
- **Instagram shop scams and buyer fears:**
  - Scam accounts post too-cheap limited items, take payment and never deliver; contact numbers "go dead after payment".
  - Scammers clone seller handles by changing one letter and reply under real sellers' posts.
  - Buyer red flags: young accounts with follower/following parity; prices 30–50% below market with no returns policy.
  - Sources: [RTI wiki guide (2026)](https://righttoinformation.wiki/instagram-store-scam-india), [Moneylife fraud alert](https://www.moneylife.in/article/fraud-alert-social-media-eshop-scams/76634.html)
- **Fake engagement** inflates trust signals: 16M Indian influencer Instagram accounts were found to be fake (older study). — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/asia/india/16-million-accounts-of-indian-instagram-influencers-fake-study-1.65208654)
- **Payment trust on social commerce** rests on UPI with screenshot confirmation. — [Redseer/Shiprocket](https://sr-website-01.shiprocket.in/sr-website/IndustryReport-MhCbDH.pdf)

### Inferences
- COD is a trust instrument as much as a payment preference. For DM sellers, a verifiable storefront (order page, tracking link, business identity) is the lever that converts COD to prepaid, alongside a small prepaid discount.
- Buyers already block 59% of spammers. Opt-in, low-frequency, relevant broadcasts (CartHedge's consent-gated offers) are a selling point, and an unthrottled "bulk blast" feature would risk sellers' numbers getting blocked.
- Hidden COD fees are a known consumer grievance (LocalCircles). Any COD surcharge in CartHedge checkout must be shown upfront.

### Gaps
- No quantitative 2025–26 survey was found on why Indian buyers choose COD, how much they trust Instagram sellers, how they view OTP at checkout, or how big a prepaid discount it takes to switch.
- No data on opt-out (STOP) rates for WhatsApp marketing templates in India. Only the 59% "block senders" figure was found.
- Quora answers on Instagram thrift-store scams were not fetchable (403), so no verbatim buyer quotes.
