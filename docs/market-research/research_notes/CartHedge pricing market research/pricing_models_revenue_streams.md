# Pricing models, packaging and extra revenue streams for Indian SMB SaaS and commerce tools (as of Oct 2026)

Context: CartHedge list prices are ₹499/100 orders, ₹999/500, ₹1,999/2,000, with per-order overage, a 15-day trial and no card. That works out to about ₹4.99, ₹2.00 and ₹1.00 per included order. Buyer money settles to the seller's own Razorpay. Research date: 4 Oct 2026. "Aggregator" marks a third-party blog or review site rather than the vendor's own page. Treat aggregator figures as directional.

## 1. Which pricing models do Indian SMB SaaS and commerce tools use (tiers, usage, % of GMV, credits, price anchors, GST display, free plan vs trial, annual prepay, daily framing)?

### Takeaway
Indian SMB tools mostly use flat tiers, often sold yearly or for several years, with a usage layer on top:
- per-message wallets for WhatsApp tools;
- % of GMV for storefronts, with fees that drop on higher tiers.

Prices are almost always shown before 18% GST. Annual discounts range from about 10% (AiSensy) to more than 50% (Instamojo). Instamojo's "₹10/day" is a real example of daily-price framing. No-card trials convert at roughly 4-15%, against 25-60% when a card is required.

### Inferences
- **CartHedge sits at sensible "Bharat" anchors (₹499/₹999/₹1,999) and is cheaper than WhatsApp-tool incumbents.** Interakt Growth is ₹2,799 and AiSensy Pro ₹3,200, both before message fees. CartHedge's ₹999 is close to Dukaan Growth (₹999) and below Shopify Basic (₹1,499 annual).
- **CartHedge's flat per-order allowance is far cheaper per order than a % of GMV.** At a ₹800 average order value:
  - Dukaan Growth's 1.99% is about ₹16 per order.
  - Instamojo Starter's 5% + ₹3 is ₹43 per order.
  - CartHedge works out to ₹1-5 per order.
  - This is a sellable message ("no cut of your sales") for sellers who saw Dukaan's 2023 fee move.
- **Annual is the price point most Indian SMB tools actually sell.**
  - The norm is to show a monthly-equivalent price "billed annually" and/or offer multi-year deals (Vyapar, Khatabook).
  - CartHedge could offer roughly 20-30% off for annual (between Interakt's 21% and Shopify's 25%).
  - It could also add a daily framing such as "₹999/mo ≈ ₹33/day", following Instamojo.
- **GST display is a real choice for CartHedge.** Peers show prices before GST. If many Instagram sellers are not GST-registered and cannot claim input credit, ₹499 becomes about ₹589 at checkout. Showing "+GST" clearly up front avoids feeling like a bait-and-switch. Alternatively, show GST-inclusive prices as a trust differentiator.
- **CartHedge's no-card 15-day trial falls in the ChartMogul "4-15%" conversion band.** A reverse trial is worth testing: full features for 15 days, then a capped free tier instead of a lockout. It keeps sellers whose orders are seasonal.

- Official current prices for myBillBook and Khatabook did not render. The figures above come from aggregators.
- No official Dukaan pricing page could be fetched. The help page returned 403.

## 2. Usage metering pitfalls: how Indian SMBs react to per-order or per-message fees, and how to avoid bill shock (caps, wallets, alerts)

### Takeaway
Per-order % fees caused public backlash when Dukaan added one without warning in Oct 2023. WhatsApp tools avoid bill shock by making all usage prepaid wallets that are separate from the subscription. Hard per-order caps (Dukaan's ₹200) and free allowances (Meta's new 1,000 free service messages) are the main ways the market limits surprise bills.

### Cited Findings
- **Dukaan backlash (older data, 2023):**
  - Dukaan began charging a per-order service fee on 10 Oct 2023, which users reported as 4-5% on top of gateway charges. It also charged ₹4 per cancellation.
  - Sellers posted on X that it "effectively makes Dukaan expensive". Long-time users said they had chosen Dukaan for affordability and were now looking at alternatives.
  - The founder answered critics sarcastically and did not address the fee rationale.
  - Source: [BusinessToday, 17 Oct 2023](https://www.businesstoday.in/entrepreneurship/start-up/story/e-commerce-platform-dukaan-introduces-service-fee-receives-backlash-on-x-founder-responds-402341-2023-10-17)
- **Dukaan caps its % fee at ₹200 per order on every plan.** — [Dukaan help centre (search snippet)](https://help.mydukaan.io/article/1014-dukaan-service-fee-payment-gateway-charges)
- **AiSensy runs prepaid billing.** "All billing is pre-paid. WhatsApp Conversation Credits are recharged & managed separately." The minimum wallet balance is ₹10. — [AiSensy pricing](https://aisensy.com/pricing)
- **Meta's India WhatsApp rates (2026):**
  - Marketing: ₹0.8631, up about 10% from ₹0.7846 in 2026. About ₹1.02 with GST.
  - Utility and authentication: ₹0.115.
  - From 1 Oct 2026, service replies cost ₹0.115 after the first 1,000 free per phone number per month.
  - Marketing messages carry no free window and no volume discount.
  - WABAs must move to INR billing by 31 Dec 2026.
  - Source: [MyOperator (aggregator)](https://myoperator.com/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india-2026); [Monty Mobile](https://montymobile.com/blogs/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-in-india-inr-rates-gst-and-the-2026-currency-migration-deadline/)
  - The 1 Oct 2026 service-message change is confirmed on AiSensy's own page. — [AiSensy pricing](https://aisensy.com/pricing)
- **Hidden per-message costs and price rises drive churn talk.** Customers report AiSensy charging 15-20% above Meta's India rate card. Third-party "alternatives" posts appeared after the May 2026 AiSensy price rise. — [Chatmitra (competitor, biased)](https://chatmitra.com/chatmitra-vs-aisensy/); [Richautomate (competitor, biased)](https://richautomate.in/blog/aisensy-price-hike-may-2026-alternatives-india); [Loop2 "hidden fees" (aggregator)](https://loop2.ai/blog/aisensy-pricing-india-2026-breakdown)
- **GoKwik's Shopify app sets subscription tiers by order count:** free under 50 orders/month, up to $29.99/mo above 500, plus 2.5% on prepaid payments. — [Shopify App Store listing (via search)](https://apps.shopify.com/kwikcheckout)

### Inferences
- **Order-count tiers like CartHedge's are a safer usage meter than % of GMV.** The unit is one the seller already understands and counts, and it does not grow with basket size. The risk sits entirely in overage.
- **Concrete guardrails for overage**, drawn from the patterns above:
  1. A hard monthly overage cap, or "pause AI replies, keep the order desk working" when the cap is hit. Never block orders.
  2. Alerts at 80% and 100% of the allowance, sent by WhatsApp.
  3. An auto-upgrade suggestion when overage would cost more than the next tier.
  4. Optional prepaid top-up packs instead of post-paid overage.
  5. 30 days' notice and grandfathering for any price change. The Dukaan episode shows how quickly an unannounced change goes public on X.
- **Meta's 1 Oct 2026 change is a cost risk.** Service replies beyond 1,000 per month per number are now billed. If CartHedge ever pays WhatsApp fees for sellers (for example in phase 2 through a partner), each AI DM conversation has a marginal Meta cost. That cost should be passed through or covered by the order allowance.

### Gaps
- No survey data was found on Indian SMB attitudes to per-order vs flat fees. The evidence is anecdotal (Dukaan backlash, competitor blogs).
- No published examples were found of Indian SaaS spend-cap or alert features beyond prepaid wallets.

## 3. Additional revenue streams (payment referrals, courier margins, WhatsApp credit resale, AI credits, COD/RTO products, setup services, themes, seats, agency programs, lending, ads)

### Takeaway
The most realistic near-term extra revenue for CartHedge:
- markup on WhatsApp message credits (Indian BSPs charge roughly 10-30% over Meta);
- AI message credit packs (AiSensy: about ₹1.35 per AI message);
- paid done-for-you setup (Instamojo "Do-It-For-Me");
- payment-gateway referral commissions (Cashfree publishes rates; Razorpay does not).

Courier, COD-verification and lending partnerships exist, but published economics are thin or sales-quoted only.

### Cited Findings

**Payment partner commissions**
- **Razorpay:** the official partner page lists Service Partners (agencies, implementers) and Technology Partners (SaaS, ERPs). It promises a "Fixed partner commission for transaction made by your clients", but publishes no rate. — [Razorpay partners](https://razorpay.com/partners/)
- **Razorpay figures from third parties only:**
  - About 0.1% commission on referred transactions plus a fixed bonus of up to ₹500 per successful referral.
  - Recurring and uncapped.
  - ISVs can build their own margin into sub-merchant rates.
  - Source: [Way2Earning (aggregator, low confidence)](https://www.way2earning.com/2022/01/razorpay-partner-program/); [Xamplify (aggregator)](https://xamplify.com/partner-programs/razorpay/)
- **Cashfree:**
  - Affiliate partners earn a commission on every transaction by referred merchants, reported as 0.25% on UPI and up to 0.35%, paid monthly, Net-30.
  - Platform Partners get "customized incentive plans".
  - Source: [Cashfree affiliate programme blog (search snippet)](https://www.cashfree.com/blog/affiliate-partner-program-industry-best-commission/); [Cashfree partner docs](https://www.cashfree.com/docs/partners/introduction)
  - The UPI figure needs checking against current UPI zero-MDR rules before relying on it.

**Courier aggregator partnerships**
- **Shiprocket's partner programme has three tracks:** Affiliate (commission on referrals), Channel (API access, and "there is no commission associated with channel partnerships"), and Incubator. It pays "acquisition & renewal payouts" on a 30-day cycle after KYC and invoicing. No percentages are published. — [Shiprocket partners](https://www.shiprocket.in/partners/)
- **Aggregator margin:** courier rates reportedly include a 5-12% aggregator margin. — [Checkthat.ai (aggregator, low confidence)](https://checkthat.ai/brands/shiprocket/pricing)

  - Source: [Razorpay Magic](https://razorpay.com/magic/); [Magic Checkout WooCommerce config](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/magic-checkout/woocommerce/configuration/)

**Done-for-you setup services and themes**
- **Instamojo:**
  - Sells a "Do-It-For-Me" store setup (price on request) with a dedicated advisor, 30 days of phone/WhatsApp support and a free domain.
  - Bundles 19+ premium themes into the ₹6,999 Starter.
  - Gives a free domain "worth ₹1,000" on Growth.
  - Source: [Instamojo pricing](https://www.instamojo.com/pricing/)
- **AiSensy** sells an Indian virtual number add-on at ₹2,000 + GST/yr. — [AiSensy pricing](https://aisensy.com/pricing)

**Extra seats**
- Indian WhatsApp tools mostly do not charge per seat. AiSensy advertises "Unlimited Users" (fair use: up to 200 agents). Interakt lists no per-seat charge for standard roles, though Sales CRM roles are excluded. — [AiSensy pricing](https://aisensy.com/pricing); [CampaignHQ](https://blog.campaignhq.co/interakt-pricing-india-2026)

Revenue-share percentages are not published, except Cashfree's affiliate rate. — [Razorpay](https://razorpay.com/partners/), [Cashfree](https://www.cashfree.com/docs/partners/introduction), [Shiprocket](https://www.shiprocket.in/partners/), [AiSensy](https://aisensy.com/pricing)

**Lending and working-capital referrals**
- Embedded lending (working capital or merchant cash advance inside the SaaS) is an established model in which the SaaS takes a share of revenue on referred loans. Examples are global: Lendflow, Adyen Capital. — [Lendflow](https://www.lendflow.com/post/embedded-lending-saas-monetization); [Adyen](https://www.adyen.com/knowledge-hub/embedded-lending)
- In India, loan referral usually runs through DSA (direct selling agent) payouts. — [Finseich (aggregator)](https://finseich.com/blog/how-to-earn-money-loan-referral)

### Inferences
- **CartHedge's architecture fits payment referrals well.** Sellers already use their own Razorpay, so joining Razorpay's Technology Partner programme (or Cashfree's) and onboarding sellers through a partner link earns recurring commission at zero cost to the seller.
- **Rough size of that commission.** At about 0.1% of prepaid GMV, a seller doing ₹3 lakh/month earns CartHedge about ₹300/month, comparable to a plan fee. The actual rate must be negotiated; the 0.1% figure is unverified.
- **WhatsApp credit resale is the industry's main margin lever,** at roughly 10-30% over Meta. It only becomes available once CartHedge runs WhatsApp sending through a BSP partner (the phase 2 credit line). Charge transparently: show the Meta rate plus a stated platform fee. Competitor blogs already attack AiSensy's undisclosed markup.
- **Verified-COD / RTO is a natural paid add-on** for Instagram sellers, who skew heavily to COD:
  - Big players sell it only through sales-quoted contracts, which leaves room for a simple self-serve version.
  - A self-serve version could cost something like ₹1-3 per verified COD order, or come bundled in the top tier.
  - Pricing is not benchmarked; GoKwik's shapes (per order / % / success fee) are the reference.
- **Done-for-you setup is a proven upsell in India** (Instamojo DIFM). Catalog upload, storefront setup and AI tone training could be a one-off ₹999-₹2,999 service. This range is a hypothesis and needs testing.
- **Extra seats are a weak lever.** Indian peers give unlimited users, so charging per seat would read as nickel-and-diming. Gate by capability (roles and permissions) on higher tiers instead.

### Gaps
- No published Razorpay commission rate or ISV markup terms on the official page. Third-party figures (0.1%, ₹500) are unverified.
- No NimbusPost or Shiprocket per-shipment partner margin was found in primary sources.
- No published GoKwik or Shiprocket Checkout COD verification prices were found. All are sales-quoted.
- No India-specific lending referral economics (bps of disbursal) were found for SaaS platforms, for example Razorpay Capital, FlexiLoans or Lendingkart partner terms.
- No data on theme marketplaces or add-on marketplace take rates for Indian SMB tools, and nothing on ads-credit resale.

## 4. Packaging lessons: which features drive upgrades, good-better-best design, decoys, launch/founder pricing and grandfathering

### Takeaway
Indian SMB tools drive upgrades mainly by lowering the usage price on higher tiers (lower % fee, cheaper messages) and by holding back automation or AI as paid add-ons. They use "most popular" badges and long-commitment gating as nudges. Launch pricing shows up as very cheap intro months (Shopify ₹20/mo). No primary evidence was found on grandfathering.

### Cited Findings
- **Fee reduction as the upgrade lever:**
  - Dukaan: the service fee falls from 4.99% to 3.99%, 1.99% and 0.99% across tiers.
  - Instamojo: the transaction fee falls from 5% + ₹3 to 2% + ₹3 on Growth.
  - Interakt: per-message rates fall with each plan.
  - Sources: [Techjockey/Dukaan](https://www.techjockey.com/detail/dukaan); [Instamojo](https://www.instamojo.com/pricing/); [CampaignHQ](https://blog.campaignhq.co/interakt-pricing-india-2026)
- **Social-proof anchor:** Instamojo badges Growth as chosen by "70% of businesses". Starter costs ₹6,999/yr and keeps the 5% fee, which effectively works as a decoy that makes Growth (₹14,999, 2%) look rational. — [Instamojo pricing](https://www.instamojo.com/pricing/)
- **AI and automation sold separately:** AiSensy sells its chatbot builder (₹2,500/mo) and AI Agent (₹1,350/mo + credits) as add-ons rather than including them in base plans. — [AiSensy pricing](https://aisensy.com/pricing)
- **Channel and commitment gating:** Interakt's monthly Starter is Instagram-only; WhatsApp requires quarterly or annual. — [CampaignHQ](https://blog.campaignhq.co/interakt-pricing-india-2026)
- **Launch/intro pricing:** Shopify India offers ₹20/mo for the first 3 months before the ₹1,499 list price. — [Noir & Blanco (aggregator)](https://noirandblanco.com/shopify-pricing-in-india/)
- **Price-rise risk:** AiSensy's May 2026 Pro increase (₹2,699 to ₹3,200) triggered competitor "alternatives" content, a sign that increases without grandfathering invite poaching. — [Richautomate (competitor)](https://richautomate.in/blog/aisensy-price-hike-may-2026-alternatives-india)
- **Hard data on AI conversion:** ChartMogul finds AI and B2B products convert slightly better (6-8% good, 15-20% great) than traditional SaaS. — [ChartMogul](https://chartmogul.com/reports/saas-conversion-report/)

### Inferences
- **CartHedge's three order-count tiers are already a good-better-best ladder with falling unit price** (₹4.99, then ₹2, then ₹1 per order), which matches the "cheaper usage at higher tiers" pattern.
- **Make the middle tier the badged default ("most popular") and give it the key capabilities.** Those capabilities should be AI DM replies, WhatsApp order alerts and broadcasts, so ₹999 is the rational choice, the way Instamojo uses Growth.
- **Keep AI replies inside plans, metered by orders, rather than as a separate add-on.** CartHedge's pitch is the AI sales assistant, and separately metered AI (AiSensy-style) adds a second meter that compounds bill-shock risk.
  - Optional AI top-up packs can cover heavy chat-to-order ratios.
- **Founder pricing.** Lock in launch prices for the first cohort, for example "founder price for life while subscribed", and give 30+ days' notice on any change. This is cheap insurance given the Dukaan and AiSensy reactions. No direct Indian evidence quantifies the effect.

### Gaps
- No Indian data was found on which specific features (staff seats, broadcast, analytics, AI) drive upgrades. The inferences come from how competitors gate features.
- No primary evidence was found on grandfathering or founder-pricing outcomes in Indian SMB SaaS.
- No OpenView, Paddle/ProfitWell or SaaSBoomi India-specific packaging data was retrieved within the search budget.
