# Storefront, checkout and shipping tools for Indian Instagram/WhatsApp-first sellers: pricing and packaging (as of Oct 2026)

Research date: 4 Oct 2026. Every price is in INR unless stated otherwise. Labels used below:
- **[official]**: taken from the vendor's own pricing page or help centre.
- **[secondary]**: from a review site, an aggregator, a competitor's blog or a press article.
- **[unverified]**: the source is weak, gives no date, or conflicts with another source.

GST at 18% is extra on almost every fee listed here unless a line says otherwise.

## Q1. Storefront builders: current INR plans, limits, transaction fees, and who shut down or pivoted

### Takeaway
Fixed subscriptions for Indian storefronts are low, about ₹0 to ₹2,000 a month. The real cost is a percentage of GMV that grows with sales:
- Shopify adds a 2% third-party fee on Basic, because Shopify Payments does not operate in India.
- Dukaan charges a service fee of 1.99% to 4.99%.
- Instamojo charges 2% to 5% plus ₹3 per transaction.
- SuperProfile charges a platform fee of 5% to 10%.

Two of the original "WhatsApp store" players are no longer competitors. Bikayi collapsed in 2022. DotPe has moved to offline and F&B businesses.

### Cited Findings

- Basic is ₹1,994/mo on monthly billing or ₹1,499/mo on annual billing.
- Grow is ₹7,447/mo monthly or ₹5,599/mo annual.
- Advanced is ₹30,164/mo monthly or ₹22,680/mo annual.
- Plus starts at ₹1,75,000/mo on annual billing.

- The surcharge is 2% on Basic, 1% on Grow and 0.6% on Advanced.
- Razorpay's own 2% plus 18% GST (2.36% effective) is charged on top of that.
- Source: [Razorpay blog, 9 Jul 2026](https://razorpay.com/blog/razorpay-shopify-payment-gateway-pricing-explained/). The same percentages appear as the "Shopify Payments" card rates on [Shopify India pricing](https://www.shopify.com/in/pricing).

### Inferences
- In this market the subscription is cheap and the GMV percentage is where the money is made. A seller with ₹4 lakh of prepaid GMV pays ₹8,000 a month in Shopify Basic's third-party fee alone, which is four times the plan price.
- CarthHedge's flat ₹499 / ₹999 / ₹1,999 with no GMV percentage is therefore a strong selling point, if it holds.
- Shopify Basic vs Grow:
  - Grow costs ₹5,453/mo more than Basic on monthly billing.
  - Grow's fee is 1 percentage point lower on prepaid GMV.
  - So Grow becomes the cheaper plan above roughly ₹5.45 lakh/mo of prepaid GMV on monthly billing, or ₹4.1 lakh on annual billing.
  - This is my calculation from the cited plan prices and fee rates.
- Instamojo Growth (₹14,999/yr) pays for itself compared with Lite once prepaid GMV is above about ₹41,700/mo, because it saves 3 percentage points: ₹1,250 ÷ 3%. This is my calculation.
- The tools built specifically for "WhatsApp stores" have either collapsed (Bikayi), moved to offline businesses (DotPe), or moved to creators (SuperProfile). That leaves room for a tool aimed squarely at social sellers of physical goods.
- The common reference prices a small seller will recognise are ₹99 to ₹999 a month (Zoho, Wix, Dukaan) and ₹1,499 to ₹1,994 a month (Shopify Basic). CarthHedge's ₹499 entry tier sits inside that range.

### Gaps
- Dukaan's current official plan table, and whether its service fee applies to COD orders, could not be extracted because the page renders with JavaScript.
- Whether Shopify's third-party fee applies to COD (manual payment) orders was not confirmed from a primary source. The usual understanding is that manual payment methods are exempt.
- No 2025 to 2026 press source was found on Dukaan's direction (international expansion or AI), so that is left out.
- No official INR prices were found for QuickSell, or for Zoho Commerce's Standard tier and above.
- The terms of Instagram/Meta's own shop or checkout in India were not researched.

## Q2. Checkout and RTO tools: pricing models, minimums and who they target

### Takeaway
None of the major Indian one-click checkout vendors publishes a rate card:
- GoKwik is "custom quote".
- Razorpay Magic Checkout is "enterprise pricing, contact sales".
- Shopflo has reported entry prices of ₹3,000 to ₹5,000 a month.

All three target D2C brands on Shopify with thousands of orders a month, not DM-led sellers. The only priced RTO tool found is Shiprocket's RTO Score at ₹2.49 per COD order. Simpl, a BNPL checkout option, was ordered by RBI to stop payment operations in September 2025.

### Cited Findings

**GoKwik**
- No public rate card as of August 2026. The pricing page ends with a "get custom quote" button.
- Indian checkout suites use one of three pricing shapes: a flat fee per order, a percentage of checkout GMV, or a success fee on COD-to-prepaid conversions and RTO avoided.
- The article warns that a 0.5% to 1% take-rate "compounds brutally at scale".
- Source: [theshizz.in, 30 Aug 2026](https://theshizz.in/blog/gokwik-vs-razorpay-magic-checkout-comparison-d2c) [secondary]
- GoKwik's best fit is COD share above about 40%, AOV under about ₹1,500, 3,000+ orders a month, and FMCG, beauty or fashion brands. Same source.
- One aggregator lists an "Emerging" plan from $31.28/mo. Source: [SaaSworthy / SoftwareSuggest](https://www.saasworthy.com/product/gokwik) [unverified].

**Razorpay Magic Checkout**
- Listed as "Enterprise pricing", contact sales. No per-order or RTO-protection price is published. Source: [Razorpay pricing](https://razorpay.com/pricing/) [official]
- Its best fit is prepaid-heavy, premium-AOV brands that already use Razorpay. Source: [theshizz.in](https://theshizz.in/blog/gokwik-vs-razorpay-magic-checkout-comparison-d2c) [secondary]

**Razorpay standard gateway [official]**
- 2% per successful domestic transaction across UPI, cards, netbanking, wallets, pay-later and EMI. UPI and RuPay debit carry zero MDR, but the 2% platform fee still applies.
- Corporate cards are 2.15%. International cards are up to 3%.
- ₹0 setup fee and ₹0 annual maintenance charge. 18% GST on the fee.
- Source: [Razorpay pricing](https://razorpay.com/pricing/)

**Shiprocket Checkout**
- Pay-as-you-go with no flat monthly fee. COD charges are reported at about 1% to 2%, and the fee per order varies with volume.
- Source: search results incl. [wiserreview.com](https://wiserreview.com/blog/shiprocket-checkout-alternatives/) [secondary, unverified]
- The official checkout pricing URL returned 404.

**Shopflo**
- Starter is reported from ₹3,000/mo, priced on monthly GMV, order volume and needs. Another report says plans start around ₹5,000 a month.
- Sources: [wiserreview.com](https://wiserreview.com/blog/shopflo-alternatives/), [Shiprocket Checkout blog](https://checkout.shiprocket.in/blog/shopflo-alternatives/) [secondary; the second one is competitor-written]

**Shiprocket RTO Score [official]**
- ₹2.49 per order on all COD orders. No free tier is mentioned.
- Source: [Shiprocket support, modified 3 Apr 2024](https://support.shiprocket.in/support/solutions/articles/152000000313-how-much-will-rto-score-cost-me-). The article is older, but no newer price was found.

**Simpl (BNPL)**
- RBI ordered it on 25 Sep 2025 to stop payment, clearing and settlement activity because it had no PSS Act authorisation.
- Separately, the ED had opened FEMA proceedings in Jul 2025.
- Staff were cut from about 220 to 50 to 60, mainly to handle collections.
- Sources: [The Paypers](https://thepaypers.com/regulations/news/simpl-to-cease-payment-operations-as-pe-rbi-order), [Business Today, 26 Sep 2025](https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/corporate/story/rbi-orders-bnpl-startup-simpl-to-halt-payment-operations-over-regulatory-breach-495875-2025-09-26), [MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2025/09/223-rbi-fintech-simpl-payment-operations-pss-act/)

### Inferences
- A seller at 100 to 500 orders a month will not get a usable quote from GoKwik or Magic Checkout. Their stated ideal customer starts around 3,000 orders a month.
- That leaves CarthHedge's ₹499 and ₹999 tiers without a direct checkout or RTO competitor. At ₹1,999 (around 2,000 orders) CarthHedge starts to meet GoKwik and Shopflo's lower end.
- To illustrate, a 0.5% to 1% checkout take-rate on ₹16 lakh GMV (2,000 orders at ₹800 AOV) would be ₹8,000 to ₹16,000 a month. This is my calculation using the theshizz range, not a quoted price.
- RTO Score at ₹2.49 per COD order costs about ₹125 at 100 orders and ₹2,490 at 2,000 orders, assuming 50% COD. This gives a concrete outside price anchor for any COD-risk feature CarthHedge bundles.
- The Simpl shutdown takes one "pay later" option away from small-seller checkouts. Snapmint was not verified.

### Gaps
- No official percentage or per-order rate was found for GoKwik, Magic Checkout, Shiprocket Checkout or Shopflo. All are sales-quoted. Real paid amounts from seller forums were not found within the search budget.
- Snapmint's merchant pricing and status were not found.
- Minimum monthly commitments for checkout tools are not published.

## Q3. Shipping aggregators: plan fees, COD remittance fees, RTO charges and RTO protection

### Takeaway
Aggregators make their money on freight margin, not on subscriptions:
- Shiprocket's plans run from free to ₹799/mo and are refunded when shipment volume thresholds are met.
- NimbusPost and iThink show no subscription fee.

The real costs per order are:
- Freight: about ₹19 to ₹45 per 500 g through aggregators, ₹55 to ₹70 going direct to Delhivery at small volume.
- COD fee: about ₹37 to ₹40 or 1.5% to 2%.
- Early COD remittance: about 0.99% for D+2, up to 5% for instant.
- RTO: return freight is charged even though the COD fee is reversed.

### Cited Findings

**Shiprocket plans [official]**

| Plan | Monthly fee | Average cost per shipment | Volume band |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lite | Free | ₹45 | Up to 5 orders/mo |
| Business | ₹199 | ₹41 | 5 to 50 orders/mo |
| Advanced | ₹499 | ₹39 | 50 to 200 orders/mo |
| Pro | ₹799 | ₹36 | Page says 3,000 to 10,000/mo, which looks like an extraction error; treat as unverified |

- The plan fee is refunded when the seller hits 100 shipments (Business), 500 (Advanced) or 1,000 (Pro) in the month.
- Detailed freight, COD and RTO rates are only visible in the seller dashboard.
- Source: [Shiprocket pricing](https://www.shiprocket.in/pricing/)

**Shiprocket fees [secondary]**
- COD fee is ₹27 + 1.5% with a minimum of ₹37, whichever is higher.
- Early COD starts at 0.99% for D+2. Instant COD can cost up to 5%.
- The COD charge is reversed on RTO orders, but return freight is still billed.
- Source: search results incl. [checkthat.ai](https://checkthat.ai/brands/shiprocket/pricing), [flexicommerce COD calculator](https://flexicommerce.in/tools/cod-calculator)

**NimbusPost [official]**
- B2C Essential is from ₹25.50 per 500 g, for up to 300 orders a month.
- Elevate is from ₹24 per 500 g, for 300 to 1,000 orders.
- Elite is from ₹19 per 500 g, for 1,000+ orders.
- No subscription fee is shown. "Early COD payouts" is an add-on with no published price.
- Source: [NimbusPost pricing](https://nimbuspost.com/pricing)

**iThink Logistics**
- No subscription or integration fee. Rates depend on volume tier and are shown in the dashboard. Source: [iThink knowledge base](https://knowledgebase.ithinklogistics.com/knowledgebase/articles/where-can-you-check-the-rate-chart-for-the-logistics) [official]
- COD is reported at 2%. Source: [iThink vs Shiprocket comparison](https://www.ithinklogistics.com/blog/ithink-logistics-vs-shiprocket-the-complete-comparison-guide/) [vendor blog, unverified]

**Delhivery direct [secondary]**
- About ₹55 to ₹70 per shipment (up to 500 g) for small businesses shipping 100 to 1,000 a month.
- COD is a flat ₹40 or 2% of COD value, with next-day remittance. An alternative figure given is ₹35 or 1.5%.
- A new network surcharge took effect on 1 Sep 2026: ₹4 per express shipment and ₹2 per surface shipment.
- Fuel, COD and GST together can push the real cost 30% to 50% above the base rate.
- Source: [ClickPost: Delhivery courier charges 2026](https://www.clickpost.ai/blog/delhivery-courier-charges)

**Meesho RTO cost, for reference [secondary]**
- The seller absorbs ₹75 to ₹140 per failed order. Source: [blooprint.in](https://blooprint.in/blog/meesho-seller-fees-explained/)

### Inferences
- An aggregator is cheaper per shipment than going direct to Delhivery at 100 to 1,000 orders a month: about ₹36 to ₹45 against ₹55 to ₹70. So the small seller's shipping stack will almost always be an aggregator.
- That makes a Shiprocket, NimbusPost or iThink integration more valuable to CarthHedge than a direct courier integration.
- The COD fee alone (about ₹37 to ₹40 per COD order) costs more per order than CarthHedge does at any tier: ₹5 per order at ₹499/100, ₹2 at ₹999/500, ₹1 at ₹1,999/2,000.
- So "fewer COD orders and fewer RTOs" is the value claim most likely to justify CarthHedge's price. One avoided RTO saves the return freight plus the original forward freight, around ₹75 to ₹140.

### Gaps
- Shiprocket's official per-500 g rate card, RTO rates and early-COD price list sit behind login, so the COD and early-COD figures above are secondary.
- No price was found for Shiprocket's other RTO-protection or verified-COD products (for example Engage) beyond RTO Score.
- NimbusPost's COD fee and RTO rates are not published.
- A current Delhivery One self-serve plan page was not fetched.

## Q4. Typical total monthly tool spend at 100, 500 and 2,000 orders a month

### Takeaway
No published survey of actual seller spend was found, so the figures below are a model built from the cited unit prices.

Assumptions:
- AOV ₹800, 50% prepaid and 50% COD.
- Freight and COD fees from Shiprocket. GST and RTO return freight are excluded.

Software plus the platform's GMV fees come to about ₹2,000 to ₹4,000 a month at 100 orders and ₹6,000 to ₹11,000 at 500. At 2,000 orders they reach ₹19,000 to ₹37,000 with Shopify-plus-Razorpay, and more once a checkout tool is added. Logistics and COD fees are 3 to 5 times larger than the software at every volume.

### Cited Findings (unit prices used in the model)
- **Shopify:** Basic ₹1,994/mo (monthly) or ₹1,499/mo (annual), Grow ₹7,447/mo. Source: [Shopify India pricing](https://www.shopify.com/in/pricing)
- **Shopify third-party fee:** 2% on Basic, 1% on Grow. Source: [Razorpay blog](https://razorpay.com/blog/razorpay-shopify-payment-gateway-pricing-explained/)
- **Razorpay:** 2% + 18% GST = 2.36%. Source: [Razorpay blog](https://razorpay.com/blog/razorpay-shopify-payment-gateway-pricing-explained/), [Razorpay pricing](https://razorpay.com/pricing/)
- **Instamojo:** Growth ₹14,999/yr (≈₹1,250/mo) with 2% + ₹3; Lite ₹0 with 5% + ₹3, payments included. Source: [Instamojo pricing](https://www.instamojo.com/pricing/)
- **Dukaan:** Growth ₹999/mo with a 1.99% service fee, plus a 1% to 3% gateway fee. Source: [Techjockey](https://www.techjockey.com/detail/dukaan), [Dukaan help snippet](https://help.mydukaan.io/article/1014-dukaan-service-fee-payment-gateway-charges) [unverified]
- **Shiprocket plans:** Advanced ₹499 at ₹39 per shipment, Pro ₹799 at ₹36. Source: [Shiprocket pricing](https://www.shiprocket.in/pricing/)
- **Shiprocket COD fee:** ₹27 + 1.5%, minimum ₹37, which comes to ₹39 at ₹800 AOV. Source: [checkthat.ai](https://checkthat.ai/brands/shiprocket/pricing) [secondary]
- **Shiprocket RTO Score:** ₹2.49 per COD order. Source: [Shiprocket support](https://support.shiprocket.in/support/solutions/articles/152000000313-how-much-will-rto-score-cost-me-)

### Inferences (my calculations; assumptions as stated in the Takeaway)

| Monthly line item | 100 orders (₹80k GMV) | 500 orders (₹4L GMV) | 2,000 orders (₹16L GMV) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shopify Basic (monthly billing) + 2% on prepaid | ₹1,994 + ₹800 = ₹2,794 | ₹1,994 + ₹4,000 = ₹5,994 | ₹1,994 + ₹16,000 = ₹17,994 (Grow: ₹7,447 + ₹8,000 = ₹15,447) |
| Razorpay 2.36% on prepaid | ₹944 | ₹4,720 | ₹18,880 |
| **Shopify + Razorpay subtotal** | **≈₹3,740** | **≈₹10,700** | **≈₹34,300 to ₹36,900** |
| Alternative: Dukaan Growth ₹999 + 1.99% of GMV + about 2% PG on prepaid [unverified basis] | ≈₹3,400 | ≈₹12,960 | ≈₹48,800 |
| Alternative: Instamojo Growth ₹1,250 + 2% + ₹3 on prepaid (payments included) | ≈₹2,200 | ≈₹6,000 | ≈₹20,250 |
| Checkout/RTO tool | Usually none | Usually none | RTO Score ≈ ₹2,490; a GoKwik-type take-rate of 0.5% to 1% ≈ ₹8,000 to ₹16,000 |
| Shiprocket plan fee | ₹499 (Advanced; not refunded below 500 shipments) | ₹499 to ₹799 (Advanced refunded at 500) | ₹799 (Pro; refunded at 1,000+) |
| Freight (forward only) | ≈₹3,900 | ≈₹18,000 to ₹19,500 | ≈₹72,000 |
| COD fees | ≈₹1,950 | ≈₹9,750 | ≈₹39,000 |

What the table shows:
- Using the Shopify-plus-Razorpay stack, software and payment fees (excluding freight and COD) work out to about ₹37 per order at 100 orders, ₹21 at 500 and ₹17 to ₹18 at 2,000.
- CarthHedge works out to ₹5, ₹2 and ₹1 per order at its three tiers. That is a small addition next to these costs, or a small fraction of them if it replaces the store layer.
- The most expensive item for a seller who cares about cost is the GMV percentage, not the subscription.
- A flat-fee storefront with 0% GMV take, using the seller's own Razorpay or UPI, saves a Shopify Basic seller about ₹800, ₹4,000 and ₹16,000 a month at the three volumes.

### Gaps
- No primary data (surveys, forum threads with invoices) was found on what Indian social sellers actually spend on tools each month.
- The 50/50 COD split and the ₹800 AOV are assumptions, not sourced figures.
- The table excludes RTO return freight, so the true logistics cost is higher.
- WhatsApp Business API messaging costs are outside this scope.

## Q5. Free vs paid, trial lengths and annual discounts

### Takeaway
Free tiers are common, but they charge for it through higher GMV fees: Instamojo Lite at 5% + ₹3, Dukaan Free at 4.99%, SuperProfile Starter at 10%. Trials are short, from 3 days (Shopify) to 14 days (Zoho, and Instamojo's money-back guarantee). Annual billing saves about 25% on Shopify and up to 60% on Dukaan, according to Dukaan.

### Cited Findings

**Shopify [official]**
- Trial: 3 days free, then ₹20/mo for 3 months.
- Annual billing discount: Basic ₹1,499 vs ₹1,994 (−24.8%), Grow ₹5,599 vs ₹7,447 (−24.8%), Advanced ₹22,680 vs ₹30,164 (−24.8%).
- Source: [Shopify India pricing](https://www.shopify.com/in/pricing)

**Instamojo [official]**
- Free Lite store with unlimited products at 5% + ₹3.
- Growth costs ₹14,999/yr against ₹2,999/mo billed monthly, a 58% saving on annual.
- 14-day money-back guarantee.
- Source: [Instamojo pricing](https://www.instamojo.com/pricing/)

**Dukaan**
- Monthly/Yearly toggle labelled "up to 60% off" on yearly. Source: [mydukaan.io/pricing](https://mydukaan.io/pricing) [official]
- Free plan with a 4.99% service fee. Source: [Dukaan help snippet](https://help.mydukaan.io/article/1014-dukaan-service-fee-payment-gateway-charges) [unverified]

**Zoho Commerce [official]**
- 14-day free trial with no credit card.
- Starter ₹99/mo is offered on annual billing only.
- 0% transaction fee on every plan.
- Source: [Zoho Commerce pricing](https://www.zoho.com/commerce/pricing.html)

**SuperProfile**
- Free Starter with a 10% platform fee; paid plans billed annually. Source: [help centre](https://help.cosmofeed.com/portal/en/kb/articles/superprofile-plans-passion-pro)

**Shiprocket [official]**
- Lite plan is free.
- Paid plan fees are refunded on hitting volume: 100, 500 or 1,000 shipments.
- No trial stated.
- Source: [Shiprocket pricing](https://www.shiprocket.in/pricing/)

**Razorpay [official]**
- ₹0 setup fee and ₹0 annual maintenance charge.
- Source: [Razorpay pricing](https://razorpay.com/pricing/)

**NimbusPost and iThink**
- No subscription fee shown. Sources: [NimbusPost pricing](https://nimbuspost.com/pricing), [iThink KB](https://knowledgebase.ithinklogistics.com/knowledgebase/articles/where-can-you-check-the-rate-chart-for-the-logistics)

### Inferences
- The sellers CarthHedge targets are used to three things: ₹0 to start, payment taken as a percentage of sales, and refunds when volume targets are hit.
- A pure upfront ₹499 with no free tier pushes against all of these. Two ways to bridge it:
  - A short trial (7 to 14 days matches Zoho and Instamojo).
  - An annual price around 20% to 25% lower (matching Shopify's 24.8%).
- Shiprocket's "fee refunded at volume" model shows that sellers in this market find volume-linked pricing reasonable. That supports CarthHedge's order-volume tiers.
- The strongest single message is a 0% GMV take compared with Shopify's 2%, Dukaan's 1.99% to 4.99% and Instamojo Lite's 5%.

### Gaps
- Trial lengths for Dukaan, QuickSell, GoKwik and Shopflo were not found.
- Annual discounts for Zoho's higher tiers and for Wix India were not confirmed on official pages.
