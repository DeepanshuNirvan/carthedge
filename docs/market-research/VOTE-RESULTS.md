## Tally (15 sellers and advisors, excluding the buyer)

**Will they pay after the trial?**
- Yes: 5 (33%)
- Maybe: 8 (53%)
- No: 2 (13%)

**Fairness:** 3.9 out of 5 on average. Nobody rated it below 3.

**Plan chosen:**
- Growth: 8, the clear centre
- Pro: 2
- Starter: 1
- Brand or Partner: 2
- Free: 1
- None: 1

**What turns a maybe into a yes:** 6 of the 8 "maybe" votes depend on WhatsApp working on the seller's own number. That one feature is worth about 40% more paying sellers on this panel.

**Price:** nobody called ₹1,299 for Growth too expensive. The objections were about features, not price.

## What changes in the plan (v2)
1. Keep the prices. Growth at ₹1,299 is accepted.
2. Starter already includes 300 AI replies, but Shabnam and Farheen missed it. Say "AI auto-reply included" in large text on the Starter card.
3. WhatsApp on the seller's own number: drop the add-on from ₹499 to ₹299 on Starter and Growth, and keep it included on Pro. Farheen, Vikram and Shabnam all pushed on the total cost.
4. Add a 7-day grace period after a failed payment before the store moves to the Free plan (Vikram).
5. Phone support in Hindi and English on every plan, with Bangla, Tamil, Gujarati and Marathi on Growth and above. Gujarati and Bangla first.
6. Drop pack: change it from ₹999 a day to ₹1,999 per drop week (Kabir and Tanya).
7. Promise in writing: if WhatsApp on the seller's own number is not live within 90 days of a seller paying, they get one month free.
8. "Verified seller" badge only after a GSTIN, or a PAN plus address, check (Pooja).
